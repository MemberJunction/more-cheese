# ICF internal document vault

> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT** — International Cheese Federation (ICF), a fictitious trade association created for the MemberJunction More Cheese demonstration environment. All names, figures, events, and decisions are invented.

This is the internal working record of the International Cheese Federation: memos,
spreadsheets, slide-deck outlines, email threads, board packets and policies. It is the corpus
that MemberJunction **Knowledge Hub** ingests from Dropbox.

It is deliberately separate from the public corpus at `content/` (which is published to
morecheese.org). Nothing in here appears on the website.

```
memos/YYYY/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md
spreadsheets/YYYY/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.csv
decks/YYYY/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md
email/YYYY/YYYY-MM-DD-thread-<slug>/NN-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM-<from>-to-<to>.md
board-packets/YYYY/YYYY-MM-<slug>.md
policies/YYYY/YYYY-MM-<slug>.md
```

Every artifact carries YAML frontmatter (or, for CSV, a `#`-comment metadata block) with
`doc_type`, `visibility: internal`, `era`, `fiscal_year`, `fictional: true` and `data_sources`.
Full specification: `content/VAULT-DESIGN.md`. Generated by the `morecheese-internal-comms` skill.

All email addresses use the reserved `.example` TLD. All bylines are functional roles — the world
model defines no named ICF staff.
