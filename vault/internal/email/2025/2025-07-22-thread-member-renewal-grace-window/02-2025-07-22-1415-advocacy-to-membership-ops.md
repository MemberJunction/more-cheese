We are definitely hearing the same concerns from producers on the ground. Feed inflation and summer utility bills are stretching operating budgets to the limit, and several members have mentioned that membership dues arrived right when their cooling equipment maintenance costs spiked.

However, I need to sound a small caveat from our side. While we want to be supportive, Advocacy cannot endorse an open-ended grace period without financial controls. If we let accounts linger indefinitely in a limbo state, our active member count of 1,214 becomes distorted for reporting purposes, and cash flow suffers.

My recommendation is that we offer a structured thirty-day extension specifically for members who submit a brief hardship note, rather than an automatic waiver. That gives them breathing room while keeping our records clean.
