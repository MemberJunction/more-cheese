From a standards perspective, I advise against a 60-day extension. If we relax the deadline to that extent for a corporate account, our entire tiered dues structure loses its teeth. Members complying with timely renewals will rightly question why others receive indefinite grace.

That said, I recognize cash flow in small and mid-sized entities can be choppy in late winter. Instead of an open-ended 60 days, why not cap any exceptional grace period at 30 days maximum, requiring a signed intent to pay before we reinstate their member pricing for upcoming events?

If they cannot meet a 30-day cutoff, they drop into a lapsed status and must re-apply at standard rates. Let's not make special rules that undermine our basic accounting integrity.
