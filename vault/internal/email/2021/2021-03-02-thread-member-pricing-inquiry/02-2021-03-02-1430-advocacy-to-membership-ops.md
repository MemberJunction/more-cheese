We cannot give them a blanket answer because the data shows regional divergence. A Midwest creamery reported almost flat component pricing this week, while spot buyers in the East felt it immediately.

Our advice should be cautionary: do not reprice based on a single monthly invoice. Tell members to track landed milk cost against finished cheese prices across two consecutive billing cycles before altering wholesale lists. If it is a one-month spike, repricing prematurely damages buyer relationships for no reason.

If they need structured guidance on how to talk to wholesale accounts about cost-driven increases without sounding apologetic, point them toward our pricing course materials.
