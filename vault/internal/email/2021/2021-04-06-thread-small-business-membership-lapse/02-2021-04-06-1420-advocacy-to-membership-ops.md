That is a tough spot, but I would advise against waiving the reinstatement fee formally or rewriting the grace-window rules across the board.

Our advocacy stance on small-producer exemptions is focused on federal raw-milk aging rules and labeling standards, not internal association billing policy. However, can we offer them a split payment option or a temporary drop to Individual tier while they bridge the cash flow gap? That keeps them in the federation without breaking the renewal terms.
