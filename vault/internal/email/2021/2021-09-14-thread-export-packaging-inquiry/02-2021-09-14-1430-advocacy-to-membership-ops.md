We do not hold regulatory authority over ocean carriers or pulp suppliers, and I want to be careful not to imply that we can intervene in port logistics. Our coalition meetings on dairy pricing and FSMA exemptions touch broader producer economics, but container routing is purely commercial.

What we can advise members to do is document their holding costs and talk to buyers early about delivery windows, exactly as outlined in our recent communications guidance. If they are holding aged cheese longer in caves due to port delays, that is an affinage management issue as much as a logistics one. Perhaps Education & Credentialing should weigh in on how extended cave hold times affect rind integrity.
