We own this issue, but let us be precise about what we can advise. The federal program requires a certified volume figure, not a net payout figure. Trying to calculate that by backing it out of settlement statements by hand is a recipe for errors.

The correct operational advice is not for members to calculate it themselves, but to call their cooperative's member services line and ask for the production-only volume directly. Most co-ops generate that report in seconds. We did not spell that out explicitly in yesterday's blog post because we thought it was standard practice, but clearly it is not.

Let us route these callers directly to that advice. If a co-op refuses or cannot provide the figure, let us know immediately so we can log it as a concrete gap.
