From our end, a 30-day grace window sounds completely reasonable, and it fits nicely with the tone of our recent blog posts acknowledging that cash flow and feed bills have been tough for producers this year.

However, I have a pushback on how we handle the database records during that window. If we leave them marked active while their credit card has failed, our order reconciliation numbers for gross sales will drift from the cash captured figures. We already logged 259 confirmed orders and 55,369 USD in membership gross across 242 purchases so far, and I do not want auditors or executive review finding uncollected balances hidden inside active member counts.

Can we tag them as 'pending renewal' rather than fully active or lapsed?
