I have reviewed the shipping guidelines against our active compliance tracking. The federal and interstate rules have not shifted in a way that blocks competition samples, but creameries must include proper temperature logging documentation inside the package, particularly for washed rinds and soft-ripened entries.

One caveat: do not state in the entry packet that our federation guidelines substitute for official state transit certificates. We can advise on best practices for cold-chain shipping, but compliance remains entirely with the producer. If that distinction is clear in the text, you are good to publish the dates.
