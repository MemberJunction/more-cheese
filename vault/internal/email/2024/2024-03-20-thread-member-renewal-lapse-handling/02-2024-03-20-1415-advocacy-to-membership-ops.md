From a standards and membership rules perspective, our bylaws do not explicitly mandate an instant cutoff on day one of an expired billing cycle. The automated lock is an operational feature, not a strict constitutional requirement.

However, we need to be careful about setting a precedent that allows indefinite extensions without payment confirmation. If a small producer is navigating food-safety attestation paperwork or trying to access our insurance webinar materials, cutting them off mid-review creates exactly the kind of support headache we try to prevent.

I support a structured 30-day grace window for accounts with pending payments, provided it is applied uniformly. Let's draft a short policy note for executive review.
