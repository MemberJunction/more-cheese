## Policy application risks

- Ad-hoc tier downgrades undermine the fee structure
- Uncontrolled grace periods distort our cash-captured metrics
- Manual tracking consumes staff time needed for advocacy
- Inconsistent enforcement creates member complaints

> notes: We need an executive decision on whether grace windows require a formal hardship statement or automated approval.

---

## Proposed adjustments

- Establish a strict 30-day ceiling for unpaid grace windows
- Require production verification for any tier downgrade request
- Coordinate renewal reminders with margin support content
- Maintain portal access only during active grace periods

> notes: These four steps close the loophole without requiring new administrative staff or bespoke policy exceptions.
