## Renewal and grace-window friction

- Failed payments triggering automated grace periods
- Small farmstead members requesting extended grace windows
- Cash-flow strain driven by summer cooling and energy costs
- Risk of permanent lapse if outreach is purely transactional

> notes: We must distinguish between temporary seasonal cash squeezes and permanent closures. Rigid cutoff dates risk alienating long-term members.

## Operational capacity constraints

- Shared staff handling conference follow-up and renewals
- Manual payment retries creating administrative backlog
- Grace-window tracking spreadsheet requires daily manual updates
- Risk of delayed outreach to accounts in final warning stages

> notes: Staff capacity is stretched thin across multiple operational priorities. We cannot sustain intensive manual follow-up without process adjustments.

## Proposed adjustments

- Establish formal 30-day hardship extension guidelines
- Implement automated payment retry notifications 7 days prior
- Standardize tier-change approval workflow with Advocacy
- Review grace-window metrics weekly with Executive office

> notes: These four steps require no new headcount but will eliminate ambiguity in handling delinquent accounts.
