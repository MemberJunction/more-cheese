## The renewal pressure point

- Small farmstead creameries calling about propane bills
- Grace-window inquiries exceeding normal seasonal averages
- Standard 30-day cutoff is triggering premature lapse notices
- Producers want to stay active but need payment breathing room

> notes: This matches what our blog posts are reporting about regional energy inflation. Members are making hard choices between fuel and dues.

---

## Proposed operational adjustment

- Extend renewal grace window from 30 days to 60 days
- Apply extension exclusively to hardship inquiries
- Maintain portal access during the extended review period
- Require explicit follow-up before automatic cancellation

> notes: This protects our active member count of 1,063 without granting permanent waivers. It buys producers time to settle heating bills.

## Capacity and staffing impact
