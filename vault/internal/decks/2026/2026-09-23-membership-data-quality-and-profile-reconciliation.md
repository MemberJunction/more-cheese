## Impact on communications

- Public blog readership and member alerts affected by bad addresses
- Staff wasting time manually verifying returned mail
- Risk of misidentifying renewing members as first-time buyers
- Distorts participation numbers for upcoming workshops and webinars

> notes: Communications relies on clean lists. When a profile splits, messages fail to deliver and engagement metrics become unreliable.

## Proposed merge workflow

- Cross-reference incoming payments against organization keys
- Archive stale profiles rather than letting them renew independently
- Establish a bi-weekly audit of mismatched employer affiliations
- Coordinate directly with administrative staff on disputed records

> notes: We need a disciplined approach to prevent duplicate creation at the point of sale.
