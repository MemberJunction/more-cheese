## The renewal problem

- Credit card processing failures are spiking on annual renewals
- Automated systems drop members immediately on failure
- Members experience unexpected account suspension
- Support queue is clogged with billing inquiries

> notes: This is not a churn problem; it is a payments and grace-window policy problem. Members want to stay active.

---

## Public blog alignment

- Public posts are discussing farmstead succession and herd cold stress
- Members are busy managing winter milk quality and ownership transitions
- Administrative billing friction distracts from valuable association resources
- Operational clarity supports member retention during transition periods

> notes: We must ensure our administrative processes do not add friction while members are dealing with larger farm and creamery decisions.

---

## Proposed grace window

- Introduce a 14-day automated grace window for failed payments
- Keep member accounts active during the grace period
- Send two automated reminder notices before suspension
- Reduce manual support intervention for standard billing retries

> notes: This aligns our workflow with standard trade association practices and reduces frustration for renewing members.
