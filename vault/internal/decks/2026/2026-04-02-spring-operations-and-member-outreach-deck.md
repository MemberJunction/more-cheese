## The quiet membership pattern

- Corporate or distributor sponsors an employee seat
- Auto-renewals process silently year after year
- Account holder logs zero course activity and ignores emails
- Silence is usually overload rather than disinterest

> notes: We highlighted this in our spotlight piece. A single phone call often reveals a quality assurance manager who never received proper onboarding.

## What the pilot outreach showed

- Direct phone calls convert better than standard renewal notices
- Asking what would be useful takes under ten minutes
- Reached a quality assurance lead stuck on shipping rules
- Member subsequently enrolled in an active course

> notes: We are not cold-calling every account, but targeting multi-year silent seats is working.
