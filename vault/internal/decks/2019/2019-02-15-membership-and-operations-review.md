## The renewal lapse problem

- Several members past their annual renewal date
- Informal grace-window requests creating tracking errors
- Failed credit card payments not clearing automatically
- Staff spending excessive time on custom extensions

> notes: We cannot continue granting ad-hoc extensions without distorting active membership numbers.

---

## Proposed operational changes

- Enforce a strict 30-day maximum grace window
- Suspend portal access automatically after 30 days unpaid
- Require formal payment plans for hardship extensions
- Coordinate messaging with Communications via the portal

> notes: These steps protect our revenue baseline and reduce administrative overhead for membership staff.
