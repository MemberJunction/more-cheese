## Operational bottlenecks

- Manual verification takes more time than automated ingestion
- Staff split between event registration and database cleanup
- Inconsistent formatting on legacy member import sheets
- Lack of mandatory field validation on self-service web forms

> notes: Until we enforce strict schema rules on web forms, cleanup will remain a weekly reactive chore.

## Proposed cleanup protocol

- Merge duplicate person records by end of month
- Audit stale employer fields against active corporate directories
- Suppress bounced email addresses until manual phone check
- Reconcile unverified payment statuses against bank ledger

> notes: Four concrete steps. Each requires sign-off from Membership Operations before execution.
