## Identified data anomalies

- Seven flagged duplicate pairs in member records
- Fourteen accounts carrying stale employer affiliations

> notes: These anomalies do not affect financial totals but disrupt direct member communication and renewal tracking.

## Root causes

- Alternate name spellings created during online registration
- Unmerged records following creamery ownership changes
- Stale email addresses from defunct domain names
- Manual entry errors during peak enrollment windows

> notes: Most duplicates originate from online sign-ups where members created new accounts instead of renewing existing lapsed profiles.

---

## Impact on communications

- Bounced addresses interfere with webinar announcements
- Wholesale Negotiation webinar notices failing on hard bounces
- Confusion regarding event registration confirmations
- Inaccurate tracking for raw-milk aging advocacy actions

> notes: Communications must rely on clean data before sending out pre-conference materials for the Madison annual conference.

---

## Proposed cleanup protocol

- Merge verified duplicate pairs under primary profile keys
- Update stale employer records against current organization files
- Purge hard-bouncing email addresses from active broadcast lists
- Re-align payment statuses with confirmed order records

> notes: A systematic approach is required to prevent data fragmentation from worsening during the summer conference rush.

---

## Timeline and resource allocation

- Complete duplicate merging by mid-July
- Finish employer affiliation updates prior to conference week
- Implement ongoing contact hygiene checks monthly
- Assign one analyst to oversee database reconciliation

> notes: Staffing is constrained by event preparation, so the cleanup must be executed in manageable batches.
