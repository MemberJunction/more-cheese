## Prerequisite verification issues

- CCP requires documented working hours alongside Foundations certification
- Candidates attempt to reconstruct hours from memory rather than logs
- Manual verification strains our small administrative team
- Informal creamery time records complicate the audit trail

> notes: We spend too much time chasing paper trails for hours that were never logged properly by employers.

## Renewal tracking gap

- CCP credentials expire after three years, unlike five-year Foundations
- Expiration dates are currently maintained in a separate ledger
- Members confuse membership renewal with credential renewal
- We need an automated flag linked to Membership Operations

> notes: Three-year renewal is vital because safety practices move fast, but our tracking is too manual.
