## The renewal problem

- Recurring credit card charges failing on SmallBusiness accounts
- Members requesting informal grace windows
- Distributor payment delays driving the backlog
- Risk of artificial churn if we cut accounts off immediately

> notes: We should not lose members over a five-day credit card processing glitch during the holiday rush.

---

## Proposed operational adjustment

- Grant a standard 30-day grace window for SmallBusiness renewals
- Freeze tier changes until past-due payments clear
- Keep member benefits and committee access active during review
- Coordinate messaging with Communications and Advocacy

> notes: This protects our retention baseline without creating manual reconciliation errors in the order tables.
