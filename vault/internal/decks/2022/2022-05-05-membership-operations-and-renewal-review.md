## Proposed grace-window protocol

- Establish a standardized 45-day grace window upon request
- Keep member benefits active during the extended window
- Automate payment reminder notices at day 15 and day 30
- Require updated billing details before day 45 cutoff

> notes: This structure removes guesswork for staff and gives members breathing room without requiring bespoke executive approvals for every renewal.
