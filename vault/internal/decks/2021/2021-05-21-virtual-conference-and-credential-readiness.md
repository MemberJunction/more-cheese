## Why we remain purely virtual

- Small creamery attendance increased during past virtual events
- Eliminates travel and hotel costs for one-person operations
- Running dual hybrid tracks creates poor audience experiences
- Allows small breakout sessions capped at productive sizes

> notes: Larger operations with travel budgets want hotel ballrooms back, but accessibility for small makers remains our primary operational priority.
