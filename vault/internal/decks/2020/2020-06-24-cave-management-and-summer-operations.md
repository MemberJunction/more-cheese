## The summer heat challenge

- Small maturation rooms fight twenty to thirty degree differentials
- Basement and walk-in caves experience severe compressor strain
- Minor temperature drifts compound over multi-week stretches
- Ripening speed shifts unpredictably across aging batches

> notes: Late June brings a surge of member inquiries regarding cave drift and condensation issues.

## Practical mitigation steps

- Reduce frequency and duration of daily door openings
- Batch monitoring visits rather than conducting quick checks
- Inspect door seals before assuming compressor failure
- Use sized humidifiers or damp cloths to stabilize moisture

> notes: Emphasize that mechanical fixes are secondary to disciplined room traffic control.
