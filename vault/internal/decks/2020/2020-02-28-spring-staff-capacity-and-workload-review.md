## The bottleneck

- Same staff cover membership operations, course comms, and event logistics
- Deadline for workshop priority review conflicts with webinar prep
- Inbound questions about retail sanitation take precedence over outreach
- Risk of delayed responses to member renewal inquiries

> notes: We cannot run four concurrent spring initiatives with two people handling all tier-one correspondence without dropping workflow steps.

---

## Proposed workload adjustments

- Shift weekly email triage rotation between Events and Membership Operations
- Pause non-essential outbound advocacy follow-ups until mid-March
- Consolidate course reminder notices into a single weekly broadcast
- Dedicate Friday mornings entirely to workshop roster verification

> notes: These adjustments require no new budget or tools, only agreement on what gets dropped for the next three weeks.
