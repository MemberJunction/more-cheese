## Proposed grace window

- Offer 60-day payment deferral on request
- Suspend automated hard lapses for qualifying accounts
- Maintain full member benefits during the grace period
- Track exceptions manually in Membership Operations

> notes: This approach protects member relationships without altering core pricing or rewriting database schemas.
