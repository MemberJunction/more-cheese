## Sixty-day rule friction points

- Raw milk cannot cross state lines for retail sale
- Cheese made from raw milk aged sixty days or more can legally cross
- Many members and buyers confuse state raw-milk sales with interstate shipment
- Confusion costs members legitimate wholesale orders and market access

> notes: This is the core finding from our current letter-writing campaign. Producers need plain-language guidance rather than complex legal text.
