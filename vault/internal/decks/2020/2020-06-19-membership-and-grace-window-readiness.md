## The payment failure bottleneck

- Inbound payment failures have doubled since April

> notes: Rigid enforcement risks losing core members who are active in our federal exemption advocacy work.

## Proposed grace-window protocol

- Implement a formal 60-day hardship extension request
- Restrict extension eligibility to SmallBusiness and Individual tiers
- Require documented pandemic revenue disruption
- Maintain internal logs to prevent accidental lapses

> notes: This keeps members engaged while giving them breathing room. Corporate accounts do not qualify.
