**Subject:** Competition logistics and the judging accreditation pipeline

With Madison's medal table and results finalized in the generated record, this memo addresses the operational aftermath of the 24 competition entries across our six categories and the emerging inquiries around judging accreditation. Members are asking how to get to the other side of the table, and Communications has published the accreditation rules this week. This memo reviews what the entry and judging data actually show, where the bottlenecks lie, and what we need from Education before the fall cohorts open.

## What the Madison entry and medal numbers show

The 2019 competition corpus records 24 entries distributed across six distinct categories: 7 in Alpine Styles, 5 in Soft-Ripened, 4 in Washed Rind, 4 in Fresh & Pasta Filata, 2 in Aged Cheddar & Territorials, and 2 in Blue Veined. On the medal side, Fromagerie Saint-Rémille took three medals under Henri Dubois (Silver in Aged Cheddar & Territorials for Old Alderwick, Silver in Alpine Styles for Reedmere Wheel, and Bronze in Fresh & Pasta Filata for Birchmoor Reserve), while other notable entries included Corbiemoor Creamery Cooperative under Aiko Dahl taking Gold in Aged Cheddar & Territorials for Brackenfell Blue and Silver in Fresh & Pasta Filata for Old Lambswick, Ivywick Creamery Cooperative under Karen Ackerley taking Gold in Washed Rind for Foxcombe Clothbound, Heathercombe Cheese & Provisions under Hugo Colton taking Gold in Fresh & Pasta Filata for Thistlemere Blue, and a second Bronze in Alpine Styles for Rushfen Reserve.

These results are clean, but they highlight an ongoing friction point: members who see the medal table want to jump straight to judging panels without respecting the prerequisite path.

## The accreditation pipeline and prerequisite reality

The public blog post published this week correctly points out that the Competition Judge Accreditation is not a standalone credential. It sits on top of the Sensory Evaluation Certificate. We are receiving inquiries from members who attended Madison and want to judge next year, but who have neither the prerequisite certificate nor supervised panel experience. Furthermore, because the accreditation lapses after two years if a judge sits out, we spend an inordinate amount of staff time verifying who is currently calibrated and who needs to return to supervised panels alongside accredited judges on real competition flights.

Education & Credentialing must ensure our course pipeline feeds this accurately. We have 21 course enrollments taken this week and three course completions, with Advanced Sensory Evaluation (Cedar cohort) starting on 4 August, Rind Development & Washing (Clover cohort) starting on 7 August, and Advanced Sensory Evaluation (Stone cohort) starting on 14 August. None of these alone grant judging accreditation, but the Sensory Evaluation courses are the mandatory gateway.

## Operational strain on shipping and scoresheets

Beyond judging credentials, the physical logistics of getting 24 entries judged and returning scoresheets within a reasonable window exposed staffing gaps. Judges struggled with deep flights—particularly in Soft-Ripened, which was deep enough this year to require extra time. When judges spend extra time on a single category, the turnaround time for scoresheets slips. We need a standardized scoresheet format and clearer shipping deadlines for next year's regional events so that entries arrive properly chilled and documentation matches the entry form without manual intervention from our team.

## What we need

- Education & Credentialing: verify that all registrants for the upcoming Advanced Sensory Evaluation (Cedar cohort) and (Stone cohort) are informed in writing about the prerequisite chain before classes commence on 4 August and 14 August.
- Communications: ensure future public posts about judging do not imply that a strong palate or conference attendance alone bypasses the supervised panel requirement.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a dedicated administrative window in September for the Events Team and Education to reconcile judge calibration records before the Judging Standards webinar (EVT-2019-WEB6) on 18 September.
