> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT** — International Cheese Federation (ICF), a fictitious trade association created for the MemberJunction More Cheese demonstration environment. All names, figures, events, and decisions are invented.

**To:** Office of the Chief Executive; Advocacy & Standards; Communications
**From:** Membership Operations
**Date:** 5 December 2019
**Subject:** Membership lapse rates, grace-window requests, and payment failures

As the year closes and Class III prices sit near nineteen dollars per hundredweight, our incoming membership queues show an unusual volume of stalled renewals and failed card charges. This is not a broad crisis across our current base of 369 members at week end, but it is a sharp friction point for small creameries whose cash flow was squeezed during the earlier, leaner months of 2019. This memo examines why these payment failures are clustering now, how we are handling grace-window requests, and what we need from Communications before the next newsletter.

## What the payment and renewal data show

For the week of 2 December 2019, membership purchases totaled 242, with 22 first-time members joining and a gross of 55,369 USD. Our tier mix in these purchases leans heavily on Individual (162) and SmallBusiness (51), alongside 14 Corporate and 15 Enthusiast purchases. Total confirmed orders across all products reached 259 with a gross of 62,044 USD and only 2 cancellations, while cash captured stood at 7,706 USD.

Beneath those healthy totals, however, we are tracking a persistent wedge of failed credit card transactions and expired payment tokens on SmallBusiness renewals. Several members who want to renew are asking for a 30-day grace window because their year-end distributor payments have not landed yet. Our standard operating procedure requires strict cutoff dates, but holding the line rigidly right now risks converting temporary cash-flow lags into permanent churn.

## The grace-window policy friction

Membership Operations is currently receiving daily requests from creameries asking for informal extensions beyond the standard renewal window. Because our order history starts in 2019, every retention metric is sensitive, and losing a SmallBusiness tier account due to a failed recurring payment on December 1st is an avoidable loss. We have authority to apply short grace periods, but we need an aligned stance so that Advocacy & Standards and Communications are not telling inquirers different things when they call about member benefits during the holiday crunch.

Furthermore, some members are attempting to change tiers mid-renewal—stepping down from SmallBusiness to Individual or vice versa—while their payment is failing. Mixing a tier change with a past-due invoice creates manual reconciliation errors in our order logs. We need to freeze tier changes on lapsed accounts until the base renewal is settled.

## What we need

- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a formal 30-day grace-window protocol for SmallBusiness tier renewals through the end of December without loss of member benefits.
- Advocacy & Standards: confirm that grace-window members retain access to federal regulatory updates and committee notices without interruption.
- Communications: ensure no public-facing newsletter implies that payment failures result in immediate membership termination during the holiday season.
