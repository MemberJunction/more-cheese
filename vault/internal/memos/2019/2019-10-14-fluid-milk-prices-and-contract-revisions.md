## What the market shift means for members

The current price firmness is driven by slower national output growth and steadier retail demand, reinforced by European cheese stockpiling ahead of potential tariffs. While farmstead producers who milk their own herds are seeing better returns on the margin, our buying members are facing rising raw-material costs that will likely persist through the fourth quarter. Several members have already contacted our regional offices regarding supplier contract adjustments. We need to ensure our communications emphasize that this market movement is not a signal to relax existing agreements, but rather a vital moment to review pricing structures before wholesale buyers become accustomed to outdated terms.
