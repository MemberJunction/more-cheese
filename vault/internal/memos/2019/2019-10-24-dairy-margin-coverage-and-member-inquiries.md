**To:** Communications; Advocacy & Standards
**From:** Membership Operations
**Date:** 24 October 2019
**Subject:** Dairy margin coverage and member inquiries

The federal dairy margin support program's enrollment window for next year opened earlier this month, and our phones have been noticeably busier than usual. Members are working through their coverage levels against a firmer but still uneven cheese market, and smaller operations milking under a few hundred cows or running farmstead herds are asking the most questions. This memo records what we are hearing on the lines and how we should handle member inquiries as the filing deadline approaches in mid-December.

## What members are asking

The lower coverage tiers are effectively free or close to it for the first slice of production, so the calculation for a small producer is less about whether to enroll and more about how high to go and whether it alters their wholesale side. A handful of members who called this week were surprised at how much of the 2020 premium math depends on feed assumptions they had not updated since spring. Larger members with diversified revenue lines are telling us the calculation matters less to them this year because underlying margins have been less volatile, but that is not universal.

We also recorded two advocacy actions on dairy pricing reform and one petition signature on the same topic this week. None of this happens in a vacuum: export demand has been mixed, tariff lines on major markets have shifted more than once since spring, and members selling into Mexico or the EU need to keep an eye on their trade lanes rather than assume this year's terms hold.

## The numbers for the week so far

As of today, Thursday, our preliminary figures for the week of 21 October show 5 membership purchases, all of them first-time members across Individual (3), SmallBusiness (1), and Enthusiast (1) tiers, totaling $891 in gross sales. We confirmed 6 orders totaling $675 with 1 cancellation, and captured $485 in cash. There were no course completions or event registrations this week, but 15 course completions were recorded for the year so far. These figures are preliminary until Sunday's close.

## What we need

- Communications: ensure any published guidance points members to the program worksheets without offering individual financial advice.
- Advocacy & Standards: keep our regional contacts updated on feed cost assumptions and tariff line shifts.
- Membership Operations: continue logging member questions on feed worksheets and refer callers to the Membership & Outreach Committee when they need a peer contact.
