We must remind everyone that first-time and retained membership comparisons against prior years carry significant structural caveats. Publishing a year-over-year growth figure or a lapsed-versus-retained split for 2019 would be a misrepresentation of the underlying data, and Communications should ensure no press release or blog post attempts to quote a prior-year comparison.

## Wholesale stocks and the export lane

Industry reports this week highlight tight national cheese stocks and firm block markets, alongside a reopened export lane to our southern neighbor following the resolution of recent tariff disputes. Several wholesale members have contacted Membership Operations regarding whether these market conditions justify long-term contract adjustments.

Our data shows that corporate and small-business members are actively inquiring about export documentation and compliance. However, our records do not track direct inventory volumes for individual creameries. We must advise members to consult their individual buyers rather than relying on association-wide generalizations when locking in fall pricing.
