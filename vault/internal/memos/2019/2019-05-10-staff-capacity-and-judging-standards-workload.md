**To:** Communications; Education & Credentialing; Office of the Chief Executive
**From:** Events Team
**Date:** 10 May 2019
**Subject:** Staff capacity and Judging Standards webinar workload

This memo addresses our operational capacity constraints for the remainder of May. With the Judging Standards 2019 webinar scheduled for May 14 and workshop registrations arriving steadily, our current team structure is stretched across event logistics, course coordination, and member inquiries regarding recent milk-price shifts. We need to agree on who covers what during this busy stretch so that routine work does not get dropped.

## Current volume and registration pacing

Registrations for upcoming events are active. For the week ending May 12, we recorded 2 event registrations taken for the ICF Annual Conference 2019, 6 for Workshop: Curd Retailing, 7 for Workshop: Meadow Affinage, and 3 for Workshop: Clover Pasta Filata. At the same time, course enrollments stand at 17 for the week, with 8 completions and one upcoming cohort starting soon on May 19 for Washed-Curd & Dutch Styles (Stone cohort).

Free webinars carry no paid registrations in our data, so we do not track attendance figures for the upcoming judging webinar. However, participant support and technical routing for an online event of this scale still consume significant staff hours. Communications is running the blog reminder, but the inbound questions about scoring rubrics and tie-resolution are landing on the Events Team and Education & Credentialing.

## The capacity pinch

The overlap between webinar preparation, upcoming summer workshop logistics, and routine course monitoring has created a bottleneck. The same small group handles event operations, course enrollments, and member correspondence. When webinar season peaks alongside spring workshop planning, something has to give. Last week, routine email backlogs grew by two days, and course reminder notices were delayed. We cannot sustain this pace without either dropping non-essential follow-up or shifting responsibilities between teams.

We evaluated whether to push the Washed-Curd & Dutch Styles (Stone cohort) start date back by a week to relieve pressure, but Education & Credentialing has confirmed that cohort scheduling is locked with instructors. Therefore, the intake date must remain fixed.

## What we need

- Communications: take ownership of all pre-webinar logistical FAQ routing so the Events Team can focus on workshop venue contracts for Meadow Affinage and Curd Retailing.
- Education & Credentialing: absorb the routine course reminder emails for the Stone cohort so Events does not have to split attention between workshop registrations and course administration.
- Office of the Chief Executive: acknowledge this temporary capacity strain and approve the deferral of non-urgent committee inquiries until after the May 14 webinar concludes.
- Everyone: adhere strictly to our communication channels and avoid ad-hoc project requests this week.

*Events Team*
