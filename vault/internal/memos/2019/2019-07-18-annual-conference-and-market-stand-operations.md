**To:** Membership Operations; Communications; Education & Credentialing
**From:** Events Team
**Date:** 18 July 2019
**Subject:** Annual conference logistics and farmers market stand operations

The ICF Annual Conference opened Tuesday in Madison, and the preliminary counts from the events table show 109 registered, 12 refunded, and an attendance breakdown of 102 attended with 7 no-shows. These figures are preliminary because the week is still in progress and the tasting tent shade panels and meeting rooms have been running under severe heat wave conditions across the region. This memo outlines our operational bottlenecks for the remainder of the week and connects our on-site findings with the wider member inquiries we are receiving regarding direct-to-consumer and market stand operations.

## Attendance and event logistics

The Tuesday trade panel ran long in a format that worked well for members discussing shifting tariff schedules, but the room scheduling for subsequent sessions is tight. The soft-ripened category judging drew an unusually deep field, requiring an extra hour in the judging room and delaying the scoring finalization. We have also noted that several members transporting product to the competition tables are ignoring the cold room check-in and leaving coolers in parking lots under extreme July temperatures. We need a clearer directive on cold chain compliance before the next event cycle, or we will face product spoilage issues that reflect poorly on the federation.

## The market stand connection

While staff are in Madison managing conference logistics, Membership Operations and Communications report a high volume of member questions concerning farmers market stands and direct-to-consumer sales. Many newer members treat the folding table as an easy entry point without realizing the realities of temperature control and pricing margins. The public blog post published this week on folding-table stands addressed part of this gap, but our course catalog and committee outreach need to ensure the Direct-to-Consumer & Farmers' Markets course offerings are clearly communicated to producers who struggle with cooling and pricing.

Furthermore, the Food Safety Committee completed its Q3 meeting on Tuesday alongside the Awards & Competition Committee meeting, while the Events Committee completed its meeting today. Committee feedback indicates that small-scale producers need more practical templates for food safety plans that match their actual floor operations rather than large-plant templates. We should align our upcoming course reminders with these committee recommendations.

## Capacity constraints

Conference follow-up, committee minutes processing, and inquiries regarding autumn courses are hitting the same core staff. We cannot absorb additional inbound member advisory requests on market stands without deferring routine event reconciliation tasks. We request that Communications direct all complex market stand operational queries to the Membership Outreach Committee rather than routing them directly to the Events Team.

## What we need

- Communications: include a reminder about cold room check-in protocols in the final conference recap.
- Membership Operations: verify the 1 order registration taken this week for the conference and reconcile it against late badge pickups.
- Education & Credentialing: ensure upcoming autumn course cohorts are highlighted in member communications regarding small-scale retailing and market stands.
- Office of the Chief Executive: note that on-site staffing is stretched to capacity due to the convergence of conference judging and committee meetings.
