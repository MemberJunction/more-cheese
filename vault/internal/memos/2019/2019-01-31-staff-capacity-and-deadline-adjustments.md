## The current operational bottleneck

The five-week federal reporting lapse has driven an unprecedented wave of inbound calls to both Membership Operations and our Standards Committee. Members are asking for guidance on raw-milk aging rules, 60-day interstate compliance, and how to price February contracts when federal feed and milk price data remains patchy. ## Advocacy and committee correspondence

Beyond workshop logistics, the Membership & Outreach Committee is managing a heavy intake of member sign-ups for our upcoming advocacy letters regarding dairy pricing reform and surplus cheese distribution. While our annual figures reflect a strong base of 371 current members on December 31, our day-to-day capacity to process member-requested documentation, environmental monitoring inquiries, and committee correspondence has reached its limit. We cannot maintain our regular publication schedule for blog posts, newsletters, and advocacy letters without moving certain deadlines.

## Proposed deadline adjustments

To protect staff from burnout during this busy stretch, we are moving the upcoming advocacy letter filing window back by ten days, and we are requiring Communications to clear all blog post drafts with Membership Operations 48 hours prior to publication to ensure data accuracy regarding milk pricing and cold-storage figures.
