With the release of the winter events calendar and the annual conference scheduled for 16 July in Madison, Wisconsin, we need to lock down the operating schedule for this year's competition entries, transport logistics, scoresheet distribution, and panel judging assignments. The competition corpus from prior cycles shows 24 total entries across six categories: Alpine Styles, Aged Cheddar & Territorials, Fresh & Pasta Filata, Washed Rind, Soft-Ripened, and Blue Veined. We cannot treat logistics casually if we expect entries to arrive in proper condition for July judging.

## Entry deadlines and category targets

The entry window must close early enough for the Awards & Competition Committee to group categories and assign bench stewards before wheels ship. Makers of Alpine styles, washed rinds, and blue-veined cheeses require strict temperature control during transit. We have seen past entries arrive past their prime or with compromised rinds simply because shipping dates were communicated too late in the spring. Communications should ensure that web announcements give clear guidance on cut-off dates without promising unrealistic delivery turnarounds.

## Judging panel calibration and accreditation

As noted in this week's blog guidance, judging accreditation and panel calibration are skills that require active maintenance. The pathway relies on the Sensory Evaluation Certificate as a prerequisite before candidates sit for Competition Judge Accreditation. The Education Committee owns this training track, and we need confirmation on how many candidates are currently cleared to sit beside veteran judges on the Madison panels this July. Placing uncalibrated judges on a panel creates inconsistencies that take days to reconcile.

## Scoresheet standardisation

Scoresheets must be standardised across all six categories before the conference. Whether a panel is evaluating a delicate Fresh & Pasta Filata wheel or an aged Alpine style, the scoring criteria for texture, paste, and rind development must be identical to the standards taught in our education courses. The Awards & Competition Committee should review the scoring rubrics this month so that printing and digital entry forms can be finalised ahead of the spring workshop schedule.

- Awards & Competition Committee: confirm the entry deadline dates and finalise category judging rubrics by 15 February.
- Education Committee: provide the list of members who have completed the Sensory Evaluation Certificate and are eligible for judge accreditation by 28 February.
- Communications: align the spring competition calls with the workshop announcements without altering entry fees or category rules.
