**Subject:** Certification administration and the Q1 exam window

The association closed 2019 with zero certification enrollments recorded for the final week, leaving our annual total at five certification enrollments and six awards across three certificate types: the Food Safety & HACCP Certificate, the Cheese Foundations Certificate, and the Sensory Evaluation Certificate. As we open the intake window for the Q1 examination sittings, several operational issues require alignment between our team and Membership Operations. Specifically, we have encountered duplicate credential records, questions regarding prerequisite enforcement, and a growing number of renewal inquiries that lack matching active membership periods.

## Prerequisite enforcement and exam sittings

The immediate operational bottleneck is the prerequisite check for advanced credentials. Candidates registering for higher-tier examinations have submitted applications before completing their foundational requirements. For instance, the Competition Judge Accreditation requires the Sensory Evaluation Certificate as an explicit prerequisite. When candidates bypass this step in the portal, our team must manually audit the member record. We need Membership Operations to freeze registration approvals for any exam sitting where the prerequisite course completion cannot be verified against the member's profile within forty-eight hours of submission.

## Credential record corrections

Data integrity audits completed at year-end reveal duplicate credential entries for members who renewed under modified organizational names or merged accounts. This has created discrepancies in our historical award counts. While the 2019 annual totals show two Food Safety & HACCP Certificates, three Cheese Foundations Certificates, and one Sensory Evaluation Certificate, several member accounts reflect partial credit that cannot be mapped to a valid completion date. We propose a joint audit of these records before the end of January to prevent issuing duplicate or invalid replacement certificates.

## Renewal timing and grace windows

We are also receiving inquiries regarding credential renewals tied to lapsed memberships. Under current rules, a certification lapses if the underlying membership expires. Several credential holders whose memberships lapsed in the final quarter of 2019 are requesting a grace window to sit for renewal exams without paying a re-application fee. While Membership Operations handled similar grace requests for SmallBusiness tier renewals last month, certification policy does not automatically grant a grace period for lapsed credentials.

## What we need

- Membership Operations: confirm the procedure for flagging membership lapses on active certification accounts before the Q1 exam registration deadline.
- Advocacy & Standards: review our proposed wording for the prerequisite waiver clause to ensure it aligns with committee oversight rules.
- Both teams: agree on a date in January for the joint credential record audit.
