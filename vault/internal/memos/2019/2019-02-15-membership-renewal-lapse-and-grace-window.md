**Subject:** Membership renewal lapse, grace-window requests, and payment failures

Membership Operations is currently managing an unusual cluster of billing inquiries, card rejections, and grace-window extension requests. While the week's new purchases show three additions — one Corporate and two Individual memberships generating a gross of $1,120 — the quiet friction on the renewal side requires a standardized procedure before it impacts our active count. Current members sit at 224 at week end, but several accounts that crossed their twelve-month renewal boundary in late January and early February are sitting in unpaid suspension without a clear policy governing how long we hold access before archiving.

## The mechanics of the current lapse queue

When a member's annual cycle terminates, automated invoices go out through the member portal, followed by a standard grace window. In practice, however, small creameries and independent cheesemongers often experience temporary cash flow pinches that coincide with their renewal dates. This aligns with what our public communications have noted regarding farm-side margins and the broader squeeze on small-scale producers. When a payment fails due to an expired card or insufficient funds, many members assume their standing is protected indefinitely or ask for bespoke extensions that Membership Operations handles inconsistently.

We currently have several accounts asking for 30-to-60-day grace extensions beyond the standard window. Granting these informally creates an audit discrepancy between our active roster and our confirmed cash capture. With total confirmed orders for the week standing at 33 with a gross of $4,964 and cash captured reaching $5,431, our intake is healthy, but uncollected renewals muddy the baseline of who actually holds active voting and portal privileges.

## Policy adjustments and communications coordination

We need a hard rule on grace windows. Allowing an open-ended pause on annual dues leaves staff guessing whether to pull portal access or leave it open while waiting on a wire transfer or updated credit card. Furthermore, Communications needs to ensure that when members reach out via the portal asking about billing issues, they are routed directly to our standard escalation queue rather than receiving casual promises of extended access from other departments.

If we want to support members facing legitimate cash flow pressures—especially smaller producers navigating difficult input costs—we should formalize a hardship grace policy rather than relying on ad-hoc extensions. That policy must have a firm cutoff date after which the account moves to lapsed status and loses access to member-only resources, judging sign-ups, and discounted event rates.

## What we need

- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a standardized 30-day maximum grace window for membership renewals with no further extensions without a signed payment plan.
- Communications: update the member portal FAQs to clearly state that failed payments automatically trigger a 30-day suspension warning before membership rights lapse.
- Membership Operations: audit all accounts currently past their renewal date by more than 30 days and issue final notices by Monday.
