**Subject:** Holiday production surge and staff capacity

As order volume turns the corner toward the end of the year, several farmstead producers have contacted the committee with the same operational bottleneck: balancing holiday demand against normal creamery sanitation and shift schedules. This memo outlines what our membership data and outreach calls indicate for the weeks ahead, and proposes how our small team should coordinate support across education and event reminders without dropping routine responsibilities.

## What the numbers show so far

For the week ending 24 November 2019, membership purchases stand at four, all first-time joins split between three Individual memberships and one SmallBusiness membership, generating a gross of $767. Confirmed orders for the week total five, with a gross of $1,137 and zero cancellations. Cash captured sits at $838. On the education side, course enrollments taken this week reached 18, with one course completion. These figures reflect a steady pre-pandemic baseline, but they land on a staff that is simultaneously supporting upcoming webinar registrations and answering member inquiries on regulatory comment periods.

## The holiday production crunch

Conversations with small-scale producers highlight a recurring structural risk during the November and December rush: delegation. Producers running small crews report that the most critical decision made in September or October is not recipe adjustments, but defining precisely who is authorized to make judgment calls when a make-sheet deviates or when a cleaning cycle threatens to run late. Cutting routine cleaning passes to catch up on holiday volume remains a persistent temptation, and one that invariably costs more time and product than it saves. Our role as an association is to ensure our members have access to the right procedural guidance before they face that pressure.

## Upcoming education and event commitments

Looking across the next eight weeks, our calendar includes three webinars: EVT-2019-WEB5 on Rennet Alternatives on 12 December 2019, followed by EVT-2020-WEB2 on Counter Culture and EVT-2020-WEB8 on Whey Management in January 2020. Additionally, two cohorts start in early December: the Washed-Curd & Dutch Styles course for the Willow cohort on 3 December 2019, and Counter Culture: Retailing for the Granite cohort on 4 December 2019. Coordinating reminders for these courses while handling year-end membership inquiries requires us to tighten our internal hand-offs.

## What we need

- Communications: align the December webinar registration reminders with the advocacy updates on federal comment periods so members are not receiving competing general broadcasts on the same day.
- Education & Credentialing: confirm cohort roster counts one week prior to the December start dates so membership records match enrollment files.
- Events Team: review webinar attendee capacity caps before the mid-December session to prevent Q&A overflow.
- Everyone: maintain agreed weekly response times for member inquiries despite the incoming holiday order volume.
