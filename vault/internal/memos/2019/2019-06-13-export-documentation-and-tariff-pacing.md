## What the course curriculum covers

Export Documentation Essentials is built around the exact problem members faced when the June 1 tariff shift hit: shipments that were invoiced and packed in May but landed in June were subjected to new duties because customs-declaration timing overrules invoice dates. The course walks through commercial invoices, certificates of origin, food health certificates, and HS classification codes. Classification remains the primary stumbling block, as a cheese that sits ambiguously between two categories on a make sheet can land in a higher tariff bracket purely due to descriptive mismatches on the paperwork.

We are keeping the class size small so participants can bring their actual export invoices and certificates of origin. In past sessions, the most valuable part of the instruction has been finding small discrepancies—such as a lab result dated a day outside the window or a facility address mismatch—before a shipment gets held in a bonded warehouse.
