**Subject:** Small-producer exemption documentation and the food safety workload

The Food Safety Committee has received an unusually high volume of inquiries this month regarding the qualified facility exemption under the federal preventive-controls rule. Members continue to call asking whether the exemption waives food-safety documentation altogether. The short answer we are giving is no. While the rule allows small facilities that sell mostly direct to consumers or local retailers to skip formal hazard analysis and written preventive-controls plans, it does not waive basic sanitation practices or the attestation itself. This memo outlines what our advisory workload looks like this week and what we need other teams to understand before publishing further guidance.

## What members are missing on paper

The core misunderstanding among callers is treating the qualified facility exemption as a permanent pass. Two criteria determine eligibility: average annual sales over the last three years and the split between direct-to-local sales versus everything else. Members who grew this year, added wholesale accounts, or picked up distributor relationships may have quietly aged out of the exemption without realizing it. Furthermore, inspectors require proof of the sales attestation, current labeling disclosing the facility name and address, and basic sanitation records. Most small creameries need a binder and a recurring calendar reminder, not an outside consultant, but they need us to explain how to structure those records.

## What the public post is communicating

The Communications Team published an overview on 19 August directing members to review last year's sales numbers now rather than waiting for inspection time. That advice aligns with what our committee is telling callers, but it has created a secondary wave of phone calls asking for template documentation. We do not provide legal forms, and we must be careful not to imply that our committee's walkthrough replaces professional compliance advice. We should ensure all staff answering member inquiries are aligned on this boundary.
