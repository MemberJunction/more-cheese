**Subject:** Interstate shipment and rating readiness

Following this week's public blog post on crossing state lines, we have seen an uptick in member inquiries regarding the federal and state milk sanitation rating system. Creameries attempting to ship product two states over are discovering that distributor compliance departments require verification of plant listing status before accepting shipments. This memo outlines the current guidance we are providing to members and the friction points we have identified through our state advisory channels.

## The rating reality

Grade A milk and milk products moving across state lines are governed by a cooperative framework between federal regulators and state milk sanitation programs. A facility must undergo rating by a certified state officer covering producing farms, receiving stations, and the pasteurization plant. This listing is what permits distributors to accept interstate shipments without duplicating inspection work.

Smaller producers expanding their distribution footprint frequently encounter this requirement only when a deal is already finalized. The lead time required to coordinate with state sanitation officers and reconcile farm records routinely catches growing creameries off guard. While the underlying standards are identical for large and small operations, the compliance burden feels disproportionately heavy for a small producer with limited administrative staff. The Membership & Outreach Committee has raised this concern with state advisory panels to ensure compliance pathways accommodate smaller staffing models.

## Alignment with education and events

Our Food Safety Committee has structured this year's plant food-safety workshop series specifically around pathogen control, environmental monitoring, and sanitation recordkeeping. These sessions are designed for small operating crews rather than large processing plants. However, we need to ensure our portal resources clearly distinguish between general food safety compliance and the specific administrative steps required to secure listing on the interstate milk shippers list.

Communications should verify that the plain-language walkthrough on the member portal remains easily accessible for members who are crossing state borders for the first time. If members encounter state-level procedural hurdles that appear excessively burdensome, those details should be routed to us for inclusion in our ongoing advisory discussions.
