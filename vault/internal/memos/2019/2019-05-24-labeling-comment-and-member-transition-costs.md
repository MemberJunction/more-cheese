**Subject:** Labeling proposal comments and member transition costs

The public advocacy post published on 20 May regarding the new labeling proposal has already drawn inquiries from several small farmstead members. As the comment window remains open through the end of next month, we need to ensure our internal guidance matches what we are advising members in writing. The core friction is not the principle of clear labeling—most members support that—it is the six-month transition timeline and the specific list of reserved terms that threaten existing packaging inventories.

## The transition window problem

Our coalition letter focuses on the transition timeline. On paper, six months looks reasonable to a larger plant with dedicated compliance staff. For a small producer who prints labels once a year in a single large batch to control unit costs, six months can mean discarding thousands of dollars of packaging that uses terms like "cave-aged" or regional style names now swept into the reserved list.

We have advised members that individual comments carry more weight with regulators than a coalition letter alone. Regulators read a coalition letter as general industry awareness, but they read an individual comment as a specific business impact. To make those comments effective, producers need to state exact numbers: how many labels they must scrap, what the shortened transition costs them in dollars, and how long their current print run is intended to last.

## Standards Committee alignment

The Standards Committee has reviewed the draft language and is assisting members who are unsure whether their current label copy falls under the proposed restrictions. We must ensure that our communications team and our membership operations staff give consistent answers when contacted by members trying to calculate their exposure before the comment deadline.

## Capacity and follow-up

Handling these inquiries is adding to the weekly workload of the Advocacy & Standards team just as we prepare for the summer workshop schedule and the upcoming annual conference preparations. We are not asking for additional headcount, but leadership should note that member questions on this docket will peak between now and the close of the comment window.

## What we need

- Communications: maintain a pointer on the member portal to the docket guide and ensure the Standards Committee contact link remains prominent for label reviews.
- Membership Operations: flag any inquiries regarding membership tiers or small-producer status changes that stem from regulatory compliance costs and route them directly to Advocacy & Standards.
- Office of the Chief Executive: note that the six-month transition window is our primary point of objection in all ongoing federal dialogue.
