**Subject:** Export demand surge and operational readiness for specialty members

Federal trade figures released this fall indicate that domestic cheese exports are running on pace for one of the strongest years on record, placing acute pressure on specialty and artisan members shipping into overseas markets. While a strong export quarter represents an encouraging demand signal and validates long-term capacity investments, the operational friction reported by members requires a coordinated response across our teams. This memo reviews the structural bottlenecks identified by our wholesale members and outlines our supportive posture for the remaining five weeks of the year.

## Current volume and membership context

Our weekly operating snapshot for the week ending 1 December 2019 records 348 current members at week end, with 2 new membership purchases totaling 477 USD, comprising one Individual membership and one SmallBusiness membership. Total confirmed orders for the week stand at 3 orders generating 622 USD in gross sales, with 290 USD in cash captured. Course enrollments taken this week reached 27, alongside 1 course completion. Notably, while our broader annual metrics reflect 1,120 confirmed orders and 243,425 USD in gross revenue across 2019, the immediate concern among our export-oriented producers is not aggregate sales volume but fulfillment logistics.

Larger operations with established distribution networks are experiencing double-digit growth in order volume compared to the same period last year. Conversely, smaller creameries utilizing third-party distributors are encountering severe friction: compressed shipping schedules, rigid cold-chain window requirements, and extended lead times for export documentation. Members report that customs paperwork, health certificates, and country-specific labeling requirements are bottlenecking in December queues as volume surges across all sectors.

## Committee alignment and resource distribution

Our governance records show 24 committee meetings held this year, with active oversight from our six standing committees, including the Standards Committee chaired by Amelia Ochoa with Iveta Jansen-Holzer as Vice Chair, and the Membership & Outreach Committee chaired by Marco Ashworth with Ruth Grantham as Vice Chair. Advocacy actions year-to-date total 32 across topics such as raw-milk aging rules, FSMA small-producer exemptions, and labeling standards. While regulatory compliance remains our baseline, members navigating international shipments are requesting practical guidance rather than policy revisions.

We must ensure that communications and member outreach efforts accurately reflect the realities on the ground without generating panic or implying formal advisory authority. Communications has published guidance emphasizing documentation lag, cold-chain capacity confirmation, and early distributor cutoffs. We must reinforce these messages internally and ensure staff do not inadvertently promise expedited regulatory clearances that fall outside our institutional scope.

## What we need

- Membership Operations: maintain the running list of export-experienced members willing to mentor peers through their first international fulfillment cycle, routing inquiries through the member portal.
- Events Team: ensure upcoming webinars, including the scheduled 12 December webinar on Rennet Alternatives, account for member pre-holiday operational strain without overloading registration commitments.
- Communications: continue aligning public messaging with our practical operational notes, ensuring no figures are published outside verified data bounds.
- Advocacy & Standards: monitor member inquiries regarding international shipping documentation bottlenecks and aggregate feedback for our next committee cycle.
