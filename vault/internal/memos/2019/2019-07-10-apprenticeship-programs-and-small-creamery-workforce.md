**Subject:** Apprenticeship programs and small creamery workforce readiness

Tomorrow's free webinar, "Apprenticeship Programs 2019," arrives at a moment when small creameries are reporting severe seasonal staffing bottlenecks. While national numbers on federal grant money for manufacturing and trades have shifted this year, dairy production has seen little of that funding. Several member creameries have asked whether a registered apprenticeship model is worth pursuing or if the informal version most small producers run is sufficient. This memo reviews what we are telling members on the call, what the limits of that guidance are, and how education data connects to workforce recruitment.

## What the webinar will and will not cover

The session on 11 July focuses entirely on the craft and mentorship side: establishing a written learning plan, sequencing skills from the first month to the ninth, and setting up internal oversight at the vat. It is designed for operations running on a single vat and a handful of staff where a formal registered-apprenticeship filing carries excessive administrative overhead.

We have been careful to draw a firm boundary: this is not a payroll or wage-and-hour briefing. If a member's question is about legal classification or compensation compliance, we redirect them to private counsel. Furthermore, the session does not cover recruiting. Members repeatedly tell us that job boards are less effective than course rosters for finding reliable candidates. Someone who has attended a federation course or workshop has already demonstrated genuine curiosity in the room.

## Capacity and seasonal pressure

Summer is when supply-chain and cold-chain challenges peak, putting maximum stress on creamery staff. A short-staffed make room or retail counter risks compromised product if milk delivery or refrigeration falters. While bringing on an apprentice can alleviate this, winging the supervision without a structured plan costs more in oversight time during the first month than a half-hour of planning saves. The webinar aims to prevent that friction by providing a ready-to-use template for internal mentoring.
