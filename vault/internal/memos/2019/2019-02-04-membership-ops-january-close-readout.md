**Subject:** January close — what the numbers say and what they don't

January is closed on the membership side. The headline is fine and the caveats are more
interesting than the headline, so please read past the first paragraph before anyone quotes
anything.

## Where we landed

222 members held an active membership period at 31 January, carrying $60,952 of dues across the
four tiers: 148 Individual, 46 Small Business, 15 Corporate, 13 Enthusiast. Auto-renew is switched
on for 64 of the 222, or 28.8 per cent, which is lower than I would like and is not distributed
evenly — Small Business is at 21.7 per cent and Individual at 29.7 per cent. Detail by tier is in
the attached close sheet.

On the commerce side we booked 266 orders in January for $67,369 gross, of which $67,245 was
applied and $124 remains outstanding. Cash captured in the month was $63,117. The gap between
applied and captured is timing on late-January card settlements and will close in February.

## The caveat that matters

**Do not publish a new-member count for January.** Our billing and membership-period history
starts at the 1 January 2019 baseline, so every currently active member has a period that begins
on 1 January regardless of when they actually joined the federation. The query that would normally
give us "first-time members this month" returns all 222, which is obviously wrong — Bob Kowalski
has been a member since 2013 and shows up in it.

Practically: for 2019 we can report **active members** and **dues billed** with confidence, and we
cannot report **new versus renewing** at all. That will resolve itself from January 2020, when
we'll have a genuine prior year to compare against.

## A variance I can't explain yet

Individual-tier membership order lines booked in January total $27,870 across 162 lines, against
$25,840 of dues sitting on the 148 Individual periods that start in January. That is a $2,030 gap
and 14 extra lines.

My working theory is that those 14 are January-booked orders against periods that start later in
the year, which would be benign. I also notice the Individual line carries two unit prices in the
data, $175 and $145, and I do not have documentation for the $145 rate. I would like an hour with
someone from Events or the Office to work out whether $145 is a legacy rate, a promotional rate, or
a mistake. Until then, please quote Individual membership at the list price of $175 and nothing
else.

## Events, briefly

Registrations for the two February workshops as of Sunday: Cedar Packaging in Tandil on 18
February has 25, and Prairie Food Safety in Lancaster on 27 February has 18. Both are early but
Lancaster is the one I would watch. Twenty-three workshop registrations came in over the last
week, so the curve is moving.
