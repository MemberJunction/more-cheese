> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT** — International Cheese Federation (ICF), a fictitious trade association created for the MemberJunction More Cheese demonstration environment. All names, figures, events, and decisions are invented.

**To:** Membership Operations; Office of the Chief Executive
**From:** Education & Credentialing
**Date:** 12 March 2019
**Subject:** Certification audit, renewal backlog, and exam administration

This memo addresses the current state of our credentialing administration following the recent award of the Cheese Foundations Certificate to Lea Marceau and the public inquiries prompted by our blog posts on the Certified Cheese Professional credential. We have a growing accumulation of records needing prerequisite verification, alongside a three-year renewal cycle for CCP holders that requires stricter tracking than our current spreadsheet allows.

## The prerequisite bottleneck

The Certified Cheese Professional credential assumes candidates hold the Cheese Foundations Certificate and can document qualifying working hours. As noted in recent public guidance, candidates frequently underestimate the time required to reconstruct their work history. Currently, Education & Credentialing is manually verifying these hours against employer sign-offs. With course enrollments at 15 for the week and a new Allergen Management cohort starting on 29 March, manual verification is creating a bottleneck for exam applicants. We cannot process candidate sign-offs at our current volume without delaying course administration.

## The three-year renewal cycle

Unlike the five-year window on basic credentials, the CCP is valid for three years before renewal. This shorter window is intentional, reflecting how quickly business and food-safety practices evolve. However, our tracking of upcoming expirations is disconnected from Membership Operations' data feed. Members whose credentials lapse often assume their status remains active as long as their annual membership dues are paid. We need a unified flag for credential expiration so that renewal notices go out alongside membership renewals rather than as a separate, uncoordinated mailing.

## Exam sitting logistics and record corrections

We also face a series of record corrections where members have changed creamery affiliations mid-career or completed coursework under former names. Each correction requires a manual trace through past enrollments. Furthermore, planning for upcoming exam sittings requires locked seat counts well in advance. If we continue to accept prerequisite documentation up to the week of the exam, we risk administrative errors on test day.

## Capacity and next steps

The division between course enrollments, certificate awards, and credential renewals is managed by the same small staff handling event coordination and committee support. We are flagging this not to request additional headcount, but to adjust our processing timelines before the summer conference run.

## What we need

- Membership Operations: agree on a shared data format for credential renewal flags by 22 March.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a hard cut-off date for exam prerequisite submissions four weeks prior to any scheduled sitting.
- Education & Credentialing: audit all pending CCP applications against logged working hours before the end of the month.
