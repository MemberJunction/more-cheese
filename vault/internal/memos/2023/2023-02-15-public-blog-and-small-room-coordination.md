Our public blog post correctly describes the session as free with no registration caps. However, we must ensure that no draft mentions attendance figures, since free events do not generate registration data. The post points members toward the member portal to register, which aligns with our operational workflow.

We must also ensure the post does not give specific legal, veterinary, or financial advice regarding food safety in converted closets or basements. Advocacy & Standards has reviewed the framing and confirmed it avoids regulatory overreach.
