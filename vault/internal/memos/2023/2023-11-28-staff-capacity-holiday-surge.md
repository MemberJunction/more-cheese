**Subject:** Staff capacity during the holiday shipping surge

Holiday volume is landing across all departments earlier than usual, and our incoming numbers show the strain. As reported in our current membership and order tracking for the week of 27 November 2023, confirmed orders have reached 531 with a gross value of $131,337, while cash captured stands at $17,316. Membership purchases total 499 with a gross of $121,095, including 21 first-time members. At the same time, we have taken 24 event registrations for the upcoming Curd Competition Prep workshop in Green Bay and 23 course enrollments, alongside 8 course completions. These volumes are overwhelming our shared operational capacity, and we need to decide immediately which tasks to defer.

## Where the friction is concentrated

The overlap between membership processing, inbound questions regarding interstate shipping rules, and cut-and-wrap course inquiries is creating bottlenecks. Advocacy & Standards is receiving a high volume of calls regarding holiday state-level documentation hurdles, while Membership Operations is managing 499 membership purchases across Individual (345), SmallBusiness (99), Corporate (23), and Enthusiast (32) tiers. Meanwhile, Education & Credentialing is supporting 23 new enrollments while preparing for the Advanced Sensory Evaluation cohort starting on 5 December. Because our staff roster is lean and shared across teams, the same people answering member shipping questions are also supporting workshop logistics and course administration.

## The deadline that has to move

We cannot sustain all parallel deliverables without dropping quality. Specifically, the proposed interim report on small-producer exemption advocacy actions must be pushed back by two weeks. Attempting to finalize that report while processing the current weekly order volume of 531 confirmed items and 4 cancellations will result in errors across both financial tracking and member communications. Advocacy & Standards has agreed that shifting the report deadline is the safest course of action.

## Work that gets dropped

To protect core transaction processing and member-facing support during the December rush, we are officially dropping routine outbound check-ins for lapsed members until the first week of January. We are also pausing non-urgent committee meeting scheduling for the remainder of this month. Staff previously allocated to those tasks will be reassigned to assist Membership Operations and Education & Credentialing with inbound volume.

## What we need

- Office of the Chief Executive: formally approve the two-week delay for the small-producer exemption advocacy report.
- Events Team & Education & Credentialing: confirm receipt of reallocated support staff by Wednesday morning.
- Advocacy & Standards: redirect incoming holiday shipping inquiries to our standard digital checklist rather than custom advisory calls where possible.
