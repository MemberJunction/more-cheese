**Subject:** Louisville readout — what the attendance record shows and what is still open

The Annual Conference ran on 25 July in Louisville. The registration record shows 290 registered, 268 attended and 22 no-shows, with no refunds. These are preliminary: they come from the attendance field as it stood when we pulled it on Friday morning, and we are still reconciling it against the badge-scan log. Communications is writing the recap that the 26 July post promised for next week, so this memo is mostly about which of these numbers it can safely use and which loose ends belong to us.

## What the record shows

Of the 290 registrations, 268 are marked Attended and 22 are marked No Show. Nothing was refunded. If the badge-scan reconciliation changes anything, we expect it to move people from No Show to Attended rather than the other way, because a missed scan is more likely than a phantom one.

The registration table also shows 14 registrations taken during the week of 24 July. We have not yet checked how many of those were taken before the conference date and how many after. Someone should look before we describe the 14 as late sign-ups in anything that leaves the building.

## What the public posts have already promised

Two things are in print that we now have to deliver.

- The 26 July post says a full recap with competition results and session takeaways will go up next week. Results were held for the closing dinner, so Communications needs the final results sheet from the Awards & Competition Committee before it drafts that section. We do not have it yet.
- The 28 July post tells entrants their scoresheets will arrive within a few weeks of the closing dinner, with comments from both the technical and aesthetic judge. That is a commitment made on our behalf without a date attached. Nobody has agreed an owner or a ship date for the scoresheet mailing.

I would rather set an internal date now than discover in three weeks that "a few weeks" meant different things to different people.

## Capacity

The team worked every day of the conference week, and the webinar on 1 August is already on the calendar. The scoresheet mailing, the no-show follow-up and the recap inputs all land in the next two weeks. I do not think we can do all three well without something moving. This is a note, not a request for headcount, but the Office of the Chief Executive should know that the post-event work is competing with routine events work.

## What we need

- Communications: tell us which attendance figures the recap will quote, so we reconcile those first and leave the rest.
- Office of the Chief Executive: agree an owner and a mailing date for entrant scoresheets by 4 August.
- Membership Operations: confirm whether the 22 no-shows should get a follow-up note, and whether that note comes from you or from us.
- Everyone: treat 268 attended as preliminary in any draft until we confirm the reconciliation.
