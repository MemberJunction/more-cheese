> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT** — International Cheese Federation (ICF), a fictitious trade association created for the MemberJunction More Cheese demonstration environment. All names, figures, events, and decisions are invented.

**To:** Advocacy & Standards; Communications; Office of the Chief Executive
**From:** Membership Operations
**Date:** 16 August 2023
**Subject:** Margin support and membership coverage review

The 14 August public post on July feed-and-milk margins and wholesale block cheese firmness has generated inbound questions from small farmstead members who want to know whether they should adjust their coverage elections. This memo reviews what the membership numbers show so far for the week of 14 August and examines whether our guidance to members matches the actual pace of renewals and tier distribution.

## What the membership numbers show

As of the middle of this week, total membership stands at 815 current members. Membership purchases for the current week total 13, comprising 5 first-time buyers and 8 renewing accounts. By tier, 12 of these purchases are in the Individual tier and 1 is in the Enthusiast tier, generating a gross of 2,082 USD in membership sales. Confirmed orders across all lines total 16 with a gross of 2,588 USD, and cash captured is 3,448 USD. Zero cancellations have been recorded.

These figures indicate steady, unremarkable intake. However, the volume of inquiries regarding feed-and-milk margin coverage suggests that members are conflating general market commentary with association administrative services. We do not administer the federal margin program, yet our staff is spending time clarifying that distinction.

## The gap in member preparation

The blog post correctly noted that members who check their coverage only during a squeeze end up deciding under pressure. Our data supports this observation: members in small business and farmstead tiers frequently let their coverage elections lapse or drift until milk price volatility forces an emergency renewal or inquiry. The Pricing & Margin for Specialty Cheese course remains our primary educational tool for this, yet course enrollments this week are concentrated elsewhere, with 28 enrollments taken across starting cohorts but no immediate rush into margin-specific sessions.

We need to decide whether Membership Operations should issue a brief, direct FAQ for members on where to find federal margin resources, or if Advocacy & Standards should handle the distribution to prevent our office from answering administrative questions outside our scope.

## Capacity and coordination

With the Listeria Control in Small Creameries (Birch cohort) starting on 19 August, staff capacity in education and membership ops is stretched across course preparation and incoming orders. If we encourage further inquiries about feed-and-milk margins without a pre-written guide, we will absorb a support bottleneck that we lack the staff to clear.

## What we need

- Advocacy & Standards: review and approve a short explanatory note on federal margin coverage sources that Membership Operations can send to inquiring members by Friday.
- Communications: ensure future blog posts on commodity prices link directly to the federal program portal so inbound calls reach the agency rather than our team.
- Office of the Chief Executive: acknowledge that small creamery margin anxiety is driving administrative overhead this month.
