## Commodity signals versus artisan reality

As noted in our recent industry outreach, commodity cheese prices track federal order milk prices closely, whereas artisan and farmstead pricing reflects specific herd management, labor, and make processes. When extension reports out of the upper Midwest describe plants short on capacity and producers dumping loads, members look to us for guidance on whether their own costs and make schedules should shift. Our education catalog is the primary tool we have to answer that question systematically, particularly through courses that address seasonal milk variation and cold-chain hygiene.

We must ensure our course promotion does not conflate commodity margin pressure with farmstead milk quality challenges. Members managing heat-stressed herds need practical guidance on set times and whey drainage rather than macroeconomic commentary.
