> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT** — International Cheese Federation (ICF), a fictitious trade association created for the MemberJunction More Cheese demonstration environment. All names, figures, events, and decisions are invented.

**To:** Advocacy & Standards; Office of the Chief Executive; Communications
**From:** Membership Operations
**Date:** 13 July 2023
**Subject:** Milk price margin response and co-op member outreach

The federal supply and demand report cut the all-milk price forecast by 65 cents to sit just below $20 per hundredweight, with Class III taking a steeper drop while Class IV inched upward. That report, combined with feed costs that continue to pinch margins, has already generated incoming calls from cooperative managers and creamery owners asking what support the federation offers. This memo outlines what our numbers show so far for the week of 10 July and proposes how we coordinate our response before member inquiries multiply next week.

## What the numbers tell us and what they don't

For the current week ending 16 July, our membership purchases stand at 14 total, generating a gross of $2,634 across SmallBusiness, Individual, and Enthusiast tiers, while total confirmed orders reached 63 with a gross of $23,435 and cash captured at $22,557. Current members at week end total 792. None of these purchasing figures capture the underlying farm-gate margin squeeze described in the recent federal report; indeed, membership sales and order volume have remained steady on the surface even as settlement statements for milk producers have softened.

We must be careful not to conflate steady federation membership purchases with healthy farm-level margins. A creamery owner buying or renewing an annual membership does so on an annual budget cycle, whereas their milk check arrives monthly and reflects the Class III erosion immediately. When members call about the margin squeeze, quoting our gross order volume or our total current member count does not answer their question and can read as tone-deaf.

## Existing resources and their limits

Our public communications have pointed members toward safety-net programs like Dairy Margin Coverage and advised fluid buyers to check their August and September pricing commitments directly with suppliers. However, neither our blog posts nor our standard guidance provide a direct mechanism for cooperative managers who need help translating federal forecast data into internal pricing sheets for artisanal wheels that take six months to age.

Furthermore, while the Membership & Outreach Committee maintains a running list of advocacy actions and open comment periods—such as the recent letter campaign on dairy pricing reform which logged one active action this week—that committee's mandate does not cover milk price stabilization or feed cost hedging. Directing a distressed cheesemaker to a federal risk management program when their immediate crisis is cash flow from an August milk check is a mismatch.

## Capacity and coordination

We are currently managing preparations for the ICF Annual Conference in Louisville with 48 new registrations taken this week, bringing our event pipeline closer to capacity. At the same time, Advocacy & Standards is tracking state-level raw milk rules and dairy pricing reform without dedicated staff for individual consultations. If we encourage members to call in with margin questions, we need a clear triage protocol so that Membership Operations does not absorb questions that require economic analysis we cannot provide.

## What we need

- Advocacy & Standards: provide a short, one-page FAQ by 19 July translating the federal milk price cut into plain terms for small cheesemakers who buy fluid milk rather than milking their own herds.
- Communications: ensure upcoming blog posts or member briefs do not oversell the efficacy of safety-net programs without noting their enrollment deadlines and coverage thresholds.
- Membership Operations: log all incoming margin-related inquiries separately from standard billing questions so we can report exact call volumes at the end of July.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a brief coordination meeting between Membership Operations and Advocacy & Standards before the Louisville conference opens.
