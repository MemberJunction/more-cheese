**Subject:** Membership renewal lapses, grace windows, and corporate tier updates

As we close out the first full week of September, membership operations is managing an unusual clustering of overdue accounts and grace-window inquiries from small business and corporate accounts. While our overall member count stands at 822 at week's end, and we recorded 14 membership purchases this week totaling 2,658 USD in gross sales across 11 Individual, 2 SmallBusiness, and 1 Enthusiast tier additions, the intake side is masking some friction in our renewal pipelines. Specifically, two mid-sized distributor accounts and one regional farmstead member have hit their renewal anniversary without payment, triggering our standard lapse workflow just as they request grace-window extensions tied to cash-flow pressures.

## The current renewal bottleneck

The gap between retail performance and wholesale board flatness, which we discussed in this week's public blog posts, is showing up directly in our membership renewal queue. Members operating wholesale distribution accounts are reporting tighter margins than anticipated following the summer renegotiation cycle. Consequently, when their annual dues invoices arrive, several are hesitating and asking for 30- to 60-day grace windows rather than letting their status drop immediately. Under our current operating rules, a grace window keeps their portal access active but leaves their financial record flagged as delinquent, creating a mismatch between our active roster and our cash-captured figures, which sit at 2,700 USD for the week against 19 confirmed orders totaling 3,518 USD.

We also face a tier-change dispute with a corporate member attempting to downgrade from a Corporate tier to a SmallBusiness tier while retaining multi-seat benefits. Our records show their production volume remains well above the small business threshold, but their billing contact insists that the recent plateau in the federal all-milk price forecast justifies a temporary dues reduction. Allowing an ad-hoc tier shift without verified production data sets a difficult precedent for our 37 Corporate and 136 SmallBusiness accounts.

## Interlock with advocacy and standards

This administrative friction intersects directly with our current advocacy messaging on labeling standards and standard of identity comment periods. Members who feel squeezed on dues are also the ones asking for staff assistance on regulatory comment letters regarding cheese product definitions and temporary market-testing permits. If our team spends half its bandwidth arbitrating grace windows and chasing failed credit card payments, we have fewer cycles to help farmstead producers process open comment windows before deadlines close.

Furthermore, our data shows 8 first-time members added this week out of the 14 total purchases, indicating that acquisition remains steady even as renewal retention stumbles on commercial accounts. We need a clear association-wide stance on how aggressively we enforce the 30-day grace window cutoff for members experiencing cash-flow gaps.

## What we need

- Exec: confirm whether we can grant a standardized 45-day grace window for commercial accounts affected by wholesale contract lags without opening us up to widespread tier downgrades.
- Advocacy: review the list of delinquent commercial accounts to ensure we do not cut off access to standard-of-identity comment alerts for members currently stuck in payment processing limbo.
- Membership Operations: finalize the weekly close spreadsheet and flag all accounts exceeding the 30-day delinquency threshold by Monday morning.
