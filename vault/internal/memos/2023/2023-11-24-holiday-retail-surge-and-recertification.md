The week ending November 26 brings together two conflicting pressures for our membership: a strong retail push in gift-boxes and holiday charcuterie wedges, balanced against persistent margin squeezes that neither a busy counter nor the recent federal farm bill extension can fully erase. At the same time, we face a hard operational wall this week with the impending November 30 recertification deadline for our Certified Cheese Professional credential. This memo reviews where our numbers stand so far and what we need to align before the month closes.

## Where the operating numbers stand

Weekly commerce data captured through Sunday shows 10 membership purchases, bringing gross membership sales for the week to 2,381 USD. Of those 10 purchases, 7 are first-time members, and the tier breakdown sits at 9 Individual and 1 Corporate. Total confirmed orders for the week reached 35, generating 5,675 USD in gross orders against 1 cancellation, with cash captured at 5,613 USD.

On the education and event side, we registered 23 attendees for the upcoming December 19 Workshop: Curd Competition Prep in Green Bay. Course enrollments taken this week reached 26, while course completions stood at 6. Advocacy actions recorded a single CoalitionMeeting on raw-milk aging rules. Our total current membership count at week's end stands at 874.

## The retail and margin split

Members operating retail counters and farm stands report heavy holiday demand for gift boxes and aged wedges, creating a very busy counter schedule. However, as noted in recent industry discussions, this front-loaded volume stresses small staffing models without erasing the underlying margin pressure from elevated feed costs and commodity milk price lags. The federal farm bill extension passed last week keeps the existing margin-coverage program running as-is, but it does not fix the formula gap that several creameries have raised with us. We should ensure our communications do not conflate a busy retail week with a healed production margin.

## The recertification deadline

Our November 30 recertification deadline for the Certified Cheese Professional credential is five days away. Credentials that lapse over this holiday week require sitting for the full exam from scratch rather than taking the shorter renewal path. Credential holders who have been away from daily counter or cave work often find the renewal check on food safety, labeling, and storage harder than expected. We should verify our tracking list to catch anyone who might lose their credential simply because thanksgiving week distracted them from the paperwork.

## What we need

- Communications: reinforce the November 30 recertification cutoff in upcoming reminders without sounding alarmist.
- Education & Credentialing: prepare support materials for members who find the renewal exam prep more demanding than anticipated after time away from the counter.
- Events Team: maintain focus on the Green Bay workshop preparations as December approaches.
