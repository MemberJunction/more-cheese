**Subject:** Membership lapse processing and grace-window requests

As wholesale cheddar prices slide into a strange summer and our spring member numbers settle at 744 current members by week's end, Membership Operations is seeing an uptick in inquiries from accounts requesting grace-window extensions on lapsed renewals. Two commercial members and one small business account have contacted us this week asking to hold their member status past the standard 30-day payment buffer while they renegotiate distributor contracts. This memo outlines our current operational volume for the week of May 29, the policy constraints around grace-window exceptions, and the decisions needed before we reply to the pending requests.

## Weekly operations and membership volume

For the week ending June 4, 2023, our commerce data records a total of 8 membership purchases, breaking down into 4 first-time members and 4 returning purchases. By tier, the week's purchases include 4 Individual memberships, 3 Enthusiast memberships, and 1 SmallBusiness membership. Total gross membership sales for the week reached $1,431, with overall confirmed orders standing at 58 for $12,457 in gross volume, one cancellation, and total cash captured at $13,795. These figures are complete through the end of the week.

Against a total base of 744 current members, an 8-purchase week is routine, but the concentration of grace-window requests is not. When accounts with wholesale exposure face a soft spot-market or pushback from buyers, cash-flow friction immediately hits their association dues. The accounts asking for extensions are pointing to the exact same commodity softening covered in recent industry reporting, arguing that a delayed distributor payment should not forfeit their member standing.

## The grace-window policy friction

Our current operating rules enforce a strict 30-day grace period following an annual membership expiry before the status reverts to lapsed and portal access is suspended. Membership Operations has no administrative authority to grant an unbilled extension beyond that window without risking audit inconsistency across tiers. Granting a waiver to a commercial or small business account without a formal policy backing creates an uneven playing field against members who paid on schedule during the same market downturn.

At the same time, forcing a hard cutoff during a week when members are already navigating complex contract pricing formulas risks permanent churn among accounts that have renewed reliably for years. We are caught between maintaining strict data integrity in our membership roster and accommodating long-standing participants who are experiencing temporary cash-flow lag due to broader dairy pricing shifts.

## Capacity and team bandwidth

Handling these manual review requests requires coordinating with the Advocacy & Standards team, who frequently field questions regarding how members can utilize association resources during margin squeezes. With event registrations also active this week—specifically 18 registrations taken for the ICF Annual Conference 2023 in Louisville and 23 registrations for the Workshop: Birch Competition Prep—our small team is stretched across registration reconciliation and membership exception processing.

## What we need

- Office of the Chief Executive: confirm whether Membership Operations is authorized to issue a standardized 15-day administrative extension for accounts currently locked in distributor contract renegotiations.
- Advocacy & Standards: advise whether our standard member advisory notices can include a brief reminder on payment grace windows without implying financial or legal counsel.
- Membership Operations: hold all three pending grace-window requests in a pending status until executive leadership responds by Friday noon.
