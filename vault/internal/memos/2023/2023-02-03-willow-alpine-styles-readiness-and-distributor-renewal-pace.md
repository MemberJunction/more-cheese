Late January and early February form our heaviest renewal window, and this week's figures show 8 membership purchases totaling $1,272 in gross sales, bringing current membership to 667 at week's end. Four of those eight purchases are first-time members. Alongside the renewal cycle, the Events Team is preparing the EVT-2023-W1 Workshop: Willow Alpine Styles in Greensboro, which has taken 44 registrations so far and stands at 134 total registered with 3 refunds. This memo reviews how our renewal inquiries intersect with the workshop preparation and why we need tighter coordination before Monday's start.

## What the renewal and registration numbers show

The 8 membership purchases this week break down to 7 Individual tier and 1 Enthusiast tier. Cash captured across all association operations reached $23,701, and confirmed orders stand at 53 with 1 cancellation. Meanwhile, the workshop in Greensboro has captured 44 registrations during this week alone, reflecting strong early February demand. Trade press commentary circulating this week notes that specialty cheese counters are rebuilding full-service offerings, which directly affects the distributor and cheesemonger segments.

However, our distributor members are asking questions about directory updates and interstate shipment rules rather than course listings. When mailbox prices for January came in noticeably lower than December's, distributor reps reported that smaller accounts grew cautious about signing new supply contracts. Several members have called to verify whether directory searches and advocacy notes are fully current before they commit to their annual renewal.

## The operational friction

We have two conflicting signals in our inbound mail. On one hand, members calling about renewal are asking for the directory and advocacy alerts, mirroring what our public blog discussed regarding the rep who tracks margin, not milk. On the other hand, attendees arriving in Greensboro on Monday expect a fully reconciled venue and immediate answers on make-sheet troubleshooting for Alpine styles.

The friction is that the workshop is hands-on rather than lecture-heavy, meaning attendees bring their own wheels—some finished, some mid-affinage—and expect facilitators to troubleshoot rind development and paste texture on the spot. If our registration count of 134 (with 3 refunds and 5 no-shows historically recorded across the event lifecycle) shifts between now and Monday morning, the Greensboro venue layout will need last-minute adjustments.

## Capacity and risk

Staff capacity is fully stretched between managing the late-January/early-February renewal notices and staging next week's workshop. We cannot afford to have staff answering duplicate inquiries about membership grace windows while venue setup in Greensboro is underway.
