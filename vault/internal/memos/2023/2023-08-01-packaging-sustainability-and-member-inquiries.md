**Subject:** Packaging sustainability webinar and incoming member inquiries

Today we ran the free webinar on packaging sustainability. Because free webinars carry no registrations in our data, we do not have a head count to quote, but the chat activity during the session was high enough to suggest that members are actively thinking about materials, supplier changes, and customer pushback on wrap. This memo sets out how our team intends to handle the follow-up questions and where that intersects with Communications and Education.

## What members are asking

Two themes came up repeatedly during the session and in the email inbox immediately following it. First, members are asking about recycling streams for cheese wrap — specifically whether multi-layer barrier films used in small-scale affinage are going to hit compliance walls in retail markets. Second, a handful of producers asked about provenance labeling and cold-chain sensor tags, picking up on the conversation that started on the floor in Louisville last week.

We need to be careful with our answers. Neither the webinar nor our committee work gives anyone a pass on local food safety rules, and a scannable provenance label is a record of conditions, not a guarantee of safety. If a member asks whether a smart label satisfies a state inspection requirement, the answer is no, and we need our team answering the inbox to give that same answer consistently.

## Alignment with public messaging

Communications published a post yesterday about Louisville and touched on trade show conversations, and another post later this week will cover smart labeling. Our technical guidance must match what is going into those posts without overpromising what packaging technology can fix. Margins are tight across the membership this summer, and members looking for a silver bullet for milk price squeeze will sometimes mistake a traceability label or a sustainable wrap for a margin solution. We must not encourage that framing.

## Education and coursework connections

Members wanting a deeper dive into technical standards and labeling rules should be pointed toward our existing courses rather than expecting staff to draft custom advice. The Labeling Law & Compliance course (Willow cohort) and Allergen Management cover the regulatory side, while the Sensory Evaluation Certificate pathway covers tasting vocabulary that ties into quality control. We should ensure the Education & Credentialing team has these course titles ready for anyone who writes in wanting more than a webinar overview.

- Education & Credentialing: confirm that course availability links in the member portal are live for the upcoming cohorts.
