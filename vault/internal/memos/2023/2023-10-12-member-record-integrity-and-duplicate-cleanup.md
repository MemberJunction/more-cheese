> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT** — International Cheese Federation (ICF), a fictitious trade association created for the MemberJunction More Cheese demonstration environment. All names, figures, events, and decisions are invented.

**To:** Communications; Office of the Chief Executive; Membership & Outreach Committee
**From:** Membership Operations
**Date:** 12 October 2023
**Subject:** Member record integrity, duplicate identities, and stale employer data

As we approach the end of the year and prepare our fourth-quarter reporting, our team has completed an internal audit of active member profiles in the database. While current membership stands at 838 members at the end of this week, our review has uncovered persistent data-quality issues that require immediate administrative intervention. Specifically, we have identified duplicate member records, bounced member email addresses, and outdated employer associations that distort our outreach capabilities. This memo outlines the scope of the problem and proposes a structured cleanup schedule before annual renewal notices go out.

## The scope of duplicate and stale records

The most pressing data anomaly involves duplicate profiles created during conference registrations and online course enrollments. The most prominent example in our active dataset is the recurring duplicate pair involving Kate O'Leary and Kathy O'Leary, who have on multiple occasions been treated as separate individuals despite sharing identical institutional affiliations, or whose contact histories have split across overlapping profile IDs. Maintaining separate records for the same individual breaks our continuing education tracking and skews our first-time member statistics.

In addition to duplicate identities, we are tracking a significant number of stale employer records. For instance, Aisha Bell's profile continues to reflect an outdated employer affiliation rather than Fernholt Creamery, creating friction when regional committee rosters or workshop lists are pulled. Furthermore, several corporate and individual tier renewals are currently generating bounced notification emails because the primary administrative contact left their organization without updating the portal credentials.

## Why this matters for external communications and committees

These database discrepancies are no longer just an internal inconvenience for Membership Operations. When Communications pulls mailing lists for blog digests or event announcements, bounced addresses increase our bounce rate and risk triggering spam filters. More importantly, when the Membership & Outreach Committee reviews engagement metrics or farmstead retention rates—such as the five-year margin coverage case highlighted in our recent member spotlight—inaccurate employer data makes it difficult to verify who is actually running a creamery versus who has moved on to a different commercial venture.

We also face discrepancies between payment statuses and active membership flags. A small subset of members show active status despite failed or unapplied payments, while others who have settled their dues are occasionally left in a pending grace window due to manual processing delays. These are data-quality problems, not true churn, but they undermine confidence in our monthly membership reports.

## Capacity and timeline for cleanup

Membership Operations is currently staffed to handle routine membership purchases—which totaled 7 new sign-ups this week, including 1 Corporate, 5 Individual, and 1 Enthusiast tier—alongside event registration processing for the 28 attendees taking the Rind Bloomy Rinds workshop and the 21 taking Granite Sensory. We cannot execute a full database deduplication without pausing other administrative tasks unless Communications and the Membership & Outreach Committee assist with manual verification of flagged accounts.

## What we need

- Communications: cross-reference our flagged bounce list against newsletter distribution logs and suppress inactive email addresses immediately.
- Membership & Outreach Committee: review the duplicate consolidation list for prominent member accounts, starting with the Kate O'Leary / Kathy O'Leary records, to confirm institutional history.
- Office of the Chief Executive: authorize a two-week administrative freeze on self-service profile creation changes while Membership Operations merges duplicate IDs.
- Membership Operations: deliver a finalized audit report on record integrity before the November board packet preparation begins.
