**To:** Advocacy & Standards; Office of the Chief Executive
**From:** Membership Operations
**Date:** 14 December 2023
**Subject:** Member renewal lapse, failed payment handling, and grace-window governance

As we close out December, Membership Operations has encountered a recurring friction point involving member accounts whose annual renewals failed due to expired payment methods or processing errors just as the holiday shipping surge began. While current members at week end stand at 856 and total membership purchases for the week reached 11 across Individual, SmallBusiness, and Enthusiast tiers with a gross of 1999 USD, a handful of prominent institutional accounts are currently sitting inside their 30-day grace window without a confirmed renewal. Because these accounts access member-only advocacy filings and the technical help desk, we need an agreed standard operating procedure for handling grace extensions when payments stall during peak holiday production.

## The current operational friction

Under our rolling 12-month membership model, accounts whose payment fails receive automated notifications, but automated messaging often gets buried under holiday order management. When a member calls in asking for a manual extension or a temporary grace override so they can participate in coalition meetings or access regulatory guidance, our team currently has no formal policy to lean on. We have been granting ad-hoc extensions, which creates inconsistency across tiers and leaves us exposed if a lapsed account participates in sensitive committee work or coalition actions.

This week's data shows 11 membership purchases totaling 1999 USD, which confirms that routine renewals are still moving, but the accounts hitting payment walls are disproportionately small businesses and multi-operator creameries that rely heavily on our advocacy and labeling support. Telling a producer whose cave is full of holiday inventory that their membership lapsed over a failed transaction fee damages retention during the exact weeks they value the federation the most.

## What the rules and numbers suggest

We know from our annual figures that we retained 567 members from the prior year while 71 lapsed, out of a year-end base of 859 current members on December 31, 2023. Every avoidable lapse hurts that retention curve. However, extending grace windows indefinitely without a credit card on file or a confirmed renewal order creates phantom memberships that distort our active counts and give unfinancial entities a vote or a voice in governance.

The core disagreement between our team and Advocacy & Standards is whether a grace window can be manually stretched past 30 days when a member claims cash-flow strain caused by feed costs or holiday receivable delays. Our position is strict: grace is a technical buffer for payment retry, not a financing tool. If an account cannot clear payment within the window, their member status must lapse until renewal funds are captured.

## Capacity and risk

Membership Operations is currently running at capacity handling year-end reconciliations, cash capture tracking (which stands at 14400 USD for the week), and event registration inquiries for the upcoming workshop in Green Bay. Chasing failed payments manually for accounts that refuse to update their billing profiles takes time away from onboarding first-time members and supporting active ones.

We cannot continue to arbitrate individual payment exceptions on a case-by-case basis without clear guidance from leadership. If Advocacy & Standards wants to protect access for members involved in active raw-milk aging rule coalitions or FSMA small-producer exemption campaigns, they need to establish a formal hardship provision rather than expecting Membership Operations to look the other way when payment authorization fails.

## What we need

- Advocacy & Standards: confirm whether members in a payment grace window retain eligibility for coalition meetings and raw-milk aging rule advocacy actions.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a standardized 30-day hard stop on payment retries, after which unpaid accounts automatically transition to lapsed status without manual override.
- Membership Operations: implement an automated pre-lapse warning email sent 10 days before the grace window expires, effective January 1.
