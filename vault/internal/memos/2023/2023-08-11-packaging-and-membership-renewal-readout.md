**Subject:** Packaging follow-up and membership renewal pace

The public blog post published on 7 August regarding last week's packaging sustainability webinar has generated a noticeable bump in member inquiries about breathable wraps and mono-material films. Several small shops and farmstead producers have contacted our helpdesk asking whether the associated worksheet covers waxed alternatives for bloomy rinds. At the same time, our weekly numbers show 8 membership purchases booked so far, with 5 of those being first-time members. This memo reviews how those two tracks intersect and where our renewal pace stands as we cross the middle of August.

## What the membership figures show

For the week of 7 August, the confirmed order total stands at 8, generating a gross order volume of $1802. Cash captured for the week reached $2126. Membership purchases totaled 8, with 5 first-time enrollments and 3 renewals, breaking down by tier into 6 Individual memberships and 2 SmallBusiness memberships. Gross membership sales for the week came to $1710. Total current membership at the end of the week sits at 810 members.

These figures are preliminary until the month-end close. However, the ratio of first-time members—5 out of 8 purchases—continues a positive trend that began in the spring. Small shops joining for the first time are frequently asking about operational resources, which directly connects to the packaging and labeling questions highlighted in recent association communications.

## The packaging inquiry volume

Following the 7 August blog post, the Membership & Outreach Committee has received several requests for clarification on whether municipal recycling streams actually accept mono-material films. Members are reporting friction between supplier marketing claims and local recycling realities. As noted in the webinar recap, cheese producers are anxious about holiday packaging changes as autumn approaches. While the webinar recording and worksheet are live on the member portal, staff are spending significant time answering individual questions about label liability and moisture management for bloomy rinds.

We need to ensure that the webinar follow-up materials are completely aligned with what our helpline team is telling callers. If members are still confused about matching wrap types to cheese styles, the worksheet may need a brief revision or an accompanying FAQ section before the autumn rush.

## Capacity and coordination

Membership Operations is currently managing both the incoming membership surge and the secondary questions spawned by recent webinars. With course enrollments at 27 for the week—including upcoming cohorts like Listeria Control in Small Creameries starting on 19 August—staff capacity is stretched across multiple operational fronts. We cannot absorb an open-ended advisory role on packaging compliance without pulling time away from membership onboarding and dues reconciliation.

## What we need

- Communications: include a brief reminder in next week's member digest pointing to the existing packaging worksheet rather than opening a new inquiry queue.
- Events Team: confirm whether any additional virtual sessions on labeling standards are planned before Q4.
- Office of the Chief Executive: acknowledge the current membership count of 810 as our working baseline for the August financial review.
