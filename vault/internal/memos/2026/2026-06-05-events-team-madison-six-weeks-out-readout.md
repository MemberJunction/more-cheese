**Subject:** Madison six weeks out — what the registration pace shows and what it does not

The Annual Conference opens in Madison on 21 July. This week we took 46 conference registrations and 13 registrations for the Stone Food Safety workshop the day after. All figures below are preliminary, pulled from the week snapshot before the week closes on Sunday, and will be restated then.

## What the week shows

Forty-six conference registrations in one week, six weeks out, is a healthy pace for a year in which staff spent the spring bracing for more hardship cases. Workshop interest is steadier than we expected: 13 registrations for a session that runs the day after the conference, in a different city, which tells us some members are building the trip around it.

Monday's public post said early registrations are "running ahead" of this point last year. That is directionally fine, but we have not run the week-matched comparison against 2025 ourselves, and this memo does not claim it. Until we do, internal use should stay with the weekly counts above and not repeat the comparison as a number.

## What it does not show

The registration count says nothing about who is coming. We have not split the 46 by member tier, and we have not checked how many are second attendees from the same creamery, which the post suggests is rising. That split matters for room-block sizing, and it is the reason the hotel block is still open.

It also says nothing about the competition. Entry counts sit with the Awards & Competition Committee and are not in this snapshot.

The early registration deadline is the next cliff. In past years cancellations clustered after it, and we would rather plan for a soft second half of the window than discover it in July. This is a judgment from experience, not a figure from the data.

## Succession and traceability tracks

The program leans on succession sessions and a full traceability recordkeeping day, both mentioned in Monday's post. Advocacy & Standards is still deciding how much of the exemption discussion belongs in the traceability day, given that nothing has been proposed (see Wednesday's post). We will not schedule speakers around a rule change that does not exist yet.

## What we need

- **Membership Operations:** a registrant split by tier and by creamery for the 46, by Thursday 11 June.
- **Communications:** hold any "ahead of last year" figure out of the next post until the matched comparison exists.
- **Office of the Chief Executive:** a decision on whether to extend the exhibitor renewal window, as the post hints we might for members who ask early.
- **Events Team (us):** keep the hotel block open through the end of June and report pickup weekly.
