## The export inquiry pattern

The inquiry pattern has changed from prior years. Historically, export questions came almost exclusively from our largest corporate members expanding existing overseas channels. This spring, smaller producer members are asking about health certificates, customs brokers, and which foreign markets are forgiving of a first-time shipment. These members are running lean offices, and the paperwork barrier is real. When they ask us whether an export line is worth the administrative overhead, we have to give them an honest appraisal of freight costs and margin erosion rather than treating export growth as an unalloyed win for every creamery.

## FSMA small-producer exemptions and regulatory workload

Our advocacy work this week included one recorded action on testimony concerning FSMA small-producer exemptions. The Food Safety Committee and our advocacy desk continue to field questions about environmental monitoring, documentation trails, and hazard analysis. While national dairy product price projections have held firmer than expected and feed cost pressures have eased compared to 2025, regulatory compliance remains the single biggest operational burden for our small business and farmstead members. A producer cannot simply pivot to exporting without ensuring their food safety documentation withstands both domestic audit and foreign customs scrutiny.

## Coordination with education and outreach

Because members are asking about export logistics and food safety simultaneously, we need to ensure our internal resources point in the same direction. The Membership & Outreach Committee is well-positioned to field initial calls, but technical questions about health certificates and brokerage naturally flow to our course offerings, such as the Export Documentation Essentials course, and our certification pathways like the Food Safety & HACCP Certificate. We should make sure staff across desks are not giving conflicting advice on whether a small creamery should attempt a first export shipment before their hazard analysis is fully updated.
