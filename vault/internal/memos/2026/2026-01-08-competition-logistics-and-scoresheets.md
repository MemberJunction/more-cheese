## Entry deadlines and category caps

The annual competition relies on clean entry data and strict adherence to cutoffs. Last year we saw a total of 94 entries, distributed across Soft-Ripened, Alpine Styles, Aged Cheddar & Territorials, Blue Veined, Fresh & Pasta Filata, and Washed Rind. For the current cycle, we must maintain strict window enforcement so that the Awards & Competition Committee chairs can assign judges without last-minute scrambling.

Membership Operations must verify that any entity submitting a cheese holds a current membership status before the intake manifest is frozen. We have noted in previous committee meetings that late entries create bottlenecks during sample intake and damage the integrity of the blind judging sheets.

## Shipping and judging logistics

Physical samples require precise cold-chain handling and timely delivery to the designated judging room. The Events Team must coordinate with our shipping logistics partners to ensure that perishable entries arriving from farmstead and small-scale creameries are unpacked, inspected for temperature compliance, and cataloged immediately upon receipt.

Judges will score entries using standardized evaluation sheets covering technical execution and aesthetic merit. The past season's medalists—including entries from Fromagerie Saint-Rémille, Nornbrook Dairy, Stonewick Cheesemonger, and Dubois Farmstead—demonstrated that detailed technical feedback is what members value most from the process. We must ensure our scoresheets capture both numerical scores and actionable qualitative remarks.

## Scoresheet turnaround and publication

One of the recurring friction points in past cycles was the delay between the closing dinner and the return of scoresheets to entrants. Producers invest significant time and resource into crafting competition entries, and waiting weeks for feedback diminishes its utility for their make-room adjustments. We are setting a strict internal target to dispatch all scoresheets within two weeks of the final medal announcements.

Communications will need the final medal tally and recipient list as soon as the committee completes its review. We must avoid publishing provisional medal lists that require subsequent corrections.
