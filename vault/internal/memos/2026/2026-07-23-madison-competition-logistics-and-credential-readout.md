The ICF Annual Conference wrapped Tuesday in Madison, and the immediate operational question is how to handle the intake logistics, scoresheet distribution, and credential exam room demand for the rest of the week. With 380 total registrations taken for the conference and 338 attendees confirmed on site alongside 42 no-shows, the reworked floor layout held up under pressure, but the intersection of competition entries and credential exams created friction we need to document before the autumn workshop cycle begins.

## Competition intake and the soft-ripened category tight finish

The competition floor ran separate from the general vendor traffic this year, which successfully kept judges isolated from the public crowd. However, the sheer volume in categories like Soft-Ripened created bottlenecks during intake. Judges reported that the top two entries were separated by less than a point, hinging entirely on rind development rather than paste texture. That narrow margin required extra deliberation time, pushing back the schedule for the public judging-observation sessions that filled every block on day two. Members waiting to observe the scoring process had to be re-routed, and while attendees praised the transparency of watching an accredited judge explain their rationale, our staffing ratios on the floor were stretched thin.

## The Certified Cheesemonger credential crunch

Education & Credentialing managed a heavy wave of exam sitters alongside the conference schedule. Members testing for Certified Cheesemonger ran into scheduling conflicts between the written exam and the hands-on portion—cutting a wheel cleanly, wrapping for sale, and describing an unfamiliar cheese. Several first-time members, including retail staff who joined only days before the event, attempted to schedule both portions back-to-back despite our advisory notes. The gap between knowing production theory and executing counter tasks under observation caught several candidates off guard.

## Capacity and cash flow during conference week

Our preliminary figures show 9 conference registrations and 52 workshop registrations for the Stone Food Safety session in Bega taken during the week, contributing to confirmed orders of 69 and gross order revenue of $13,620 with equal cash captured. These numbers are solid, but staff capacity is near its limit. The same individuals handling badge reconciliation are also answering retail member inquiries about institutional wholesale purchasing solicitations posted for October through December delivery. We cannot absorb additional administrative tasks without dropping follow-up response times.

- Awards & Competition Committee: finalize the category results format for the member portal by Friday so communications can link out without manual data checks.
- Education & Credentialing: issue explicit scheduling separation guidance (requiring a full day between written and practical exams) for all autumn certification sittings.
- Membership Operations: reconcile the 42 conference no-shows against badge scans before issuing the post-event survey.
