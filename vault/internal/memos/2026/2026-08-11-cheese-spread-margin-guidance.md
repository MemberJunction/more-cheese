## What the spot spread signals

The block-and-barrel spread reflects diverging milk supplies for different processors, not an overall shortage of cheese. First-half production data shows national cheese output running roughly one percent ahead of last year. However, barrel-cheese makers supplying the shred and process markets are currently tighter on milk than block makers. Distributors moving barrel-format products for foodservice are operating from a compressed supply window and will push back hard on wholesale price increases, whereas retail block buyers face a softer market.

Members negotiating contract renewals in the next few weeks need to understand which side of that spread their buyers inhabit. A barrel distributor has less negotiating flexibility than a retail block buyer. We must ensure our guidance remains descriptive rather than prescriptive, pointing members to their own cost sheets rather than suggesting specific price floors.
