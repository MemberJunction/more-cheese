**Subject:** Judging & Competition Preparation — what we say about accreditation before Friday

Communications has Friday's post on Competition Judge Accreditation in draft. The Judging & Competition Preparation course (Curd cohort) starts on 20 May, and this memo sets out what the draft can safely say, where the wording needs care, and what we still do not know about demand. The short version: the credential description is fine, the lapse language needs a softer edge, and we have no enrollment number to quote yet.

## What the post can say

The Sensory Evaluation Certificate is the prerequisite, and supervised panel experience comes before the accreditation is issued. Both are accurate and both can stay. The description of paired panel roles (one judge scoring down from a perfect score for flaws, one scoring up for qualities that stand out) matches how the Awards & Competition Committee described it when it met on Monday 12 April for its Q2 meeting. Observers are welcome at panels. That is in the draft and it is correct.

## Where the wording needs care

The draft says the accreditation "lapses fast." That is true in direction but we do not publish a lapse interval, and I would rather the post not imply one. Members who read "fast" will ask us for a number, and the number belongs to the committee, not to this team. I would keep the sentence about checking with the committee and drop any hint of a timeframe.

I would also keep the post from suggesting the course guarantees a panel seat. The course is the supervised step; seating is the committee's call.

## What we do not know yet

Enrollment for the Curd cohort is zero as of this week. The `vault-facts.mjs week` output for 12–18 April shows no course enrollments taken, and that is a preliminary read: it reflects orders already processed, not interest. Three course completions landed this week, all in other cohorts. I think this is a timing problem, not a demand problem. Registration opens this month and the post has not run yet. Please do not read the zero as weak interest.

Because the 2021 flagship competition is still virtual, panels are meeting in shorter, more frequent sessions. That is the case for a first-timer, and it is the reason the post leans on it. The Events Team should confirm the session cadence with the committee before we repeat it anywhere else.

## What we need

- **Communications:** soften the lapse sentence as above and remove any implied timeframe before the Friday post goes out.
- **Events Team:** confirm with the Awards & Competition Committee that the shorter, more frequent virtual panel sessions are still the plan.
- **Office of the Chief Executive:** no decision needed; flagging that enrollment is at zero for now so it does not surprise anyone at the next check-in.
- **Everyone:** do not quote an enrollment or attendee figure for the Curd cohort until Education & Credentialing supplies one.
