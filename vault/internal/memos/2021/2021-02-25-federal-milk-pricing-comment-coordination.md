**Subject:** Federal milk pricing comment coordination and small-producer outreach

The federal milk pricing formula review has generated more inbound questions this month than any advocacy topic since last autumn. Our 2021 advocacy totals stand at 100 recorded actions across all categories, with dairy pricing reform and raw-milk aging rules capturing the heaviest member engagement. While the federal margin coverage safety net triggered again this week, providing some relief to members insured under the program, the underlying class price formula remains built around large-plant commodity economics. Small-batch and farmstead producers continue to report that the official formula fails to reflect multi-product make schedules and real input costs.

## What the comment window requires

Regulators are accepting public comments on minimum class price formulas, but small-batch members routinely hesitate to file because they assume their individual figures carry no weight against submissions from high-volume processors. Our task this week is to lower that barrier. The Standards Committee has drafted a short, adaptable template that focuses strictly on local volume, make schedules, and actual milk costs rather than legal arguments. We need Communications to point members toward this resource without making it sound like a bureaucratic chore, and Membership Operations to help us flag creameries that have expressed margin concerns in recent correspondence.

It is vital that we keep our messaging distinct from the margin payment news. The margin check is an income-over-feed-cost safety net; the pricing rule is about class minimums. Conflating the two in member communications invites confusion about what regulators have the power to change. We want members submitting concrete numbers from their own operations so hearing officers see a representative cross-section of small-scale realities.

## Capacity and coordination

Advocacy & Standards is managing the comment template distribution alongside our routine committee work. We cannot individually assist every member who requests help, so we are relying on a self-service model supported by committee volunteers. If the volume of requests increases next week, we may need Communications to assist with email triage. We should also ensure our public blog posts continue pointing interested makers to our committee contacts without overwhelming our small staff.

## What we need

- Communications: verify that the pricing comment template link in our upcoming messaging points directly to the current draft and matches our committee guidance.
- Membership Operations: share any recurring questions from farmstead members regarding milk pricing so we can refine the template notes.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve the final wording of the comment submission guide before we circulate it more broadly next week.
