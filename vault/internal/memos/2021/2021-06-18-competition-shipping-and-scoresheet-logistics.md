## Entry Verification and Member Records

Verifying incoming cheese entries against active member tiers has revealed friction between membership status and submission eligibility. Small creameries submitting wheels for categories like Washed Rind or Blue Veined occasionally experience lag between their membership renewal date and entry processing. We must ensure that every entry fee of $65 per cheese is correctly reconciled against confirmed order lines before the judging panel receives the physical wheels. The Awards & Competition Committee, chaired by Mary Ives with Vice Chair Danielle Marchand, requires clean entry manifests without unresolved billing questions. If a member's record is under review or caught in a grace window, their submission must be flagged immediately so we do not disqualify valid entries due to administrative delays.

## Shipping Logistics and Cold Chain Integrity

Transporting perishable cheese samples to judging sites requires strict adherence to cold-chain requirements. Members shipping entries for our fall workshops and conference competitions frequently ask whether courier delays or temperature fluctuations will invalidate their score. We need clear guidance published on the member portal regarding insulation standards, transit time limits, and tracking numbers. A wheel arriving compromised by heat is an avoidable loss for a small farmstead producer. The Events Team will coordinate directly with designated receiving cold rooms to log arrival times and immediately notify producers if a consignment is delayed or damaged in transit.

## Scoresheet Standardization

Feedback from past judging panels indicates that scoresheet formats vary slightly between categories, causing confusion during tabulation. We must standardize the evaluation criteria across all six categories—covering rind development, paste texture, aroma, and flavor profile—so that judges evaluate Alpine Styles and Soft-Ripened wheels under identical scoring thresholds. Education & Credentialing should review the technical rubrics to align them with our Certified Cheesemonger and Sensory Evaluation course materials, ensuring consistency for candidates pursuing their Competition Judge Accreditation.
