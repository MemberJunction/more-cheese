**Subject:** Packaging compliance and holiday order risk

As members build their holiday cases, packaging questions are moving from a secondary compliance chore to an active order risk. State and local restrictions on single-use plastics continue to tighten, and retail buyers are increasingly asking wholesale suppliers to defend recyclability claims before they lock in fourth-quarter inventory. With the Webinar: Packaging Sustainability 2021 scheduled for November 2, we need to ensure our internal messaging and guidance to members accurately reflect what is defensible at small-batch production volumes.

## Defensible claims versus marketing shorthand

The central issue is that much of the material labeled as recyclable in the specialty cheese sector does not actually flow through standard recycling streams without contamination. Vacuum-sealed films, wax-coated rinds, and laminated paper-and-plastic wraps frequently fail municipal sorting standards. Members who print "recyclable" on their labels without verifying local recycling capabilities are exposing themselves to regulatory scrutiny and pushback from wholesale buyers. Our advocacy and education guidance must encourage members to move toward mono-material packaging where possible, or to drop unverified claims rather than risk enforcement action.

## Wholesale lead times and distributor friction

Beyond labeling language, physical packaging supply chains are presenting real friction. Regional distribution partners report that lead times between order and delivery have lengthened compared to last year. Members relying on specialized wrap materials are experiencing backorders that threaten to leave cut-and-wrap stations idle during peak holiday volume. We must ensure that our upcoming events and communications emphasize lead-time buffer planning so members do not assume their December orders will arrive on the previous standard schedule of a week or less.

## Alignment with public communications

The Communications team has highlighted the upcoming November 2 webinar on packaging sustainability, which aligns well with our regulatory priorities. However, we must ensure that neither the blog posts nor our direct member advisories overstate what small creameries can realistically achieve before the holiday rush. Advising a complete packaging redesign weeks before Thanksgiving is counterproductive; the focus must remain on immediate label compliance and securing existing packaging stock.

## What we need

- Communications: review the November 2 webinar messaging to ensure it emphasizes practical labeling checks rather than capital-intensive packaging overhauls.
- Events Team: confirm that the November 2 webinar Q&A session reserves sufficient time for members to ask about mono-material availability at small-batch order volumes.
- Membership Operations: cross-reference current member inquiries regarding wholesale shipping delays with our upcoming advisory notes.
