**Subject:** Certification administration and prerequisite review for the current cycle

This week has brought a familiar administrative friction point to the surface: credential records that require prerequisite verification before an examination sitting or certificate award can be finalized. With certification enrollment at 1 for the week and zero awards so far, our volume remains modest, but the manual verification overhead per candidate is climbing. We need a standardized approach to prerequisite checks, specifically for candidates attempting advanced credentials without completing foundational coursework.

## The prerequisite gap

The root of the administrative delay is a mismatch between how applications are submitted and how our credential tracking records are queried. Candidates frequently apply for specialized credentials while completing prerequisite courses elsewhere or relying on professional experience waivers that lack proper documentation in the member profile. When Education & Credentialing receives an exam fee payment, our team has to manually verify whether the prerequisite modules—such as sensory foundations or food safety units—have been formally completed and recorded.

In practice, this means applications sit pending while we email instructors or cross-reference enrollment tables. That delay frustrates candidates who have already paid their exam fees and expects staff to act as auditors rather than administrators. We currently have no automated flag in the member portal to prevent exam registration when a prerequisite is missing.

## Market context and timing

As noted in our recent public communications regarding pricing models and operational planning, members are tightening their administrative processes this spring. When a small creamery invests in staff credentials, they expect a clear timeline from registration to exam sitting. Delays caused by internal record-keeping gaps undermine confidence in our credentialing framework. Furthermore, our annual data shows that certification awards across the entire year are concentrated in specific certificates—such as the Food Safety & HACCP Certificate, Cheese Foundations, and Certified Cheesemonger—each requiring distinct prerequisite paths that our current manual review process handles inconsistently.

## What we need

- Membership Operations: establish a shared protocol by 14 May for flagging unverified prerequisites at the moment an exam fee order is confirmed.
- Education & Credentialing: audit all pending candidate files this week to ensure no applicant is waiting on an internal sign-off.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a brief policy clarification stating that exam fees are non-refundable if a candidate fails to provide prerequisite documentation within thirty days of registration.
