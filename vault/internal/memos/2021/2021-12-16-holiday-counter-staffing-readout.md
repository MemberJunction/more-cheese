**To:** Communications; Events Team; Education & Credentialing
**From:** Membership Operations
**Date:** 16 December 2021
**Subject:** Holiday counter staffing and remote pairing support

Retail members are managing holiday rushes with skeleton crews this month, echoing the broader national labor shortages that have left specialty counters struggling to backfill shifts. In conversations with our regional contacts, counter managers report dropping complex gift-box assortments and moving in-person tastings online to survive the December push without burning out remaining staff. This operational strain sits alongside our ongoing advocacy and education efforts, and it highlights how closely our members' day-to-day survival depends on practical, low-friction support from the federation.

## What the week's numbers show

Membership purchasing activity continues to move steadily despite the seasonal crunch. For the week ending 19 December, we recorded 9 membership purchases totaling 1,552 gross dollars, with 5 first-time joiners across Enthusiast, Individual, and SmallBusiness tiers. Total confirmed orders for the week reached 18, generating 2,731 gross dollars with zero cancellations, and cash captured stood at 7,850 dollars. Event registrations saw traction with 6 sign-ups for the upcoming Cedar Competition Prep workshop in Chicago and 3 for the Valley Export Readiness workshop in Seattle. While course enrollments remained flat this week, Cheese Chemistry's Prairie cohort is two weeks into its run, proving that technical online learning fills up when makers need immediate answers.

## Retail coping strategies and our response

Members running specialty cheese retail are leaning on pragmatic simplifications: reducing twelve-tier gift box menus down to four manageable options, prerecording tasting videos instead of staffing live pairing tables, and reassigning senior mongers to repetitive tasks so temporary help can be deployed safely. We should ensure our regional check-ins through the Membership & Outreach Committee actively capture these workarounds. When retail members call to compare notes, our team needs to offer concrete operational insights rather than generic encouragement.

## Capacity and coordination

Our internal capacity is stretched as we close out the fourth quarter and prepare for early 2022 workshops and courses. Between managing membership renewals, fielding pricing reform inquiries, and supporting online cohorts, our staff is operating with zero slack. We must ensure that communications and advocacy efforts do not inadvertently increase administrative burdens on retail members who are currently managing counters down one or two people.

## What we need

- Communications: ensure upcoming blog posts and member briefs acknowledge current retail staffing realities without sounding overly optimistic.
- Events Team: maintain clear coordination on upcoming workshop registrations for Chicago and Seattle so we can track capacity accurately.
- Education & Credentialing: monitor the Prairie cohort's progression and prepare registration details for the next course run in the new year.
