**Subject:** Storytelling cohort operations, relief documentation readiness, and member outreach

This week brought two overlapping developments that require coordinated messaging across our teams: the opening of the Storytelling & Provenance in Marketing course cohorts and the anticipated federal pandemic relief package containing direct support for small and midsized food processors. While market conditions show Class III futures climbing out of February's slump, small creameries remain pressed on direct sales and administrative readiness. We need to align our member guidance so that we do not overwhelm small producers with premature relief applications while successfully capturing course engagement.

## Course cohort pacing and direct sales demand

The Storytelling & Provenance in Marketing course opened its newest sessions this week, specifically the Dairy cohort starting 9 March and the Brook cohort starting 11 March. Overall association activity for the week closed with 1 course enrollment taken and 2 course completions, alongside 8 membership purchases yielding a gross total of 1421 USD. Total confirmed orders reached 11 with 2110 USD gross, and cash captured stood at 2575 USD. Total members at week end reached 444. These figures reflect a stabilizing membership base where small-scale direct-to-consumer adoption continues to drive interest in marketing and provenance copy, mirroring the struggles members face when moving away from traditional counter conversations.

## Relief package preparation and avoiding premature guidance

The federal pandemic relief legislation moving through Washington this week includes specific allocations for small food processors, direct producers, and commodity purchases. However, administering agencies have not yet published rules, application forms, or eligibility criteria. Our public guidance must avoid guessing at figures or deadlines. Members who spend March chasing non-existent portals lose valuable operating time. Instead, our immediate priority is advising members to assemble basic administrative records—EIN numbers, prior relief documentation, and current profit-and-loss statements—so they can turn applications around within 48 hours of portal opening.

## Membership inquiries and committee support

The Membership & Outreach Committee has experienced an uptick in inquiries regarding how last year's relief programs interacted with individual business operations. Staff should route complex questions directly to committee members rather than attempting to provide financial counseling. At the same time, Education & Credentialing should ensure course instructors emphasize the practical worksheet nature of the marketing course rather than abstract branding theory, helping members revise their online product descriptions and FAQ sections effectively.

## What we need

- Communications: maintain strict messaging boundaries on federal relief, emphasizing paperwork preparation over speculative application timelines.
- Education & Credentialing: monitor cohort participation for the Dairy and Brook groups and feed practical member writing examples back into general guidance.
- Advocacy & Standards: prepare briefing materials for committee members handling inbound questions on small-producer relief eligibility.
