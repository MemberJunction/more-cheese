**Subject:** Staff capacity constraints and direct shipment advocacy inquiries

This week's public blog post on interstate shipment rules has generated a sharp spike in incoming inquiries from small farmstead producers asking whether they are legally allowed to ship aged raw-milk cheese across state lines. Because membership operations and advocacy share a narrow band of staff, the volume of questions is currently outstripping our capacity to answer them individually. We need an immediate agreement on how we triage these submissions before the comment period backlog spills over into our spring workshop preparation.

## Current inquiry volume and the capacity bottleneck

For the week of Monday 2021-03-22, we recorded eight total membership purchases grossing 1399 USD, alongside 14 confirmed orders totaling 2859 USD and 2772 USD in cash captured. While our financial metrics and retention figures remain stable—supported by 448 current members at week end—our operational bandwidth is stretched. The advocacy team is currently tracking a total of 100 recorded advocacy actions for the year across topics like raw-milk aging rules and FSMA small-producer exemptions, but we do not have dedicated personnel assigned solely to sorting raw-milk shipment inquiries from direct-to-consumer retailers.

Producers writing in after reading the recent blog post expect detailed legal breakdowns of state-level shipping restrictions. Because we cannot provide legal advice, every response requires manual review by advocacy staff to ensure we point members toward official public guidance without overstepping association boundaries. Right now, messages are sitting in the shared queue for up to 48 hours.

## Competing spring deadlines

This capacity strain is colliding directly with upcoming event milestones. We have registrations coming in for our upcoming events—specifically 4 registrations for EVT-2021-W1 Workshop: Stone Alpine Styles in Reggio Emilia and 2 registrations for the ICF Annual Conference 2021. Furthermore, we are preparing for the upcoming virtual webinar EVT-2021-WEB6 Webinar: Farmers' Market Sales 2021 on 21 April.

When communications, advocacy, and membership operations all rely on the same small pool of rotating staff to manage event registrations, workshop logistics, and advocacy comment gathering, something has to give. Last week's inquiries were manageable, but the combination of the tariff pause fallout and the direct shipment post has doubled our daily email intake.

## What we need

- Communications: pause any active social promotion of the shipment rule post until Friday afternoon so we can clear the current ticket backlog.
- Advocacy & Standards: draft a standard templated response for interstate shipment queries that directs producers to submit their experience logs directly through the member portal rather than via personal email.
- Events Team: take over the processing of incoming workshop registrations for the Reggio Emilia alpine style workshop so membership operations can dedicate full time to sorting the advocacy queue.
- Office of the Chief Executive: note that spring project deadlines may slip by two business days if inquiry volume continues at its present rate.
