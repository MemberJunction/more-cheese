## Burlington operations and format adjustments

Moving away from the four concurrent tracks used in 2020 directly addressed member feedback regarding fatigue and fragmented attention. Restricting the agenda to two tracks eliminated the split audience that hampered last year's engagement. Similarly, shortening the schedule to five hours across two blocks with a longer midday gap prevented the exhaustion reported during the nine-hour single-day format of the previous year.

The structured activity stream, incorporating a poll in every session and a five-minute open floor after each block rather than a single session at day's end, captured member questions far more effectively than the text boxes of 2020. However, the lack of an in-person trade floor or physical gathering space remains a recognized limitation. The closing hour open office hours hosted by the Membership & Outreach Committee provided a dedicated point for members to address renewal queries and chapter ideas directly with staff.
