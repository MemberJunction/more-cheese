As we move into the second week of January, planning is underway for our upcoming virtual event, Webinar: Cheese & Cider Pairing 2021 (EVT-2021-WEB9), scheduled for 9 February 2021. Because this is a free webinar, our data reflects zero paid registrations at this stage, and we will not quote an attendance figure. However, operating a virtual session of this scale requires aligning our venue setup, streaming AV, speaker logistics, and digital participant materials well ahead of broadcast day.

## Venue, streaming AV, and participant materials

Since this event is entirely virtual, our physical venue footprint is limited to the association studio room in Chicago. The streaming AV configuration requires a stable fiber connection, dual-camera switching, and dedicated audio mixing for both the host and the invited guest speaker. We have confirmed the technical run-through for 2 February.

Digital participant materials are currently being assembled by the Education & Credentialing team. Unlike in-person workshops, our virtual handouts must be formatted for immediate download prior to the stream. The tasting list—featuring specific cider styles and matching cheese categories—needs to be finalized and uploaded to the platform portal by 1 February so attendees have time to source their pairings locally.

## Catering and speaker arrangements

While attendees participate from home, we are arranging local artisan cider and cheese sample boxes for the presenter and the guest speaker to use live on screen. The delivery logistics are coordinated with a regional distributor in the upper Midwest. The guest speaker has confirmed availability for both the technical check and the live broadcast.

Underneath these logistical details, our committee calls this week have echoed the broader market pressures covered in the public blog posts regarding rising Class III and Class IV milk prices. Several small creamery members on recent committee check-ins noted that input costs are squeezing their margins while their direct-to-consumer and retail channels are shifting. While a free webinar does not directly solve a milk pricing problem, ensuring our educational programming addresses real operational constraints remains critical as members evaluate their spring contracts.

The Pricing & Margin for Specialty Cheese course is currently running a new virtual cohort this month, and staff time is split between supporting enrolled learners and setting up the February cider webinar. We need clear ownership of the slide deck review to avoid last-minute bottlenecks.
