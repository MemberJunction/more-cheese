Our recent member spot checks reveal a recurring structural risk: small-creamery operators who spend months heads-down on cold-chain logistics and direct shipping often let their federation touchpoints lapse simply because autopay runs silently and portal logins become unfamiliar. While automated renewal notices are easily skimmed, personalized check-in calls from volunteers have proven effective at rescuing accounts before they drift away. We need to decide whether to expand these targeted outreach calls ahead of the Q4 renewal cycle.
