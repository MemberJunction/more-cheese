> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT** — International Cheese Federation (ICF), a fictitious trade association created for the MemberJunction More Cheese demonstration environment. All names, figures, events, and decisions are invented.

**To:** Communications; Membership Operations; Office of the Chief Executive
**From:** Events Team
**Date:** 27 May 2021
**Subject:** Virtual conference platform readiness and feedback from the 2021 format

Registration for the ICF Annual Conference opened this week, bringing the second consecutive year of an online format built for members whose travel plans still change fast. The public blog post makes the case for why we are staying virtual, but our team has to manage the technical and logistical reality behind that decision. Last year taught us that content alone does not carry a virtual event if the platform interface causes friction in the first ten minutes. This memo reviews what we are changing based on member feedback, where the registration numbers stand so far, and what operational bottlenecks remain before July.

## What members told us and what we changed

The primary complaint from last year was not the quality of the sessions—which remain our most-watched resource library item months later—but the lack of informal hallway conversations. We cannot fully replicate a hotel lobby on a laptop screen, but we have adjusted the breakout room design. This year, rooms are capped at fifteen people and organized strictly by production type rather than by a generic track. A washed-rind producer from a coastal creamery will end up in the same room as one from three states inland. That peer exchange has historically generated more actionable troubleshooting than any panel.

We are also running the trade-show floor as scheduled video tables instead of a sprawling virtual hall. Regional producers, distributors, and suppliers will host structured 30-minute windows where attendees can drop in, talk, and move on. It is smaller, but members actually finish it.

## Registration pace and preliminary numbers

Registration opened on Monday, and the early registration pricing runs for two weeks. As of today, we have taken 10 registrations for EVT-2021-CONF, alongside 4 individual membership purchases and 6 course enrollments across the wider operation. Total confirmed orders for the association stand at 15, generating 4,642 gross, with 5,255 in cash captured so far this week. These figures are preliminary and will change as the early registration window progresses. For returning attendees from last year, discount codes have been automatically applied to accounts, but the membership desk should expect a steady volume of login inquiries.

## Platform onboarding and support load

Last year's help desk received over a hundred tickets in the first hour solely due to login trouble. To prevent a repeat, Communications published a short walkthrough video on the registration page this week. We are requiring staff in member services to direct every incoming registrant to watch that video before logging into the live sessions. If members skip the walkthrough, our support queue will bottleneck before the opening remarks.

## What we need

- Communications: verify that the walkthrough video link on the registration page is active and mobile-responsive by Friday.
- Membership Operations: monitor early registration confirmation emails to ensure returning attendees see their applied discount codes without manual intervention.
- Everyone: treat all registration and cash figures in this memo as preliminary until the week closes on Sunday.
