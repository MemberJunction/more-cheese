> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT** — International Cheese Federation (ICF), a fictitious trade association created for the MemberJunction More Cheese demonstration environment. All names, figures, events, and decisions are invented.

**To:** Events Team; Education & Credentialing; Office of the Chief Executive
**From:** Membership Operations
**Date:** 29 June 2021
**Subject:** Workload rebalancing for the July conference stretch

The week of 28 June 2021 brings a sharp rise in administrative friction across multiple functional areas. With the ICF Annual Conference 2021 registration intake standing at 15 confirmed registrations so far this week and the Workshop: Lark Defect Diagnosis drawing 10 registrations ahead of its 6 July run in Brooklyn, our small operational footprint is stretched. This memo addresses staff capacity during the busy stretch leading into July and August events, the deadlines that must move, and the work that will be temporarily dropped to keep core systems stable.

## Current volume and pinch points

Operational records show that membership purchases continue at a steady pace—4 total membership purchases this week, including 3 Individual and 1 SmallBusiness tier purchase, generating a gross of $809. Cash captured across all lines reached $9,090 with 31 confirmed orders and zero cancellations. However, this financial stability masks a severe internal bottleneck. The Events Team and Education & Credentialing share identical personnel overlaps. With course enrollments at 18 taken this week and the Counter Culture: Retailing (Hollow cohort) starting on 14 July, staff cannot simultaneously manage conference badge audits, workshop logistics in Brooklyn, and routine course registration reminders without dropping critical tasks.

## Moving the deadline

We cannot maintain three concurrent multi-channel reminders while handling venue intake for the Brooklyn workshop. The deadline for standard conference attendee materials distribution must move by five business days. Pushing this date back allows the Events Team to absorb the 29 total registrations (1 registered, 1 refunded, 25 attended, 4 no-shows in historical tallies) for the Lark Defect Diagnosis workshop without compromising data accuracy. Membership Operations will absorb the routine payment reconciliation backlog so that Events can focus entirely on venue coordination.

## Dropped work

To preserve capacity, we are formally pausing non-essential data hygiene cleanups and historical duplicate record reviews until 1 August. Specifically, the manual verification of stale member contact fields and secondary list-building for free webinars will not be performed this month. Free webinars carry no registrations in our data and generate no revenue, yet they consume disproportionate inquiry management time. Pausing webinar-specific helpdesk routing frees approximately six staff-hours weekly, which will be redirected straight to conference registration support.

## What we need

- Events Team: confirm receipt of the adjusted deadline for conference materials distribution and notify external venue vendors by Thursday.
- Education & Credentialing: pause all automated reminder sequences for upcoming free webinars until 1 August as agreed.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve the temporary suspension of secondary list-building tasks so staff can maintain focus on core membership renewals and workshop logistics.
