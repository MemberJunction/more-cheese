**Subject:** Member record integrity, stale employers, and database discrepancies

Recent member outreach and bounce-backs this spring have exposed persistent data-quality vulnerabilities in our member records. While total membership sits at 391 at week end, our internal database contains several unverified profiles, duplicate pairs, and stale employer attributions that complicate direct member communications. As production shifts and farmstead operations restructure under the current pandemic shock, these discrepancies risk misdirecting advocacy mailings and grant announcements.

## The nature of the record gaps

Our database review highlights three recurring categories of record errors that require immediate remediation before our next membership billing cycle:

- **Duplicate identities and merged records:** We have verified at least one duplicate pair involving Kate O'Leary and Kathy O'Leary, whose conference registrations and certification notes have occasionally merged under separate member IDs. Treating these as distinct individuals skews our participation metrics and creates confusion in credential tracking.
- **Stale employer and contact attributions:** Several professional members, notably Aisha Bell, retain outdated creamery affiliations in their primary profiles rather than current placements such as Fernholt Creamery. When members transition between farmstead sites during seasonal shifts, our database fails to capture the update without manual intervention.
- **Bounced advocacy and newsletter addresses:** Recent email campaigns targeting raw-milk aging rules and labeling standards returned a noticeable cluster of undelivered notices due to unrefreshed domain entries and inactive mailboxes, particularly among small producers whose business models shifted rapidly this spring.

## Why this matters now

During stable operating periods, minor database discrepancies are inconvenient but manageable. Under current economic conditions, however, members rely on accurate credential listings and prompt advocacy updates. If an active member's payment status does not match their recorded tier, or if their designated shipping and contact details point to a closed retail counter, automated membership notices fail to reach the correct decision-maker.

Furthermore, our records show 4 membership purchases so far this week, including 3 Individual tier purchases and 1 Corporate tier purchase, with total membership purchases reaching 4 for the week. Ensuring these new accounts enter a clean database is essential for maintaining retention past the first renewal cycle.

## Capacity and remediation steps

Membership Operations is currently processing inquiries and managing the spring membership renewal window with limited staff time. We cannot audit all 391 member records manually while simultaneously supporting the events and education teams. We need a systematic deduplication protocol that flags mismatched payment statuses and unconfirmed employer entries before the next board reporting cycle.

## What we need

- Communications: cross-reference any member spotlight subjects or quoted practitioners against our master organization table before publishing quotes, ensuring outdated employers are not repeated.
- Advocacy & Standards: verify mailing lists against our bounced-address log before distributing policy comment updates.
- Office of the Chief Executive: authorize a dedicated data-cleanup sprint next month to reconcile duplicate profiles and resolve stale employer tags.
