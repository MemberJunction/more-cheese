This memorandum provides an internal reconciliation of orders against cash captured for the week ending 19 January 2020, alongside a detailed review of an ongoing dispute regarding a corporate membership invoice. As dairy market softness persists and administrative shifts follow our recent webinars on retail strategies and whey management, friction around billing has risen. ## Reconciliation of Orders Against Cash

For the week of 13 January 2020 to 19 January 2020, our order records show 9 confirmed orders generating a gross value of 2334 USD, with 0 cancellations recorded. Total cash captured during this same period stands at 1516 USD across 8 total membership purchases, which include 6 first-time members and 2 repeat renewals, split between 1 Corporate tier purchase and 7 Individual tier purchases, totaling 1921 USD in membership-specific gross. The variance between total confirmed order gross and cash captured reflects standard processing lags and timing differences between invoice issuance and payment settlement. However, these figures must be audited weekly to prevent discrepancies from compounding.

## The Corporate Invoice Dispute

We are currently handling a formal dispute from a corporate member regarding a multi-tier invoice issued at the turn of the year. The member claims that the tier assignment and associated dues do not reflect their current operational footprint, arguing that market contractions in the dairy sector warrant a temporary adjustment or reclassification. While our published list prices—such as 1000 USD for Corporate membership and 175 USD for Individual membership—remain standard across our catalog, this dispute highlights a broader vulnerability. Members are scrutinizing every line item as cheese prices remain soft and export rules shift following this week's international trade agreements. Our current invoicing system lacks a clear grace-period protocol for members contesting corporate dues during economic downturns, forcing ad-hoc resolutions that consume valuable staff hours.

## Operational Strain and Member Communications

Administrative capacity is currently strained. The team handling membership operations is also supporting follow-ups from the Counter Culture 2020 and Whey Management 2020 webinars, as well as inquiries regarding the raw-milk aging rules campaign. When billing disputes drag on without a definitive policy framework, members experience frustration that spills over into their engagement with our educational and advocacy programs. We need a standardized workflow for invoice disputes that separates routine collection from formal tier-adjustment requests.

## What we need

- Office of the Chief Executive: Approve a formal policy framework for handling corporate membership invoice disputes by the end of the month.
- Communications: Ensure that member-facing correspondence regarding billing inquiries directs queries exclusively to the membership operations mailbox rather than individual functional channels.
- Membership Operations: Complete a full line-by-line reconciliation of all outstanding January invoices before the monthly close.
