**Subject:** January pricing shifts and member record clean-up

As we enter January, our operational focus must divide between the softer market conditions affecting our sector and the persistent data-quality issues in our member records. The recent public commentary regarding Class III futures and barrel-versus-block cheddar spreads makes it vital that our internal database reflects accurate member employment and active payment statuses before we issue renewal notices or process corporate tier updates. We currently stand at 362 current members at week end, with 4 membership purchases totaling $596 gross for the week of Monday 2020-01-06, all under the Individual tier. However, our records continue to harbor administrative friction that compromises our reporting.

## Data quality and record duplication

We have identified multiple record anomalies that require immediate remediation. Specifically, we are tracking a persistent duplicate pair involving duplicate person profiles with mismatched email domains, as well as several instances where corporate members retain stale employer designations from prior acquisitions. Furthermore, we are reviewing a stagnant member whose status does not match their payment ledger: their profile indicates an active membership period, yet their last payment record shows an unapplied credit and a bounced renewal notice from the late fourth quarter. Allowing these records to persist will distort our first-time member counts and renewal rates as we close out the first month of 2020.

## Market softness and member inquiries

The softening cheese and butter markets noted in our public communications are already generating inbound member inquiries regarding dues tier adjustments. Several small producers are asking whether we offer hardship categories or deferred billing schedules. While our standard tiers remain Individual ($175 list price), SmallBusiness ($400), Corporate ($1,000), and Enthusiast ($150), our current database hygiene issues make it difficult to evaluate which inquiring members are eligible for renewal grace periods versus those who have lapsed outright. We must resolve these profile discrepancies before responding to individual restructuring requests.

## Committee alignment and advocacy tracking

Our committee schedule is active this week, with the Awards & Competition Committee and Events Committee having completed their Q1 meetings on 6 January, followed by the Membership & Outreach Committee on 9 January. These bodies rely on clean membership extracts to plan regional workshops and competition entry rosters. When member records carry outdated employer data or duplicate IDs, committee mailings fail to reach the correct desks, and advocacy actions—such as the 3 raw-milk aging rule petition signatures and the 1 interstate cheese shipment coalition meeting recorded so far this week—risk being attributed to incorrect entities.

## What we need

- Communications: do not cite preliminary membership totals or tier splits in upcoming drafts until Membership Operations completes the duplicate record merge.
- Advocacy & Standards: cross-reference all incoming petition signatures and coalition participant lists against our validated organizational ID table to prevent duplicate crediting.
- Office of the Chief Executive: authorize a temporary freeze on automated renewal notices for accounts flagged with payment-status mismatches until the database audit is finalized by the end of next week.
