> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT** — International Cheese Federation (ICF), a fictitious trade association created for the MemberJunction More Cheese demonstration environment. All names, figures, events, and decisions are invented.

**To:** Office of the Chief Executive; Membership & Outreach Committee
**From:** Membership Operations
**Date:** 2 July 2020
**Subject:** Membership operations review and renewal patterns in early summer

This memo reviews our membership and cash-flow position as we enter July 2020. Total confirmed membership at the close of last year stood at 390, and our count as of the end of this week sits at 405. While the overall roster shows a slight upward trend despite the pandemic shock, the mechanics of how members are paying and renewing have shifted significantly. The current week shows zero membership purchases, matching an environment where creamery members and retail shops are carefully managing their cash rather than committing to annual renewals ahead of time.

## Where the numbers stand

For the week ending 5 July 2020, confirmed orders totaled 23 with a gross value of 5412 USD, alongside 3 cancellations. Total cash captured over the same period reached 7296 USD. Event registrations taken during the week include 13 for the ICF Annual Conference 2020 and 6 for the workshop on Harvest Alpine Styles. However, membership acquisitions remain at zero for the week, continuing a pattern where members lean heavily on grace windows and informal extensions rather than paying upfront annual dues.

This behavior is tied directly to the wider economic turbulence covered in recent industry reports. Class III milk prices have swung sharply by the week, leaving cheese plants and small-scale processors unsure of their next invoice. When wholesale block prices and milk input costs jump on short notice, cash flow freezes. Members who would normally renew their corporate or small business memberships in June are instead asking whether dues can be split or deferred.

## The retail and wholesale split

Retail grocery demand has remained steady, driven by home cooking and bulk food-box purchase programs. Members supplying into federal or regional distribution channels report reliable, predictable orders. Conversely, members tied to foodservice and restaurant accounts are experiencing severe whiplash as local jurisdictions pause or roll back reopening steps. That operational instability directly impacts their willingness to engage with association programs that require upfront fees.

Our Membership & Outreach Committee has been fielding questions all month regarding relief programs and grace periods. We have already published guidance on navigating these financial strains, but members need more than links; they need operational flexibility.

## What we need

- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a temporary extension of the membership renewal grace window from 60 days to 90 days for small business and individual tiers.
- Membership & Outreach Committee: prioritize outreach to members whose annual dues fall due this month, focusing on payment plan options rather than automated lapse notices.
- Communications: ensure future member briefings emphasize that relief program guidance remains accessible through the committee.
