**Subject:** Farmers'-market reopening guidance review and member reports

As local jurisdictions issue reopening rules for outdoor markets, our advocacy team has been collecting member reports on where guidance conflicts or creates outsized compliance burdens for small farmstead operations. The spotlight post scheduled for this week details one member's experience with ten-foot booth spacing, mandatory mask rules, and strict sampling bans, but our inbound log shows that individual counties are applying very different administrative standards. This memo summarizes what we are hearing from members, how it connects to our existing small-producer exemption priorities, and what we need from communications before the next advocacy summary goes out.

## What members are reporting

The patchwork of local rules is the primary frustration. A cheese producer selling at three different markets across two counties is currently required to maintain three separate compliance logs for sanitizing point-of-sale terminals, wearing gloves during hand-offs, and managing customer queuing. While our members generally understand public-health precautions like distancing and enhanced sanitation, the paperwork requirement is frequently scaled for large retail distribution rather than a two-person farmstead stand.

We cannot cite vague frustrations in our engagement with state regulators. We need concrete examples of where record-keeping rules or spacing requirements assume a retail footprint that small producers do not have. The two-person creamery model is being treated as identical to twelve-person regional facilities in several counties, and that is where we are directing our pushback.

## Connection to public communications

The communications team is preparing material highlighting how members are navigating the return to physical tables. We need to ensure that our advocacy stance on small-producer exemptions remains distinct from the practical tips shared in member spotlights. We are not objecting to public-health safeguards; we are objecting to disproportionate paperwork and conflicting county-line mandates that prevent small vendors from operating efficiently.

## Capacity and next steps

The advocacy log currently holds records across multiple topics, including labeling standards and interstate shipment rules, but farmers'-market reopening guidance has generated the sharpest rise in inquiries this month. We must ensure our guidance to members remains practical while we continue drafting comment letters for state and federal agencies.
