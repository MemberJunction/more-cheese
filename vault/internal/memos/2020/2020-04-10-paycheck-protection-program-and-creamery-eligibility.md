Small creameries and cheese shops are attempting to determine if they qualify and what specific costs are covered. ## Eligibility and payroll friction

Two primary friction points emerged across the advocacy inbox and committee discussions this week. First, agricultural producers and seasonal creameries with family labor or variable staffing find that standard payroll documentation models do not fit their structures. Several members reported that their local lenders are demanding different documentation than lenders in neighboring districts are requesting from other members. Second, the treatment of owner draws remains ambiguous for sole proprietors and small partnership creameries, leading to inconsistent guidance from participating lenders.

Furthermore, several members discovered that their primary commercial banks are not participating lenders in the program, forcing them to seek out alternative financial institutions before they can even submit an application. This administrative hurdle adds delay during a period when cash-flow pressures are acute, particularly for businesses experiencing wholesale and foodservice cancellations.
