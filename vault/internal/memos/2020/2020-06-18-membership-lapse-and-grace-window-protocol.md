**Subject:** Membership lapse rates and grace-window management during the pandemic shock

As we process the ongoing economic contraction following the spring collapse, membership renewal patterns have fundamentally altered. Current membership sits at 402 members at the end of the week so far, with only 1 membership purchase recorded this week (an Individual tier purchase yielding 149 USD). While cash captured across all transactions for the week stands at 7116 USD from 18 confirmed orders, our inbound payment failures and grace-window requests have doubled since April. Smaller producers and artisan makers whose retail and wholesale channels dried up in March are hitting the end of their standard renewal cycles and are unable to pay standard dues without risking payroll.

This memo outlines our operational exposure, reviews the limits of our current grace-window policy, and recommends a structured extension protocol that prevents permanent attrition without compromising association revenue integrity.

## The current operational reality

Our historical renewal cycle assumes predictable cash flow and steady operating margins. The current fiscal reality is entirely different. Members are contacting Membership Operations asking for informal grace periods ranging from thirty to ninety days. Under existing operating rules, we offer a strict thirty-day administrative grace window before shifting an account to lapsed status. Beyond that window, access to the member portal, technical resources, and regulatory updates is suspended.

Applying this strict rule rigidly right now is counterproductive. Several long-term members who are actively participating in our food-safety advocacy campaigns—particularly those wrestling with federal compliance dates and small-producer exemptions—suffer from cash flow pinches. Losing them to an unyielding automated lapse sequence damages our advocacy coalition weight at the exact moment we need unified member voices in front of regulators.

## Policy adjustments and grace-window protocol

We propose an intentional, time-bound extension of the grace window for SmallBusiness and Individual tier accounts experiencing documented pandemic-related revenue disruption. Rather than an open-ended pause, we should implement a formal sixty-day extension request form on the member portal. This aligns with our recent outreach regarding federal compliance deadlines and ensures that members do not feel alienated while trying to keep their creameries afloat.

However, we must maintain strict boundaries. Corporate and larger SmallBusiness accounts with robust distribution channels do not qualify for hardship extensions. Furthermore, we must not confuse an active grace period with an unrecorded renewal. Every extension must be logged internally with a scheduled follow-up date.

## What we need

- Communications: review our proposed grace-window messaging to ensure it coordinates with portal notices without conflicting with public advocacy posts.
- Advocacy & Standards: confirm whether any members currently requesting payment grace windows are active participants in our small-producer exemption petitions, so we can prioritize manual outreach.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve the temporary sixty-day extension protocol for hardship accounts through the end of Q3 2020.
