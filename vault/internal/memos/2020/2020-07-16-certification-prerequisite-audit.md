As the federation shifts its annual conference online for the 21st, our certification administration requires an immediate operational review. The pandemic shock has accelerated our online coursework, but credentialing prerequisites have created friction points for candidates attempting to complete their paths remotely. This memo reviews our current enrollment backlog, prerequisite verifications, and upcoming exam sitting readiness.

## Prerequisite verification bottlenecks

Our year-to-date data shows zero certification enrollments and no certifications awarded so far this week, mirroring a broader slowdown in formal credential completion. While course completions stand at six for the week, candidates attempting to bridge foundational courses into advanced certification paths are routinely stalled by manual prerequisite checks. The current workflow requires staff to cross-reference course completion records manually against certification eligibility rules, resulting in processing delays that frustrate candidates who are otherwise ready to sit for exams.

Candidates applying for specialised credentials, such as the Sensory Evaluation Certificate or the Advanced Affinage Certificate, frequently discover that prerequisite course titles from earlier cohorts lack automated synchronisation with the certification database. This administrative friction risks dampening the 85% surge in online coursework that we are otherwise capturing across our virtual offerings. If we cannot verify prerequisites before the exam window opens, candidates drop out of the pipeline entirely.

## Exam sitting administration for virtual cohorts

Administering proctored exams remotely without an in-person conference floor presents distinct logistical hurdles. Candidates scheduled for upcoming testing windows require secure digital proctoring links, verifiable identity checks, and clear guidance on material submissions. Unlike in previous years where exam sittings were anchored to physical conference schedules, our virtual pivot requires a streamlined digital workflow that protects exam integrity without imposing impossible technical burdens on remote candidates.

Furthermore, the Events Committee is finalising the virtual conference platform for the 21st, and credentialing information must be clearly linked within the attendee portal. Candidates expect seamless navigation between course completion records and exam registration pages. Any misalignment between the education catalog and active certification requirements damages our credibility during a delicate operating window.

## Staffing capacity and cross-departmental coordination

Education and credentialing oversight currently relies on a minimal team balancing course management, cohort scheduling, and exam logistics. With conference preparations consuming staff bandwidth across the federation, prerequisite audits are falling behind schedule. We need a defined protocol between Membership Operations and Education to reconcile candidate records before exam dates are published.

- Membership Operations: provide an updated roster of active members who have completed foundational course prerequisites by 18 July.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a temporary waiver protocol for candidates whose manual prerequisite verifications are delayed by system lag.
