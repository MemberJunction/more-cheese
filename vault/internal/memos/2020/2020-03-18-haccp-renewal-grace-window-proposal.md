**Subject:** HACCP renewal grace window — what we are proposing and what stays fixed

This is a draft position, not yet announced. If the Office of the Chief Executive signs off today, Communications can run it in Friday's public post (20 March). Three members have written to us this week with the same problem: the Food Safety & HACCP Certificate renewal calls for an on-site verification visit, and they cannot book one. We propose a 90-day extension past the listed expiration, with the verification still owed.

## What we are proposing

Renewals falling due between now and the end of May get their current credential extended by 90 days past the listed expiration. Two conditions apply. The renewal must already be in good standing, and the member must be able to point to a documented site restriction or an auditor who cannot travel. We would not ask for more paperwork than a short note.

The extended member is not shown as lapsed in the directory. They do not re-sit coursework to use the extension. They do owe the on-site verification once one can reasonably be scheduled.

## What stays fixed

- **The standard.** This is a grace window on scheduling, not a waiver. Verification is still required.
- **New applicants.** Same coursework and the same eventual on-site step. The extension is for renewals only.
- **The other six certifications.** Competition Judge Accreditation, Certified Cheesemonger and the rest carry no site-visit requirement. Their renewal timelines do not change.

## Where I am less sure

I do not yet have a count of renewals falling due before the end of May. Membership Operations holds the renewal dates, and I would rather not guess a number in anything that leaves this building. Until we have that count, the public post should describe the window and give no estimate of how many members it covers.

I also think the public wording should say "documented" and keep it there. If we write "any member who asks," we lose the ability to tell a real site restriction from a convenience request, and I would rather defend a narrow rule than a wide one.

Separately, Allergen Management (Granite cohort) starts 11 May. If members use a slow spring to get ahead on coursework, that is the nearest open start. I would not promise seats beyond what the course roster shows.

## What we need

- **Office of the Chief Executive:** a yes or no on the 90-day window by end of day Thursday 19 March.
- **Membership Operations:** a list of HACCP renewals due through 31 May, marked preliminary, by Monday 23 March.
- **Communications:** hold the public wording to "documented site restriction or auditor unable to travel," and tell us before Friday if the post says anything about numbers.
- **All:** route member questions on which credential is affected to the credentialing desk, not to individual replies.
