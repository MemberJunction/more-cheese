## Aligning association programming with cost realities

The blog post on December 12 correctly noted that a tighter margin environment does not always announce itself with a dramatic price drop; instead, invisible inefficiencies in packaging, portioning, and rework eat away at the invoice before it reaches the producer's bottom line. When our members return from the holiday rush in early January, their cutting stations will have papered over structural waste through sheer staffing and overtime. That makes the January 5 webinar on Cut & Wrap Efficiency not just a routine operational review, but a critical intervention for members who need to protect their margins before spring price lists are printed.

We must ensure that our outreach for this free virtual session explicitly connects station workflow to the cost of raw milk. If a member's cutting program suffers from high rework rates or unoptimized station layouts, the record milk prices of 2022 will transform into a severe margin pinch in the first quarter of 2023. We are coordinating with the Events Team to ensure registration links in the member portal are fully operational and that the session targets counter and production staff directly, rather than focusing solely on ownership.
