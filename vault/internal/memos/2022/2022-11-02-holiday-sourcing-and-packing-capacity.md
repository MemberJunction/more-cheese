## Sourcing and packaging strain

Domestic cheese exports have maintained a growth streak spanning seven months, pulling domestic supply away from local wholesale reserves and toward overseas buyers. Simultaneously, European wholesale energy costs have squeezed aging and drying operations abroad, resulting in a notably thinner import case. Members calling the Membership & Outreach Committee are reporting that traditional Q4 wheel allocations are either subject to hedging or missing entirely.

Simultaneously, first-time gift box packers are learning that cutting consistent wedges and selecting breathable wrap materials require entirely different operational disciplines than weekly market stands. Our Cut & Wrap Fundamentals course addresses this gap directly, but members scaling up programs this week are absorbing yield losses before learning which wrap preserves rind integrity during transit.
