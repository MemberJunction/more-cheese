> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT** — International Cheese Federation (ICF), a fictitious trade association created for the MemberJunction More Cheese demonstration environment. All names, figures, events, and decisions are invented.

**To:** Membership Operations; Events Team; Education & Credentialing
**From:** Communications
**Date:** 4 August 2022
**Subject:** Public post coordination and market data limits

This week's public blog posts have raised several operational questions about what our Association web properties can and cannot publish regarding current dairy price pullbacks and retail wedge pricing. The 1 August post ("Prices cooled off. Did your margin follow?") and the 3 August post ("The counter that started pricing by the wedge") touch on sensitive financial realities for our members. This memo establishes what data may be cited in upcoming commentary and where our internal records must remain distinct from public guidance.

## The wholesale pullback and export divergence

The wholesale milk and block cheese pricing pullback observed through July must be handled carefully. The public post correctly notes that wholesale milk and block cheddar prices eased after a record spring, and that whey has come off hard. However, it contrasts this with a still-expanding U.S. export market, particularly shipments to Mexico.

When members ask staff about these figures, we must be precise. We cannot invent or extrapolate wholesale figures beyond what is published in our verified research sources and weekly order data. The week ending 7 August shows confirmed orders of 8, gross orders of 957 USD, and cash captured of 1,146 USD, with 3 new individual membership purchases grossing 471 USD and 4 course completions. These operational figures are for internal tracking and preliminary association health checks; they must not be broadcast as broader macroeconomic market indexes.

## Wedge pricing versus wheel pricing

The 3 August post highlights a retail cheesemonger member who rebuilt her case pricing around the wedge instead of the wheel. While this is an effective member spotlight, staff must avoid turning anecdotal retail practices into Association pricing policy. The Membership & Outreach Committee maintains a referral list for members seeking peer comparisons on retail pricing, but staff are not permitted to recommend specific markup formulas or endorse individual distributor arrangements.

Furthermore, staff answering inquiries from members about cost-basis tracking should direct them to the appropriate committee resources rather than offering financial advice. Food safety, legal, and tax guidance remain strictly outside our operational scope.

## Certification and workshop messaging

The 5 August post on the Advanced Affinage Certificate has driven inquiries regarding prerequisites and cave management. The Education & Credentialing team must ensure that members inquiring about the Advanced Affinage Certificate are reminded of the CCP prerequisite before enrolling in preparatory sessions such as the upcoming Workshop: Birch Alpine Styles in Querétaro scheduled for 7 September. Note that free webinars carry zero paid registrations in our data; staff must never quote attendance or registration figures for free webinars.

## What we need

- Communications: Screen all upcoming blog drafts against verified order and course data before publication.
- Membership Operations: Flag any discrepancies between preliminary weekly order totals and monthly revenue closes before they are referenced in member communications.
- Education & Credentialing: Direct all Advanced Affinage inquiries through the proper portal gate and verify CCP eligibility prior to workshop sign-ups.
- Everyone: Adhere strictly to authorized data sources and avoid quoting unverified figures or providing financial advice.
