## Affinage overhead and pricing mechanics

Simultaneously, small farmstead producers operating converted root cellars and unheated outbuildings are reporting sharp increases in heating and propane bills during late winter cold snaps. The cave does not adjust its temperature requirements for market conditions; maintaining a stable 52 degrees requires burning fuel that follows the same upward curve as transport energy. This fixed-cost reality makes cost-plus pricing dangerous if producers price only against last month's invoices. The Pricing & Margin for Specialty Cheese course addresses this directly by teaching members how to build an active cost model and communicate price adjustments to wholesale and retail buyers four to six weeks in advance, preventing invoice surprises.
