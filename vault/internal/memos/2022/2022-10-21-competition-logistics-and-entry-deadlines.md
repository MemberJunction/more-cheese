The Awards & Competition Committee has met to review our annual competition pipeline and the logistics surrounding entry deadlines, shipping, judging sheets, and results reporting. With forty-one total entries recorded for the year across categories such as Alpine Styles, Blue Veined, Soft-Ripened, Fresh & Pasta Filata, Washed Rind, and Aged Cheddar & Territorials, we need a standardized procedure for how entries are received, how judges record feedback, and how results are distributed to producers like Fromagerie Saint-Rémille, Harrowfen Dairy Systems, Elderfen Farm, Kilnbrook Farm, Dapplewick Creamery, Lindenfen Center for Cheese Studies, Mistlebrook Dairy Institute, Kestrelmoor Creamery Cooperative, Plumgate Farm, Briarholt Cheese Co., and Pippinvale Farm.

## Entry deadlines and shipping logistics

Members frequently ask whether entry deadlines are flexible. Past cycles show that late shipments disrupt the staging room and complicate the stewards' work. We must enforce a firm closure date for submissions. Shipping logistics also require tighter coordination. Cheeses arriving without proper temperature monitoring or incomplete documentation cannot be held in our staging facility indefinitely. We need Communications to push clear shipping guidelines in the member newsletter two weeks prior to the intake window so that producers understand the penalties for late or unlabelled deliveries.

## Scoresheets and judge calibration

Our judges rely on structured scoresheets to evaluate rind development, texture, and flavor profile. However, feedback from past medalists indicates that numerical scores without qualitative commentary leave cheesemakers guessing how to improve. We propose a mandatory two-part scoresheet for the upcoming cycle, separating technical evaluation from aesthetic commentary. This will ensure that entrants receiving Gold, Silver, or Bronze medals—as well as those who fall short—understand the precise basis of the panel's decision.

## Results reporting and public transparency

When competition results are finalized, the timing between the closing dinner and the public announcement must be synchronized. Last year, winners were circulated informally before the official press release reached the broader membership. We must ensure that the Awards & Competition Committee signs off on the final medal list before Communications publishes the results page. This prevents discrepancies between internal records and public announcements.
