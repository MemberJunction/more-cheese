## The holiday promotional squeeze and export allocation

While the workshop filled its stations, members selling direct to retail or wholesale grocery accounts are flagging a real margin squeeze. Federal export data released this fall indicates another strong month for American cheese shipped abroad, extending a growth streak that has run more than a year. When export demand pulls hard on specific wheel inventories, wholesale allocations for domestic specialty accounts tighten right when grocery buyers want more product for holiday promotions.

Simultaneously, several members report holiday cheese promotions launching with steeper discounts than last year to move volume against broader grocery price inflation. A deep discount on a marquee holiday wheel can drive traffic, but only if the pricing economics were built into fall planning rather than panic-matched in November. Members reviewing their price sheets this week need to determine which items can absorb promotional discounts without going underwater.
