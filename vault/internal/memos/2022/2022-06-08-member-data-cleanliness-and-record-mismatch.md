**To:** Office of the Chief Executive; Communications
**From:** Membership Operations
**Date:** 8 June 2022
**Subject:** Member record cleanliness, duplicate entries, and payment status mismatches

Membership Operations has completed an internal review of member records following the recent surge in renewals and new sign-ups. We currently stand at 550 members at week end. While the aggregate numbers show steady growth—including 3 membership purchases this week, 2 of which are first-time members in the Individual tier and 1 in the SmallBusiness tier—our underlying records require immediate remediation. We have identified multiple data-quality issues, specifically concerning duplicate profile entries, mismatched membership statuses against payment logs, and stale employer affiliations that do not reflect current member activities.

## Duplicate profiles and status mismatches

The most pressing friction involves duplicate member profiles created during conference registration rushes and online renewals. We have tracked down overlapping entries where a single member holds two distinct identification numbers, leading to conflicting records on dues status. In at least two instances, the membership status recorded in the directory does not match the actual cash captured in our payment ledgers. One member who completed a SmallBusiness tier renewal shows as lapsed in the primary directory because the order confirmation number was decoupled from their legacy profile ID during the spring migration.

Furthermore, we continue to encounter stale employer names for members who changed creameries or transitioned to farmstead operations over the past eighteen months. Communications relying on these records risk sending member alerts or committee notices to defunct email addresses or incorrect organizational affiliations. This administrative friction makes it difficult to provide accurate membership reports to the board or answer basic queries without manual cross-referencing.

## Scope of the remediation

Fixing these records cannot wait for the annual close. When members call or write in to discuss contract decisions or feed cost pressures—topics that many producers are actively weighing this spring—they expect their profile to accurately reflect their standing. If a member's dues payment is marked unconfirmed despite a captured payment of 6143 in our cash ledger for the week, trust erodes quickly.

We have also noted that current membership figures must be handled carefully when referenced alongside public announcements. While our week confirmed 21 orders resulting in a gross of 6078, internal data discrepancies must be cleaned before we publish any detailed regional membership breakdowns.

## What we need

- Communications: Hold off on publishing any member-count breakouts or regional spotlights until Membership Operations clears the current duplicate queue.
- Office of the Chief Executive: Authorize a two-week administrative freeze on profile creation exceptions so we can merge duplicate pairs and verify all active SmallBusiness tier accounts against confirmed payments.
- Membership Operations: Deliver a finalized reconciliation spreadsheet by 13 June listing every mismatched payment status and merged duplicate record.
