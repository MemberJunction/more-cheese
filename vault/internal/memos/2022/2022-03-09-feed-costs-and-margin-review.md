The 7 March public blog post on milk prices and feed costs correctly identified that our members are seeing record per-hundredweight checks without feeling the expected financial relief. ## Margin pressures and member inquiries

Calls to Membership Operations this week reflect the themes of the blog post: small creameries running bulk tanks, pasteurizers, and cave dehumidifiers around the clock are watching utility bills climb alongside milk prices. Members asking whether to raise retail and wholesale prices now are being directed to review their actual feed cost per hundredweight and fixed energy costs rather than relying on stale winter figures. We must ensure our education team's Pricing & Margin for Specialty Cheese course is actively promoted to members who need a structured way to evaluate their make sheets.

With the federal Dairy Margin Coverage program enrollment window extended into late March, members are evaluating how to hedge feed cost volatility. We need to make sure our communications align with what the Food Safety Committee and advocacy volunteers are handling in their respective committees.
