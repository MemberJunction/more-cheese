As the Association moves three weeks past the Asheville conference and prepares for the upcoming September workshop cycle, our planning for the Birch cohort's Alpine Styles workshop in Querétaro requires immediate alignment. We currently have 19 registrations taken during this week alone for EVT-2022-W5 Workshop: Birch Alpine Styles, bringing total registrations to 123 against a registered baseline of 123 in the annual figures. This memo outlines the logistical pressures, the overlap with recent member communications regarding feed costs, and the specific decisions required to ensure the Querétaro session runs smoothly.

## Registration pace and venue capacity

The Birch cohort workshop on September 7 is an in-person, hands-on session structured around small-group makes and washed-curd techniques. Because the format relies entirely on bank-of-makes access and direct vat time, seats are strictly capped. The 19 registrations taken this week show that late summer is prompting a surge in sign-ups, but it also increases the administrative load on venue prep and materials handling in Querétaro.

We must confirm that the 123 registered attendees are accounted for in the room block, catering orders, and AV setup. More importantly, we need to ensure that the instructor has the correct breakdown of skill levels based on whether attendees hold a Cheese Foundations Certificate or equivalent hands-on experience, so tables can be grouped by technique rather than alphabetically.

## The feed-cost and travel-cost friction

Members are weighing the registration fee against broader financial pressures. Recent member communications have highlighted how farmstead producers are spending heavily on supplemental hay due to falling pasture growth, mirroring international drought impacts. While cheese prices at the case have remained firm, the cash flow squeeze of a high feed bill makes members look twice at travel and lodging expenses.

We cannot subsidize their travel, but we can ensure that the workshop delivers immediate, practical value that justifies the trip. That means the make schedule and the afternoon cave discussion must directly address the moisture, rind-wash schedules, and maturation questions that members are currently writing into the Membership & Outreach Committee about.

## AV, materials, and speaker readiness

The technical setup for the Alpine Styles workshop involves active brine stations, demonstration vats, and a lecture space that can handle a large group without losing audio clarity. The venue in Querétaro has confirmed the room block and basic catering, but we still need final sign-off on the printed make-sheet handouts and the bilingual translation support required for the host country venue.

If we do not lock down the material printing schedule by next Monday, we risk paying expedited courier fees for freight that is already sensitive to fuel surcharges.
