## Market signals and contract renegotiations

The federal forecast projects calm raw milk costs alongside tighter finished-product pricing margins at wholesale. For members negotiating annual supply contracts this autumn rather than rolling over previous terms, this environment requires a careful review of supplier list prices. While raw costs remain steady, slower growth in milk production per cow combined with modest herd expansion means regional buyers may begin competing more aggressively for available milk supply before winter sets in. Producers should be encouraged to reach out to their buyers early to determine whether regional terms have shifted, even if headline prices appear calm.

Retail and counter-staff members face a different dynamic. A softer finished-product forecast means wholesale margins have less room, and rising import volumes in the cheese and butter pipeline will increase shelf competition for domestic makers of imported-style wheels. Watching competitor pricing behind the counter is a more urgent priority this quarter than tracking input costs. We must ensure our membership guidance reflects this shift rather than dwelling solely on farm-gate economics.

## The 60-day rule and cave documentation

Our recent advocacy work around raw-milk interstate shipment brought fresh attention to the federal 60-day aging floor. A member testifying this week reminded the legislative panel that 60 days is a regulatory minimum rather than a target for biological safety. Her testimony emphasized that cave flora and microclimate variations require makers to establish and document house standards well past the federal floor, keeping dated batch records to satisfy state regulators and buyers alike.

This practical reality reinforces the need for our educational offerings. Members navigating interstate shipment rules need clear guidance on maintaining verifiable aging logs. We should ensure our course catalog properly supports makers who are formalizing their internal aging standards to protect against regulatory ambiguity.
