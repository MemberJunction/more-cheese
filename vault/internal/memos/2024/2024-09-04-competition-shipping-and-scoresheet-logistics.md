## Entry intake and category distribution

The 65 active competition entries are distributed across our standard categories: Soft-Ripened leads with 13 entries, followed by Alpine Styles (11), Aged Cheddar & Territorials (11), Washed Rind (11), Blue Veined (10), and Fresh & Pasta Filata (9). Several smaller farmstead members have contacted us regarding shipping windows and cold-storage compliance during transit. Because cheese judging demands absolute integrity in sample temperature upon arrival, we cannot grant grace periods for late shipments without compromising the credibility of the entire flight. The Events Team is enforcing the posted cutoff dates strictly.

## Judging panels and accreditation requirements

As noted in our recent federation publishing on sensory standards, Competition Judge Accreditation is the one credential that lapses from disuse. Panelists must hold the Sensory Evaluation Certificate and demonstrate active tasting experience. We have observed instances where accredited judges nearly lapsed due to insufficient panel hours over the past year.

To prevent last-minute disqualifications at the table, the Awards & Competition Committee must verify that every shadowing and scoring slot is filled by members with active credentials. We cannot rely on informal experience when evaluating complex entries like the Cindervale Reserve from Dapplewick Creamery or the Damsonwick Tomme from Mistlebrook Farm.

## Scoresheet formatting and member feedback

Entrants expect detailed feedback, particularly when competing in rigorous categories where previous medalists—such as Dubois Farmstead, Juniperfen Dairy Institute, and Dovetarn Cheese Co.—set a high benchmark. Our current scoresheet template captures defect identification and structural scores, but feedback turnaround times have historically slipped when administrative staff are pulled into event logistics.

We are committing to a strict two-week window for scoresheet dispatch following the final gavel. Communications must ensure that public-facing announcements regarding results do not outpace our ability to get physical or digital scoresheets into the hands of the entrants.
