## Market divergence and price sheet reality

The split between firmed wholesale cheese and butter prices and slower milk production forecasts is not an invitation for an across-the-board price hike. Commodity block and barrel prices move on a different track than differentiated specialty items. Creameries mixing both categories on a single price sheet risk pricing their specialty lines out of step with actual labor and aging costs. ## Aging rooms, sixty days, and the buffer trap

The sixty-day raw-milk aging requirement remains untouched, but our advocacy work with the Food Safety Committee shows that members pad their aging schedules far beyond sixty days out of caution, tying up cave space and carrying costs. The Food Safety & HACCP Certificate course covers exactly this recordkeeping detail, but members need advance notice of any traceability recordkeeping adjustments rather than retroactive surprises. We need to ensure that inquiries about aging schedules are met with practical guidance on batch documentation rather than generic assurances.
