**To:** Events Team; Education & Credentialing; Office of the Chief Executive
**From:** Membership Operations
**Date:** 13 June 2024
**Subject:** Staff capacity constraints during the summer direct sales and conference rush

This week's numbers reflect a busy stretch across multiple operational fronts. Confirmed orders reached 59 with a gross value of $19,373, while cash captured hit $21,905. Membership purchases stand at 16 (grossing $3,058), with 11 first-time joiners mostly in the Individual tier (13 purchases, plus 1 Enthusiast and 2 SmallBusiness). Simultaneously, event registrations taken for the ICF Annual Conference 2024 reached 35, along with 5 for Workshop: Hollow Food Safety, and course enrollments recorded 10 new sign-ups. While revenue and participation metrics are trending upward in line with the direct-to-consumer and market stand surge covered in public communications, our internal operating capacity is under severe strain. Shared staff are currently trying to handle conference logistics, direct sales inquiries, and self-paced course support concurrently, resulting in dropped follow-ups and missed administrative deadlines.

## The current operational bottleneck

The overlap between pre-conference processing for Des Moines and the influx of direct-to-market inquiries from members setting up farm stands has created a pinch point. The Education & Credentialing team is fielding questions from members adjusting their make-sheet documentation and looking at self-paced modules, while the Events Team is processing conference registrations that require manual verification. Because our staffing model relies on functional overlap without dedicated administrative bandwidth for seasonal spikes, routine member intake and renewal processing are absorbing delays. The 16 membership purchases this week were processed, but response times for member portal support have stretched past our internal 48-hour target.

## Reallocating responsibilities

To prevent further dropped tasks, we must temporarily shift responsibilities until the July conference window opens. Education & Credentialing will pause non-essential outreach for upcoming cohorts starting in July (such as Storytelling & Provenance in Marketing for the Willow cohort and Pasta Filata Techniques for the Hollow cohort) and redirect administrative hours toward order reconciliation and basic membership inquiries. The Events Team will absorb the remaining conference intake processing directly without asking Membership Operations for daily manual audits until Monday morning. This division ensures that cash-generating order processing does not stall while events staff finalize venue logistics.

## Margin and pricing inquiries

Public reporting and member calls indicate that milk prices and specialty cheese trade-ups are prompting members to re-evaluate their retail price lists. Members are asking for guidance on whether their cost-of-milk adjustments justify higher counter prices. Membership Operations and Advocacy & Standards are receiving these inquiries but lack the bandwidth to compile a formal advisory note this month. We must communicate clearly to inquirers that pricing decisions remain strictly at the creamery level while pointing them to existing self-paced margin resources in the portal rather than opening individual advisory tickets.

## What we need

- Events Team: take over complete ownership of EVT-2024-CONF registration reconciliation through the end of June without requesting intermediary audits.
- Education & Credentialing: pause outbound course marketing reminders for July cohorts and dedicate two staff days to back-office member data cleanup.
- Office of the Chief Executive: formally approve this temporary task reallocation and acknowledge that routine response times will remain extended until 1 July.
