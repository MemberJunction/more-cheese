## Current Certification Status and Bottlenecks

Data for the week ending 26 May shows zero new certification enrollments and zero certifications awarded, matching the broader association-wide trend where total active certification enrollments stand at 27 for the year. However, interest in the specific coursework required for judging—such as the Judging & Competition Preparation course—is spiking as members read the recent guidance on professional development.

The core friction is that members conflate holding the Sensory Evaluation Certificate with automatic Competition Judge Accreditation. As noted in association standards, accreditation requires the sensory certificate plus supervised panel experience over a two-year cycle. Telling members this after they have already booked travel to Des Moines creates avoidable friction. We need a standardized response across all inbound channels.
