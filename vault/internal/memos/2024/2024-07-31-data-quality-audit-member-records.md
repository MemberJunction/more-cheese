**Subject:** Data-quality audit of member records and employer discrepancies

This audit reviews ongoing data-quality issues within our member database, specifically focusing on duplicate profiles, stale employer affiliations, bounced mailing addresses, and payment-to-status mismatches. As our active membership count stands at 998 at the end of the week, maintaining clean member records is critical for our policy representation and advocacy efforts. If our database contains conflicting employer data or duplicate individual accounts, our committee liaisons cannot accurately track who represents which creamery during policy discussions.

## Duplicate records and name variations

Our most recurring data-quality challenge involves duplicate entries for the same individual under slight name variations. The most prominent example in our records is the duplicate pair involving Kate O'Leary and Kathy O'Leary, where two distinct profiles exist in the system despite representing the same individual and contact details. Similar minor discrepancies appear in several other records where maiden and married names have both been registered as active accounts without being merged. When these duplicates occur, membership renewal counts and first-time purchase tallies become skewed, leading to an overcount in our annual metrics.

## Stale employer affiliations and bounced addresses

Beyond duplicate names, we have identified a significant number of stale employer records where members have changed creameries but their billing profiles still point to their former employers. For instance, Aisha Bell's record continues to list an outdated employer rather than Fernholt Creamery, which creates confusion when regional reports are pulled for committee meetings. Furthermore, our recent email campaigns and print mailings to select institutional members have suffered from a high rate of bounced messages due to unupdated physical and digital addresses. Left uncorrected, these stale records disrupt our communications cadence and lead members to believe our outreach is out of touch with their current operations.

## Status mismatches against payment records

Another friction point is the divergence between membership status flags and actual payment transactions. A small subset of member accounts show active status in our directory despite their annual dues payment failing or remaining unpaid past the grace window. Conversely, members who paid their individual dues during the 5 membership purchases processed this week occasionally find their dashboard status lagging by several days. This discrepancy undermines confidence in our self-service portal and generates unnecessary support inquiries for our operations team.

## What we need

- Communications: do not pull direct contact lists or employment data for blog spotlights or press releases without clearing the record through Membership Operations first.
- Membership & Outreach: direct committee chairs to verify their roster affiliations against current employer data before the next quarterly meeting cycle.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a dedicated two-week data-cleaning sprint in August to merge duplicate profiles and resolve stale employer tags before the fall course cohorts begin.
