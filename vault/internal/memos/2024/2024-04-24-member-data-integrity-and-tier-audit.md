## The nature of the record defects

The most pressing issue involves duplicate profiles for individuals who renewed under slightly altered names or secondary email addresses, most notably involving recurring duplicates in our credentialed roster such as the Kate O'Leary and Kathy O'Leary entries. These separate records split a single member's course history and certification progress across two distinct identifiers, making it impossible to accurately track continuing education or renewal eligibility without manual intervention.

Compounding this is the issue of stale employer affiliations. For example, records for members associated with Fernholt Creamery still reflect pre-merger structures, preventing our advocacy and standards teams from accurately mapping small-producer concentrations during ongoing regulatory comment periods.

## Bounced communications and payment mismatches

We are also tracking a cluster of bounced administrative emails and mismatched payment tiers. Several accounts registered under SmallBusiness and Individual tiers show payments that do not reconcile with the active benefit package assigned to their profile. These anomalies typically arise when a member upgrades or downgrades mid-cycle without completing the administrative verification step, leaving their profile tagged at a higher tier while dues captured reflect a lower rate.

While our membership purchases for this week total 13 (comprising 6 first-time members across 3 SmallBusiness, 9 Individual, and 1 Enthusiast tier purchases totaling $2,773 in gross sales), we are spending disproportionate staff time reconciling these entries against our confirmed orders and payment logs.
