## The core tension in member feedback

We have heard from two distinct camps that both offer reasonable positions. Producers seeking relief from the sixty-day aging floor argue that certain styles cannot cross state lines legally despite rigorous testing and clean pathogen records. On the buyer and retail side, operators who manage cheese counters or wholesale distribution maintain that any relaxation of aging rules must be paired with explicit, written-in testing schedules to protect retail confidence. As noted by Tom Reyes, who has tracked these hearings across multiple legislative cycles, rules that pair looser shipment language with specific testing schedules survive legal challenges far better than blanket exemptions that leave safety questions to subsequent regulations.

Because our membership does not share a single consensus, the federation's comment will avoid picking an arbitrary side. Instead, it will reflect the true range of member experience: the operational appetite for shipment flexibility among small farmsteads, the legitimate testing caution raised by retailers, and a firm request that whatever federal language eventually passes must be legally robust enough to withstand judicial review.
