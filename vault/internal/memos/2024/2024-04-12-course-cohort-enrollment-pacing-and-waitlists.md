## Instructor Scheduling and Material Lead Times

Instructor availability remains the primary constraint for our specialized cohorts. Unlike general webinars, which carry no paid registrations and require no fixed instructor-to-student ratios, our cohort-based courses demand active grading, make-sheet reviews, and direct feedback sessions. Instructors assigned to the Lark and Hollow cohorts have noted that late curriculum revisions and last-minute material shipments lead directly to increased student drop-off during the first three weeks of instruction.

We need a firmer commitment from scheduling regarding lead times for syllabus finalization. If instructors are locked in at least six weeks prior to a cohort start date, Membership Operations can synchronize invoice generation and material mailings without relying on manual overrides. Furthermore, our experience with cave management and rind-washing modules shows that students perform significantly better when their reading packets arrive before the introductory session rather than during it.
