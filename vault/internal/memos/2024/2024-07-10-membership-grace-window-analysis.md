**Subject:** Membership grace-window analysis and renewal follow-up

Membership standing at the end of the second quarter sits at 989 current members, with 14 membership purchases recorded during this current week (all Individual tier, totaling 2333 gross USD, of which 9 are first-time members). While our volume remains steady, an internal review of renewals currently sitting in the grace window reveals a recurring friction point: members who rely heavily on directory access let their renewal notices sit behind day-to-day creamery tasks until auto-renew failures or grace-window warnings force their hand. This memo examines why routine renewals slip and outlines what steps Membership Operations is taking before the summer conference rush.

## The mechanics of the grace window

A membership sliding into the grace window is not lapsed, but its access state is vulnerable. Buyers who use the buyer directory weekly report that the uncertainty of whether their access will lapse is far more unsettling than any specific benefit notice. In several instances reviewed this quarter, members assumed auto-renewal was active when it was not. Because billing history begins in 2019, our multi-year members have developed habits around other professional subscriptions that handle background charges automatically, whereas ICF requires a manual checkout step unless properly toggled.

What actually breaks the inertia is rarely the second automated reminder email. It is direct contact from a committee volunteer or operations analyst asking if everything is working properly. A standard renewal notice is easily filed away as a task for later, but a direct check-in forces the member to evaluate what losing directory access would mean to their purchasing relationships.

## Operational findings on first-time versus renewing buyers

Of this week's 14 purchases, 9 are first-time individual members. That proportion is consistent with our broader annual trajectory—our year-end target sits at 1,051 members with 305 first-time joiners overall—but it highlights a support requirement. First-time joiners require onboarding prompts that established cheesemongers do not need, particularly around portal settings and auto-renewal preferences. When those prompts are missed, the member drifts toward the grace window precisely when they are getting the most value out of the network.

Furthermore, our data shows that engaged members—those showing up for workshops, reading bulletins weekly, and planning for Des Moines—are just as likely to let a routine renewal drift as anyone else. Their engagement is tied to the tool, not the billing mechanism underneath it.

## Capacity and seasonal strain

With the Annual Conference in Des Moines opening next week (EVT-2024-CONF, registering 351 members total so far, alongside 45 registrations for the Hollow Food Safety workshop), our team's capacity is heavily committed to badge processing and logistics reconciliation. We cannot staff a heavy manual calling campaign for every grace-window account right now without dropping event preparation.

## What we need

- Membership & Outreach Committee: review the draft volunteer check-in script for grace-window accounts by 17 July.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a targeted email adjustment to clarify auto-renewal status inside the member portal before the next renewal cycle.
- Membership Operations: run a weekly automated audit of portal settings to flag accounts with disabled auto-renew before they reach the final week of their grace window.
