This week's public advocacy post on the federal traceability rule has triggered a sharp rise in inbound inquiries from small creameries making soft and raw-milk cheeses. Members are discovering that their washed-rind and soft-ripened lines fall squarely within the covered product list while their hard cheeses remain exempt, despite both product types entering the same facility and utilizing identical milk intake logs. This operational friction is arriving precisely during Q4 membership renewal discussions, creating an avoidable churn risk if our field guidance does not match their actual production realities.

## The scope of the compliance burden

The federal rule requires enhanced critical-tracking-event records at receiving, transformation, and shipping, with a 24-hour turnaround requirement for handing records over to inspectors. For a one- or two-person creamery operating a curd table rather than an automated packing line, maintaining separate lot-code tracking specifically for soft cheeses while carving out hard cheeses introduces duplicate recordkeeping. Our advocacy work this month, which included a coalition meeting on labeling standards, highlights that small-producer exemptions remain narrowly tailored. Members shipping wholesale across state lines are finding themselves covered regardless of their annual volume, leaving farmstead producers exposed to rules written with industrial distribution centers in mind.

## Overlap with Q4 membership renewals

Membership Operations reports 8 total membership purchases confirmed so far this week, totaling 1,469 gross across SmallBusiness, Individual, and Enthusiast tiers, alongside 13 course completions. However, creameries struggling to interpret whether their specific cheese make falls inside the covered food list are questioning the practical value of their trade association membership if compliance answers are delayed. We cannot afford to treat regulatory inquiries as separate from membership retention. When a producer calls the member line asking whether their mixed-style creamery is compliant, a slow response directly impacts whether their SmallBusiness tier renewal clears next month.
