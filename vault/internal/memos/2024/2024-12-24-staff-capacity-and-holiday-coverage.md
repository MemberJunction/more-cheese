## The capacity bottleneck

The real friction this week is not our transaction volume; it is the collision between holiday staffing and the advocacy comment deadline. Petition and comment participation traditionally drops sharply between Christmas and New Year's, and members are flooding Advocacy & Standards and Membership Operations with questions about whether the 60-day aging floor will change or how to document water activity of 0.85 or below under a pH above 4.6. The same small group covering our holiday schedule is also trying to aggregate member comments on interstate shipments before the January close. We cannot maintain normal turnaround times while operating on a skeleton crew without dropping member inquiries or missing our internal filing windows.

## What we need

- Advocacy & Standards: confirm by Friday whether our running list of open comment periods needs an updated submission template before members write in during the first week of January.
- Communications: ensure our public-facing blog pointers and advocacy page links direct members to submit comments this week rather than waiting until the final rush in January.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a temporary shift in our phone coverage schedule so that afternoon inquiries are routed to email, freeing up staff to process year-end membership renewals and complete our course completion audits.
