This memo addresses our operational bandwidth for the upcoming spring workshop schedule and the competing demands of member inquiries following recent federal milk price reports. As we move into March 2024, our events schedule ramps up significantly with the Hobart workshop taking place on Tuesday 5 March and subsequent webinars and regional sessions following over the next eight weeks. ## The Hobart Workshop and Spring Volume

Tuesday's Workshop: Dairy Blue Styles in Hobart has 200 registered attendees. That is a heavy load for a single-day regional session, matching our prior large event footprints. Experience from last year tells us that post-event reconciliation, badge-scan cleanups, and participant follow-ups take at least three full working days following the event date.

At the same time, the federal milk price report released today has triggered a wave of member calls and portal inquiries about spring price sheets and contract gaps. Membership Operations and Communications are answering questions that cross into our workshop logistics territory. Because our staffing model relies on shared roles rather than dedicated event staff, every hour spent on milk pricing inquiries is an hour pulled away from the Birch Retailing workshop preparation in Querétaro scheduled for 2 April.

## The Eight-Week Calendar Pressure

Looking across the next eight weeks, our calendar includes Webinar: Insurance for Creameries 2024 on 20 March and 27 March, followed by the Querétaro workshop. Free webinars carry no registrations in our data, which saves us registration processing time, but the attendee support load is unpredictable when milk prices are fluctuating. Members want to know how the Class I base price improvement affects their contract terms, and they are calling our main line regardless of whether the topic is listed on the webinar agenda.

We cannot run two regional workshops, support two insurance webinars, and manage routine membership renewals with the current allocation of hours. Something has to move. Either we push the non-urgent membership operation reviews to late April, or we bring in temporary cross-coverage from Education & Credentialing.

- Office of the Chief Executive: approve a temporary reallocation of one staff member from Education & Credentialing to assist Events Team with workshop logistics for the next four weeks.
