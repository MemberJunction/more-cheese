While overall milk production runs comfortably ahead of last year, smaller cheese producers report that operating input costs such as propane for aging rooms, specialized packaging materials, and skilled labor do not follow the same downward schedule as feed costs. At our current total of 1132 members, smaller independent producers are forced to carry fixed overhead expenses across much tighter per-wheel margins, leading several members to quietly rethink and streamline their product lines this spring.

Members calling into our office this week are not inquiring about general association benefits; they are asking for urgent guidance on how to re-run their cost-per-wheel calculations and whether their existing wholesale contracts permit mid-quarter pricing adjustments to protect their bottom line.
