**Subject:** Milk price decline and member support readiness

This week's public communications discuss the seventh straight decline in the international dairy auction index and the margin squeeze facing small farmstead producers. While wholesale milk prices are falling, our membership numbers and order receipts for the week show a mixed operational picture: 16 membership purchases were recorded, totaling $3,329 in gross sales, with 11 at the Individual tier, 3 at Small Business, and 2 at Enthusiast. Total confirmed orders for the week reached 64 with a gross value of $10,390 against 1 cancellation, while cash captured stood at $11,187. This memo examines how current milk price trends intersect with our renewal patterns and what support channels we should prepare for the winter months.

## The gap between national indexes and local ledgers

The public commentary correctly notes that federal margin coverage does not capture the true cost structure of small farmstead operations that source or grow their own hay and feed locally. In our membership data, the Small Business tier renewal rate and new purchases continue to reflect severe operating friction. Even though feed costs have eased slightly this fall, the drop in farm-gate milk prices is hitting producers whose processing margins cannot absorb a simultaneous revenue contraction. We are seeing inquiries from members asking whether hardship grace periods will be extended through December, echoing the pressure points documented in our regional closure tracking.

## Course enrollment and advisory engagement

The Pricing & Margin for Specialty Cheese course has been highlighted as a relevant resource for members trying to reconcile national market averages with their actual books. Course enrollments this week reached 21, indicating that members are actively looking for analytical tools. However, zero course completions were recorded this week, and certification enrollment stands at just 1. This suggests members are turning to educational offerings for operational guidance rather than formal credentialing during periods of immediate financial stress. Our advisory response must therefore prioritize practical cash-flow and margin-analysis resources over long-term certification tracks.

## Operational alignment across departments

Communications has published guidance advising members to review their actual feed and energy spend against milk statements rather than national averages. We need to ensure our incoming member support inquiries are routed efficiently to match that advice. Membership Operations is currently tracking 1,440 members at week end, and we must ensure that grace-window requests are handled consistently without imposing administrative bottlenecks that alienate small producers facing cash-flow delays.

## What we need

- Advocacy & Standards: confirm whether any federal coalition meetings scheduled for next month will address regional feed-cost formulas or margin trigger adjustments.
- Communications: ensure future blog posts or member briefs continue to link directly to the Pricing & Margin course and related operational modules.
