**Subject:** Standards-of-identity docket — what we can say Wednesday and what we can't

Wednesday's post covers the federal proposal to strike a batch of old dairy standards of identity. The comment window has been extended and is still open, but we have not pinned down its closing date to our own satisfaction. This memo sets out what the post can safely claim, where the Standards Committee stands, and what we need from each of you before Wednesday morning.

## What is settled and what is not

Settled: the proposal exists, the window was extended, and members who make any product on the list can still file. The list is mostly low-volume items, but one standard touches a labeling detail on reduced-sodium cheddar, and we know of at least one member whose retail account depends on that label.

Not settled: the exact closing date. Two internal notes disagree by a few days, and neither cites the docket itself. Until someone reads the notice text and writes the date down with the page reference, nobody should print a date, a countdown, or the words "closes this week." The post can say the window is open and tell members to check the docket.

Also not settled: the separate ultra-filtered milk proposal. It has its own docket and its own timeline. Members confuse the two, and so did one of our own drafts last week. The post should keep them apart and should not imply a federation position on either.

## Where the Standards Committee is

The committee has tracked the docket since it opened and has a draft template for member testimony: what the standard currently requires, how the member's product and label work today, what the change would alter in practice. It does not have a position on repeal, and it should not acquire one by accident through the blog. Members are split. Some feel boxed in by old recipes; others rely on a standard as the floor behind a name they spent years building.

We are also logging member contact on this docket as advocacy activity, so that the count of members who asked for help is something we can report later rather than reconstruct. That log is manual for now. I think this is a tracking problem, not a participation problem: most of the questions arrive by phone and never reach the portal.

## Seams worth knowing about

- The committee's template is written for a creamery with a regulatory contact. A farmstead selling direct will find it heavy. A one-page version would help.
- The Standards handbook (list price $140) is the obvious reference for members unsure what their product's standard says. We should not promote it as a requirement for commenting. It isn't one.
- Nothing in this memo is legal advice, and the post must not read as telling a member what their label may lawfully say.

## What we need

- **Communications:** hold the closing date out of Wednesday's post unless Advocacy & Standards confirms it in writing by Tuesday afternoon.
- **Membership Operations:** tell us how member calls about the docket are being tagged, so the manual log matches.
- **Office of the Chief Executive:** confirm the federation stays neutral on repeal in public copy.
- **Advocacy & Standards (us):** read the notice text, record the closing date and page reference, and circulate the one-page template.
