## What the Madison workshop actually tested

Costing workshops are no longer continuing education in the traditional sense; they are emergency triage for farmstead creameries caught between rigid wholesale pricing and volatile energy and feed math. Of the 206 registered for Madison, 184 made it into the room, leaving 22 no-shows. In a normal operating environment, a 22 no-show count is unremarkable travel friction. In this era, several attendees arrived having already shuttered or downscaled their secondary herd operations over the winter, treating the make-sheet costing modules not as theory but as a survival audit.

The timing intersected directly with the public release on 28 April 2025 noting that the federal dairy margin figure improved slightly. Our participants were blunt during the break-out sessions: a single recovering margin month does not erase twelve months of accumulated cost inflation on feed, cultures, and refrigerated transport. Knowing your real cost per wheel—the core objective of the Prairie Costing curriculum—is the only defense against being misled by a single positive headline on milk production forecasts.

## Intersecting with membership and distribution realities

During the same week, Membership Operations booked 7 new membership purchases across Individual (5), Corporate (1), and Enthusiast (1) tiers, totaling $1970 in gross sales, bringing total current members to 1159 by week end. Meanwhile, advocacy logged a Coalition Meeting on raw-milk aging rules. The connection between these operational silos is tightening. Retailers and distributors are finding that small producers who completed our pricing and costing courses are far better equipped to renegotiate supply terms when wholesale accounts pull back. A recovery in Class III milk volume helps spot supplies, but it does not fix a broken margin structure at the farm gate.

We also observed that certification enrollments remain quiet (1 enrolled, 0 awarded this week), which indicates staff are too stretched with basic survival tasks to pursue credential polish right now. ## Capacity and internal coordination friction
