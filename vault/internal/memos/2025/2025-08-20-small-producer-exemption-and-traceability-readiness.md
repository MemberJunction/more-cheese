**Subject:** Small-producer exemption and traceability readiness

The federal food-safety regulator formally proposed pushing back the compliance date on its expanded food-traceability recordkeeping rule by thirty months, opening a comment period that closes in early September. Cheese is one of the high-risk foods explicitly covered once the rule takes effect. While an extended timeline gives small farmstead operators much-needed runway, the exemption language built into the rule remains rigid about how direct-to-consumer sales interact with regional wholesale channels. This memo outlines what our comment filing needs to address and why our current membership data shows that many small creameries could inadvertently lose their exemption status simply by diversifying their sales mix.

## Why the exemption threshold needs closer scrutiny

The existing small-producer exemption was drafted with a straightforward retail pattern in mind: direct farm-stand or farmers' market sales where the maker retains custody of the lot-level tracking until the consumer takes possession. However, a creamery that qualifies for the exemption through direct sales alone often loses that protection the moment a meaningful share of the same product moves through a covered regional distributor, even if total production volume remains constant. In the current economic climate, where feed and energy cost inflation are squeezing small farmstead margins, members are diversifying sales channels out of necessity rather than expansion. A rule that penalizes a creamery for adding a local wholesale account by stripping away its small-producer exemption is unworkable.

Our advocacy actions this week include filing initial testimony on interstate cheese shipment and compiling member feedback on labeling and traceability. We need real-world channel mix data from members to substantiate our comments before the September deadline. General objections carry little weight with regulators; specific accounts of how distribution shifts affect lot-level recordkeeping burdens are what drive text changes.
