**Subject:** Small creamery margin risk and the federal shutdown data gap

The federal government shutdown that began October 1 has directly impacted our membership by delaying the USDA milk production data required for dairy margin coverage calculations. Without this state-level yield adjustment data, risk program payments are stalled indefinitely. While feed prices have remained favorable this fall with corn supply strong and grain costs down from a year ago, those favorable input costs do not solve the cash-flow pressure faced by farmstead creameries whose insurance payments are trapped behind a federal data gap. Operations must prepare for an influx of member inquiries this week.

## The scale of the exposure

Our current member count stands at 1359 at week end, with 21 membership purchases recorded this week alone—13 of them first-time enrollments split across Individual (13), Corporate (3), Enthusiast (3), and SmallBusiness (2) tiers, generating a gross of $6348. Total confirmed orders for the week reached 26 with a gross of $7960 and zero cancellations, while cash captured came in at $6575.

While these top-line figures show steady acquisition, the underlying exposure for small-scale producers is severe. Larger operations with substantial working capital can absorb a payment delay of several weeks. In contrast, a farmstead creamery running on tight margins cannot easily wait out an unknown payment window when deciding whether to commit working capital to winter hay purchases or equipment maintenance at current price levels.

## Committee activity and member touchpoints

Our committees are already actively addressing these operational strains. The Food Safety Committee completed its Q4 2025 meeting today, 6 October, and the Membership & Outreach Committee completed its Q4 meeting on 8 October. Both groups report rising anxiety among members who rely on predictable risk-program disbursements to manage seasonal cash crunches. Furthermore, course enrollments remain active with 28 new course sign-ups this week, six completions, and upcoming cohorts such as Humidity & Airflow Control starting on 29 October and Maturation Planning for Small Rooms starting on 30 November. Members are investing in education even as their margin calculations are upended by federal delays.

## Communications alignment

Communications has published initial guidance advising members not to assume their third-quarter numbers are rejected, and to keep meticulous personal records of milk prices and feed costs. We must ensure our staff mailboxes speak with a unified voice. Membership Operations should direct inbound queries regarding delayed margin checks to our advocacy guidance without promising an artificial federal timeline that we cannot guarantee.

## What we need

- Membership Operations: log every inbound shutdown-related margin inquiry so we can quantify member impact for future advocacy actions.
- Communications: reinforce in all member newsletters that feed cost relief should be factored into overall make-sheets independently of delayed federal risk payments.
- Office of the Chief Executive: approve outreach to regional lenders regarding processing delays for small farmstead operations.
