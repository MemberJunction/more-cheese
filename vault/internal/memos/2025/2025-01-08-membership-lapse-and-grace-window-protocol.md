**Subject:** Membership lapse rates and grace-window protocol during energy inflation

As small farmstead creameries face steep winter heating and propane bills, Membership Operations has received an increased volume of requests regarding membership renewals, payment extensions, and temporary grace-window applications. At the close of last year, total membership stood at 1,447, reflecting 537 first-time members, 894 retained accounts, and 157 lapsed accounts from the prior base of 1,051. While the overall year-end tally shows growth, January renewal inquiries indicate that single-family creameries facing high cave heating costs are hesitant to commit to annual dues payments. This memo outlines our current operational exposure, reviews the grace-window request volume handled so far this week, and proposes a formalised extension protocol to prevent avoidable lapses.

## Current volume and renewal friction

For the week of 6 January 2025, total membership purchases reached 14, comprising 6 first-time buyers and 8 renewals, generating $2,527 in gross revenue across Individual (10 purchases), Enthusiast (3 purchases), and SmallBusiness (1 purchase) tiers. Total confirmed orders for the week reached 49, contributing $7,817 in gross revenue with zero cancellations, and total cash captured was $9,225. Active membership at week-end sits at 1,063. However, these figures mask a growing number of members contacting our team to request 60-day payment extensions or tier downgrades because their propane and feed bills have outpaced their cash flow.

Under our standard operating procedures, a lapsed member is dropped from the portal immediately upon expiration of their 30-day grace period. In the current economic climate, applying a rigid cutoff risks alienating long-standing farmstead producers who need federation resources—such as our cost-tracking worksheet and advocacy updates—most urgently. The recent public blog posts have correctly pointed out that energy costs and feed bills are driving difficult decisions in rural creameries; our internal response must match that reality without compromising our financial reporting.

## Proposed grace-window adjustments

We propose expanding the standard renewal grace window from 30 days to 60 days specifically for SmallBusiness and Individual tier members who submit a written hardship inquiry citing energy or feed cost pressures. This adjustment will not waive dues, but it will maintain portal access and continuous membership standing while payment plans are structured. Membership Operations will track these extended accounts separately to ensure gross revenue projections are not distorted in weekly reporting.

## What we need

- Office of the Chief Executive: Formal approval of the 60-day hardship grace window for propane-affected farmstead members.
- Advocacy & Standards: Alignment on messaging so that members inquiring about hardship receive consistent guidance across advocacy and membership teams.
- Communications: Ensure future blog updates continue to direct distressed producers to Membership Operations before they let their accounts lapse.
- Membership Operations: Implement the extended tracking spreadsheet starting 10 January 2025.
