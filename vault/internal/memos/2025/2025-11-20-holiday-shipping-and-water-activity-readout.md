As we head into the peak holiday shipping crush, small producers are facing compounding cost pressures from feed and energy inflation alongside severe transit delays. Our upcoming free webinar on water activity and shelf life is timed directly for this window. Producers need practical guidance on how to write defensible shelf-life claims and why two cheeses from the same vat can behave differently once exposed to warehouse temperature swings. ## The context for members

Members are contacting the office with urgent questions about transit delays holding gift boxes in carrier facilities for days longer than scheduled. When a box sits in a warm distribution center, a soft-ripened wheel or a delicate natural rind can suffer moisture migration and surface sweating that compromises the final presentation. Water activity measures the unbound moisture available for microbial growth and spoilage reactions, providing a much more reliable indicator of shelf-life safety and quality stability than total moisture content alone.

Our webinar next week is designed to bridge the gap between academic food science and counter-staff reality. We will cover how packaging choices affect water activity drift during transit, how to establish realistic expiration guidelines for direct-to-consumer fulfillment, and how to separate safety limits from sensory degradation. Members shipping direct for the first time this season have reported this is their weakest operating area.
