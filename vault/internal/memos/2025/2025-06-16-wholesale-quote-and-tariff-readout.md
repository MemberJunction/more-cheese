## The domestic wholesale transmission mechanism

When export-oriented commodity cheese and surplus fluid volume lose their traditional foreign destinations due to retaliatory duties, processors seek alternative markets. A significant portion of this displaced volume is redirected into domestic wholesale channels where a large segment of our membership operates. Increased supply competing for the same pool of domestic buyers naturally depresses the price points buyers are willing to accept. Consequently, regional farmstead creameries observing flatter spring quotes should understand that this phenomenon often reflects redirected commodity milk and cheese rather than a decline in the intrinsic quality of their own artisan products.

Our advocacy actions this week reflect this broader market strain.
