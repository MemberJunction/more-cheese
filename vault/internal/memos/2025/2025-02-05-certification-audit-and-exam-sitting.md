## Credential records and prerequisite checks

During our review of candidate files, we noted that several applications for advanced credential tracks moved forward without verified completion of foundational prerequisites. While Ritu Ferris successfully earned the Cheese Foundations Certificate this week, other candidate files lack complete documentation of prerequisite modules. In a period where small farmstead creameries face acute economic pressures, candidates are naturally eager to accelerate their credentials to support market positioning. However, bypassing administrative verification compromises the integrity of our credentialing standards. We must ensure that no exam sitting is confirmed without a fully audited prerequisite trail.

Furthermore, recertification fee processing has revealed a misalignment between payment records and active credential status. The generated payment data indicates modest recertification fee volume over the past year, yet our manual tracking sheets do not consistently tie those payments back to the correct member ID. This creates vulnerability during audit reviews and complicates renewal inquiries from members.

## Administrative workflow adjustments

To resolve these gaps, Education & Credentialing and Membership Operations must establish a shared verification checklist before any future exam sitting is scheduled. We cannot rely on informal approvals or assume that course completion records automatically synchronize with credential databases. Every candidate dossier must be signed off by both teams at least two weeks prior to an exam date.

We also need to clarify the handling of renewal grace windows. As detailed in recent internal discussions regarding member retention, small-producer distress often leads to delayed administrative filings. When a member applies for recertification past their renewal date, our current system lacks a standardized grace period policy, forcing staff to make ad-hoc decisions on fee waivers.
