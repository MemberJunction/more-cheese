# More Cheese — content calendar (one page)

*All content is fictional demonstration material for the International Cheese Federation (ICF), the association
behind the More Cheese demo. Every piece carries a fiction disclaimer. Full design: `content/VAULT-DESIGN.md`.*

## Where it lives

| Surface | What | Audience | Status (2026-09-11) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **morecheese.org** (WordPress) | Blog posts, later newsletters, annual reports, press releases, competition results, committee minutes | Public — the corpus Betty should ground on | 82 posts live (Jan–Jun 2019); Jul–Sep 2019 publishing this weekend |
| **Dropbox → MemberJunction Knowledge Hub** | Memos, decks, spreadsheets, email threads (`vault/internal/`) | Staff only — searchable inside Explorer, tagged and vectorized | 6 sample files indexed; volume run follows the blog |
| **Repo `content/` (Markdown + frontmatter)** | Source of truth for both; publishing scripts read the frontmatter (`visibility`, `doc_type`, `category`, `date`, `era`) | Internal | — |

## Cadence

| Rhythm | Public (website) | Internal (Dropbox) |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | 3 blog posts (Mon / Wed / Fri) | 1 memo or deck outline; 1 email thread; 1 CSV when there is a number worth tabling |
| Monthly | 1 newsletter (digest of the month's posts) | 1 spreadsheet (membership / revenue close) |
| Quarterly | 6 committee minutes (one per committee) | 1 quarterly business review deck; board packet in Q4 |
| Per event | 1 press release for the Annual Conference; 1 for competition results | pre-event deck, post-event thread, registration CSV |
| Annually | 1 annual report; 1 competition-results page | board packet; budget CSV |

Steady state is roughly 156 posts, 12 newsletters, 24 minutes and ~8 releases a year on the public side. Seven years
(2019–2025) is about 1,300 public and 900 internal pieces.

## Blog categories (the six Betty will see most)

Industry News · Member Spotlight · Education · Certification · Events · Advocacy — each week mixes three of them.
Posts run 600–800 words, first-person association voice, and cite real dated trade/government sources from that week.

## The seven-year arc (what each year is about)

| Year | Era | Public storyline |
|---|---|---|
| 2019 | Pre-pandemic baseline | Confident growth: conference previews, dues framing, straightforward advocacy |
| 2020 | Pandemic shock | Event cancellations, emergency member support, online-course pivot |
| 2021 | Virtual pivot | Virtual symposium, online credentials, retention wins among small creameries |
| 2022–24 | Artisan boom | In-person conference returns, competition entries climb, three growth years |
| 2025 | Creamery closures | Cost inflation, hardship dues, closure support |

## Generation order

1. Blog 2019 → 2025, one quarter at a time, validator-gated, published as each quarter passes review (running now).
2. Internal weekly comms alongside each blog quarter (memos, threads, spreadsheets → Dropbox).
3. Newsletters, minutes and press releases per year once that year's posts exist.
4. Annual reports and competition results last — they need the final structured dataset.
