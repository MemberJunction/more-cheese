This is the week the application window closes on the industry's broadest cheese-knowledge exam — the one that covers everything from make process to affinage to sales, in a single sitting, open to anyone who's logged enough hours in the trade. We field a wave of questions every year right about now, mostly some version of: is this worth it, and how does it relate to what the federation offers?

We can't speak for exams we don't administer. What we can do is walk through our own **Certified Cheese Professional (CCP)** credential, since the overlap in subject matter is real and members ask us to compare them constantly.

## What CCP actually covers

The CCP track isn't a single test at the end — it's a sequence built to mirror how a cheese career actually develops. Members typically arrive having already completed foundational coursework (our *Cheese Foundations Certificate* is the common starting point), then move through modules covering production chemistry, sensory evaluation, affinage and maturation, and business fundamentals like pricing and labeling compliance. The credential is designed for people who touch more than one part of the business — a head cheesemaker who also handles wholesale accounts, or a cheesemonger who's started helping a small producer with recipe development.

Where it differs from a single-sitting exam is pacing. You can complete CCP modules across multiple course cohorts over a year or two, which matters if you're working full-time in a creamery or behind a counter and can't clear three uninterrupted hours for a written exam. Several members have told us they used our track specifically to prepare for the broader industry exam — not as a substitute for it, but as a structured way to shore up the sections where their day-to-day work doesn't naturally cover the material. A cheesemonger, for instance, gets less daily exposure to make-floor chemistry than someone working the vat every morning, and vice versa.

## If you're deciding this week

If you're staring down an application deadline for an outside credential and wondering whether you're ready, our advice hasn't changed in years: pull your actual work history before you decide anything. Both that exam and our own track require a real accounting of hours across production, sales, and service — not a guess. Members who've been through it say the eligibility paperwork takes longer than expected, so don't leave it for the last day even if the application itself is short.

If the timing doesn't work this cycle, that's not a wasted year. Our Education Committee hears from members every spring who use a missed exam window to instead complete a CCP module or two, so that when the next application period opens, they're walking in with a stronger foundation rather than cramming.

If you haven't started any credential track yet, the *Cheese Foundations Certificate* is still the right entry point regardless of which broader credential you're eyeing later — it's designed to be a common base under all of our advanced tracks. Reach out to the Education Committee if you want a rough read on how many hours of coursework you're likely looking at based on where you already are.
