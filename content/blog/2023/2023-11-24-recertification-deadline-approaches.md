If you earned your Certified Cheese Professional (CCP) credential a few years back and haven't thought about it since, this is the week to think about it. Our recertification window for CCP runs through November 30, and members who let it lapse don't lose their original exam result — they just have to sit for the full exam again from scratch to get the credential back, rather than completing the shorter renewal path that's open right now.

That distinction trips people up every year. A lapsed credential isn't a paperwork problem you can quietly fix in January. It's a real gap that puts you back at the starting line, and it's one of the more avoidable ones we see, since the renewal window is announced well in advance and doesn't move on us or on you.

## What recertification actually checks, and who tends to find it harder than expected

Recertification isn't a rubber stamp. It's built to confirm that a credential holder's knowledge has kept pace with a category that keeps changing, from evolving food-safety expectations to a specialty cheese market that looks meaningfully different than it did when the CCP program started. Expect the renewal to touch current handling and labeling practices, updates to any standards your credential covers, and a general check that you're still current on service and storage guidance, not just able to recite what you learned the first time around.

For most members who've stayed active in the industry, this is a lighter lift than the original exam. You're not learning the material for the first time; you're demonstrating you haven't drifted from it. Members who've been out of daily counter or cave work for a stretch, whether from a role change or time away from the industry, tend to find the renewal harder than they expect, and that's exactly the group the window is designed to catch before a lapse becomes permanent. If that describes your last year or two, budget more study time than you think you need, not less.

If you don't remember when your CCP was issued or when your current renewal cycle closes, don't guess. Check your member record before the 30th rather than after — a credential that lapses over a holiday week is a genuinely common way people lose track of this, since the deadline sits right in the middle of a season most of us are distracted by other things, between family, travel, and a retail counter that's suddenly busier than it's been all year.

## Building toward a first credential

If you're earlier in your career and CCP still feels like a distant goal, the more accessible starting point is our Cheese Foundations Certificate, which several current CCP holders describe as the credential that made the bigger exam feel achievable rather than intimidating. From there, a Food Safety & HACCP Certificate or Sensory Evaluation Certificate builds toward the fuller CCP scope at a pace that fits around an actual working schedule, rather than asking you to cram a career's worth of knowledge into one sitting.

Either way, don't let this particular deadline sneak past you the way a lapsed membership sometimes does. Thirty days from now, the easier path closes, and the harder one is the only one left. If you're not sure whether you're inside the window, ask us this week rather than the week after Thanksgiving — we'd rather answer the question twice than watch a member miss it by a few days.
