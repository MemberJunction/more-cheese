## 1. World Cheese Awards (Guild of Fine Food, UK) — THE international competition analog

Run by the **Guild of Fine Food** (UK, gff.co.uk). First held **1988**; the 2025 edition was the **37th**. A
single-venue, **cheese-only** competition that **travels internationally** — hosted by a different partner city/region
in collaboration with the Guild each year. Judged in **autumn** (mid-October–mid-November), all medals decided in
**one judging day**, with a trade congress/festival wrapped around it (2–3 days).

### Entries, countries, hosts (2022–2027)

| Year | Edition | Host city | Entries | Countries | World Champion | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 34th | Newport, Wales | ~4,434 | ~40 | Le Gruyère AOP surchoix (CH) | gff.co.uk archive ⚠️ |
| 2023 | 35th | Trondheim, Norway | **4,502** (record at the time) | **43** | Nidelven Blå (Norway, 3rd win) | gff.co.uk, CNN Travel, WCA TV (2023) ✅ |
| 2024 | 36th | Viseu, Portugal | **4,786** (record) | **~47** | Queijo de Ovelha Amanteigado, Quinta do Pomar (Portugal) | gff.co.uk, culture mag, rennetandrind.co.uk, WCA TV (2024) ✅ |
| 2025 | 37th | Bern, Switzerland | **5,244** (record) | **46** | Gruyère AOP Vorderfultigen Spezial, Bergkäserei Vorderfultigen (CH), 18-mo | gff.co.uk, swissinfo, dairyreporter.com, capitalofcheese.com (2025) ✅ |
| 2026–27 | 38th | Córdoba, Spain (announced) | — | — | — | WCA Instagram (Nov 2025) ⚠️ |

Host-country effect is real and large: Switzerland alone entered **nearly 1,000 cheeses** in 2025 (dairyreporter.com).
Portugal similarly surged in 2024. Model this as a **+15–25% host-country entry bump**.

### Judging structure (2025 edition, well documented)

- **~240–265 judges** from ~40 countries (cheesemakers, retailers, buyers, journalists) — gff.co.uk / culture mag (2025) ⚠️
- Judges work in **teams of 2–3 per table**, each table holding **~45 cheeses**; ~110 tables total — culturecheesemag.com "Reflections on the WCA in Switzerland" (2025) ✅
- Medal tiers per table: **Bronze / Silver / Gold**, plus each table nominates **one Super Gold** (its best cheese)
- 2025: **107 Super Golds** → an international **Super Jury** (14 jurors) debates on stage → **14 finalists** ranked live → World Champion — cuisinehelvetica.com (2025) ✅
- Scale of awards: several thousand medals across bronze/silver/gold (medal rate far higher than ACS's ~25% — roughly **40–50% of entries medal** ❓ spot-check)
- Entries are open internationally — **no membership prerequisite** (unlike ACS, where only org members may enter)

**Key structural differences vs ACS J&C** (for the data model): rotates host city/country annually; one-day judging;
table-based (not category-based) first-round judging; Super Gold → Super Jury → ranked Top 14 finale; entries ~3x ACS.

## 3. International & European cheese-sector organizations

### Does an INTERNATIONAL individual-membership cheese professional society exist? — Yes: the Guilde

**Guilde Internationale des Fromagers / Confrérie de Saint-Uguzon** (Paris, France) — the closest real analog to the
fictional "International Cheese Federation":

- Founded **1969** in France — guildedesfromagers.us/about-us, wisfarmer.com ✅
- **~5,950 members in 33 countries** (press release, mid-2010s, max-mccalman.com PDF ✅); a 2019 inductee blog claims
  she was **member #7,000** (everythingerica.com ⚠️) → model **6,000–7,500 members, 33–40 countries**
- **Individual membership with ceremonial grades/ranks** (from guildedesfromagers.fr induction reports ✅):
  professional track — **Compagnon → Garde et Juré (Guard & Juror) → Prud'homme → Maître Fromager (Master
  Cheesemaker) → Maître Honoris Causa**; a parallel **Confrérie** track exists for non-professionals/enthusiasts ⚠️
- **International chapters**: e.g. the **New World Chapter** covers USA, Canada, Mexico, Australia (guildedesfromagers.us ✅);
  inductions happen at events worldwide (Montréal, Buffalo, Madison…)
- Led by a **Provost** (Roland Barthélémy), who also chairs the Mondial du Fromage jury — nice cross-linkage for demo data
- Character: honorific/networking society (induction by recognition, not exam) — **no dues-driven renewal machinery
  documented**, so the demo's renewal/dues engine still has to borrow from association-management benchmarks (§4)

| **Italy** | One **consorzio di tutela** per DOP cheese (e.g. Parmigiano Reggiano ~300 dairies) + Assolatte (industry) | **56 DOP cheeses registered** | pdononpdocheeses.it ✅, clal.it |

**Structural insight for the demo**: real international bodies are either (a) **federations of national associations**
(EDA, FACE — org-level members, no individuals) or (b) **honorific individual societies** (Guilde — grades, chapters,
no exam). The fictional ICF can plausibly blend both: individual professional members in country chapters +
organizational members (creameries), which conveniently preserves the ACS-style dual membership already modeled.

## 4. International association-management benchmarks

**Honest finding: there is no solid public Europe/global equivalent of the MGI renewal benchmarks.**

- **MGI Membership Marketing Benchmarking Report** (2024: median renewal **85%**; trade > individual; 2025: 45%
  reporting growth, down from 47%/49%) — sample is overwhelmingly **North American** associations. marketinggeneral.com,
  aro.org PDF (2024) ✅. MGI does not publish a Europe-only renewal cut.
- **ESAE (European Society of Association Executives)** exists and runs surveys — but its flagship is the
  **ESAE–Mavence Leadership & Remuneration Survey** (2nd edition, 2025–26), i.e. staff pay/leadership benchmarks,
  **not member-renewal or dues benchmarks**. esae.eu, esae.glueup.com ✅
- Boardroom magazine / ASAE international research: qualitative coverage of European association trends (Brussels-based
  intl. associations, congress rotation) — **no hard renewal-rate datasets** surfaced.
- Adjacent data points: MSAE "State of Membership" repeats the 85% median (US); Rossell/MGI 18-year retrospective:
  40–50% of associations report growth in a typical year (US-dominated).

**Recommendation**: keep MGI's curves (renewal 80–90% band, trade higher than individual) as the demo's engine, with an
explicit **confidence downgrade** for non-US members (see "WHAT STAYS US/ACS-CALIBRATED" below). Known qualitative
differences to bake in as assumptions, not facts: European professional bodies lean more heavily on **employer-paid /
org-level membership** and **VAT-inclusive, lower individual dues**; Brussels-based international federations are
funded ~100% by national-association dues (per EDA's lobbyfacts entry ✅).

## 5. Regulatory / legislative topics for an INTERNATIONAL cheese federation

Replaces the US-only list (FDA raw-milk, FSMA, US labeling). 12 topics, EU/global framing:

1. **EU PDO/PGI protected names** — Reg. 1151/2012 (recast as Reg. 2024/1143): **~180+ protected cheeses** EU-wide (Italy 56 DOP, France 46 AOP); registration, amendment, and enforcement workload. (iloveimportedcheese.com, pdononpdocheeses.it ✅)
2. **Common-names dispute, EU side** — the EU exports GI protection through FTAs: CETA (Canada) grandfathered compromises on feta/asiago/gorgonzola; **Australia–EU FTA: 56 dairy GIs incl. 53 cheeses**; the 2023 US court ruling that "gruyère" is generic is the EU's headline setback. (dfat.gov.au ✅, cato.org, lcojlaw.com)
3. **Raw-milk cheese rules** — EU hygiene package (Reg. 853/2004) permits raw-milk cheese under process controls + "made with raw milk" labelling — **no aging minimum** — vs the **US FDA 60-day rule**; market-access asymmetry for young raw-milk cheeses. (marlerblog.com, fda.gov ✅)
4. **Codex Alimentarius dairy standards** — General Standard for Cheese **CXS 283-1978** plus ~35 individual cheese standards; battleground for international naming/composition norms. (codexalimentarius.org ❓ standard number from LLM knowledge — spot-check)
5. **UK post-Brexit trade friction** — export health certificates, 2024 UK Border Target Operating Model import checks on EU cheese, "Not for EU" labelling (Windsor Framework), and the 2025 UK–EU SPS-agreement negotiations. (⚠️ LLM knowledge — spot-check dates)
6. **Listeria & microbiological criteria** — EU Reg. 2073/2005 (Listeria monocytogenes limits in RTE foods), periodic EU-wide soft-cheese recalls; global equivalents (FSMA in US). (⚠️)
7. **Tariffs & market access** — recurring US tariff actions against EU food (Airbus-dispute 25% tariffs 2019–21; renewed 2025 tariff tensions), CETA cheese-quota utilization in Canada. (⚠️ spot-check current status)
8. **Sustainability & methane** — EU Green Deal / Farm-to-Fork; **Industrial & Livestock Rearing Emissions Directive (IED 2.0)** scope debate for cattle; Dutch nitrogen-crisis herd reductions; New Zealand's on-again/off-again agricultural methane pricing. (environment.ec.europa.eu ✅, catf.us, BBC Future)
9. **EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR)** — traceability duties on imported feed (soy) cascading into dairy supply chains. (⚠️)
10. **Green Claims / eco-labelling** — EU Green Claims Directive limiting "climate-neutral cheese" marketing; Nutri-Score front-of-pack fights (cheese scores poorly; AOP sector lobbied against). (⚠️)
11. **Packaging & Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR)** — recyclability and reuse mandates hitting cheese wax, rind coatings, and deli counter packaging. (⚠️)
12. **Dairy-term protection vs plant-based & precision fermentation** — EU Reg. 1308/2013 dairy designations + CJEU *TofuTown* ruling (plant products can't be called "cheese" in the EU); novel-food approval pathway for precision-fermentation casein "cheese". (⚠️)

## OPEN DECISIONS FOR THE TEAM

1. **Member geography mix** — adopt the proposed 60% Europe / 20% NA / 20% RoW split, or keep NA-heavier (35–40%) to
   reflect a federation that *grew out of* a North American society? Affects seed-data generators and demo storylines.
2. **Single vs multi-currency dues** — one settlement currency (EUR, since the flagship rotates in Europe? or USD/GBP)
   vs multi-currency invoicing per chapter (EUR/GBP/USD/CHF). Multi-currency showcases more schema (ExchangeRate,
   InvoiceCurrency) but complicates rollups.
3. **One flagship traveling awards+congress (WCA model) vs fixed annual conference + regional satellite events (ACS
   model)** — the traveling model needs `HostCity`/`HostCountry`/host-partner entities and the host-country entry bump;
   the satellite model reuses more of the prior ACS-shaped schema. Hybrid (traveling flagship + 3–4 regional
   competitions at 500–2,000 entries, per §2) is most realistic.
4. **Competition entry eligibility** — keep the ACS rule (org members only → FK constraint from CompetitionEntry to an
   active org membership) or the WCA rule (open to all, membership optional)? The ACS rule is better for demoing
   referential integrity; the WCA rule is more authentic internationally.
5. **Medal scheme** — WCA scheme (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Super Gold/Champion, ~40–50% medal rate) vs ACS scheme
   (1st/2nd/3rd per category, ~25%). Changes medal-volume anchors materially.
6. **Honorific grades track** — add the Guilde-style grades (Compagnon → Maître Fromager) as a parallel recognition
   ladder alongside exam-based certification? Adds international flavor + a second progression dataset.
7. **Chapter model** — country chapters (Guilde-style, incl. a multi-country "New World Chapter") vs region chapters
   matching the Region value list; determines whether Chapter is a first-class entity with its own events/officers.
