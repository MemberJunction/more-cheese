# Association Finance & Operations Benchmarks (Workstream R6)

> **Provenance**: LLM-researched via web search (July 2026) for the synthetic-data generator modeling a
> ~2,500-member US professional society (cheese industry). Primary sources were located and, where
> possible, read directly (MGI 2024 full report PDF; PARN Financial Benchmarking 2020–21 full report PDF;
> ASAE Foundation volunteer-management study PDF). Numbers quoted from full-text extraction of those PDFs
> are high confidence; secondary-source citations and all ESTIMATE rows should be **spot-checked by a human**
> before being treated as authoritative. The paywalled ASAE *Association Operating Ratio Report* (now
> "Benchmarking: Financial and Operational Performance") was not directly accessible; its headline dues
> ratios are cited via ASAE's own Associations Now article.

Key primary sources used:

- **MGI** — Marketing General Inc., *2024 Membership Marketing Benchmarking Report* (16th ed., n = 695
  associations; IMO = individual membership organization subsample n ≈ 200–280 per question). Full PDF read.
- **PARN** — Professional Associations Research Network, *Financial Benchmarking for Professional Bodies
  2020–2021* (UK professional bodies, n = 215–249 per metric; FY2021 accounts). Full PDF read. UK data —
  directionally applicable to US professional societies; currency ≈ £1 ≈ $1.25–1.35 for the period.
- **ASAE ORR** — ASAE Foundation, *Association Operating Ratio Report*, 15th ed. (2016), cited via
  Associations Now "Data Nugget: Dues Downsizing" (Nov/Dec 2016).
- **ASAE RF Volunteer Study** — ASAE Research Foundation, *A Holistic Approach to Association Volunteer
  Management* (2023; staff survey covering 2017–2021). Full PDF read (mirror hosted at same.org).
- **BoardSource** — *Leading with Intent* summary via boardsource.org "Structuring Board Committees".

PARN is the best available guide to the *relative* ordering of non-dues categories
(events/education > publications > sponsorship > investments).

**Typical org chart at this scale (ESTIMATE, consistent with the ratios above)**: CEO/ED; membership
manager (+ coordinator); meetings/events manager (+ coordinator); education/certification manager;
communications/marketing manager; finance/ops manager (often part-time or outsourced CPA); admin
assistant — i.e., **8–12 FTE** with several staff wearing two hats.

**Reasoning for the estimate**: member touches cluster around renewal (1 renewal cycle/yr, with 98% of
associations emailing and ~half phoning), event registration (1–2 events/yr), and credential/login issues.
A passive member generates ~0 contacts; an engaged member 2–5. A blended **0.4–1.0 inbound service
contacts per member per year** is consistent with a 2–3 person membership team (≈ 9–12 contacts/day at
2,500 members × 1.0/yr — well within one coordinator's capacity alongside other duties).

## Proposed generator targets (2,500-member US professional society, cheese industry)

Tolerances are for validating generated data against targets. **ESTIMATE** = no published number for this
exact metric/scale; value is derived/judged from the sources above.

| Metric | Target | Tolerance | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Total annual revenue** | **$1.5M** | ±$0.4M ($1.1–1.9M) | PARN ≤5k-member avg $1.4M; MGI budget distribution; dues-share sanity math |
| **Dues as % of revenue** | **35%** | ±8 pts (27–43%) | ASAE professional-society 30% avg; small societies skew higher (less non-dues infrastructure) |
| **Individual dues rate** | **$210/yr** (blended across member types) | ±$50 | Derived: $1.5M × 35% ÷ 2,500 = $210. ESTIMATE (dues rate itself not benchmarked here) |
| **Non-dues mix** (as % of total revenue) | Meetings/events **28%**, education/certification **15%**, sponsorship/advertising **10%**, publications **5%**, investments/other **7%** | ±5 pts each; must sum with dues to 100% | ESTIMATE — ordering per PARN (events/education > publications > sponsorship > investments), scaled to a small US society where the annual conference dominates |
| **Grace period** | **2 months** (org-level config) | Acceptable alternates: 0, 1, 3 months | MGI IMO distribution — mode is 2–3 months (40%); 23% none, 21% one month |
| **Renewal basis** | ~~Anniversary-date renewal~~ **SUPERSEDED (R8, 2026-07-02): calendar-year Dec-31 expiry** — ACS ground truth; drives the December renewal-spike regime | — | MGI: IMOs 50% anniversary vs 45% fixed, but the in-domain analog wins; JSON `membership_cycle_convention` is canonical |
| **Overall renewal rate** | **82%** | ±4 pts (78–86%) | MGI IMO median 82% |
| **First-year renewal rate** | **64%** | ±6 pts | MGI IMO median 64% |
| **Share of renewals arriving after due date** | **25%** of successful renewals land 1–90 days late (roughly half of those in the first 30 days) | 15–35% | ESTIMATE — anchored on 81% of associations honoring post-expiry grace, renewal efforts running ≥3 months past expiry, and 22% never stopping contact |
| **Renewal-notice cadence** | First touch 3 months pre-expiration; ~6–8 touches; final touch ~3 months post-expiration | Start 1–4 months pre; end 1–6 months post | MGI start/end distributions (modes: 3 months before, 3 months after) |
| **Staff FTE** | **10** | 8–13 | PARN <5k-member avg 13 FTE; members/staff median 281 ⇒ ~9; $1.5M ÷ ~$140k/FTE ⇒ ~11 |
| **Members per staff** | **250** | 175–485 (PARN IQR) | PARN median 281, IQR 176–485 |
| **Board size** | **14** | 11–18 | ASAE RF median 14 |
| **Standing committees** | **7** | 5–9 | ASAE RF median 8 (sample skews larger orgs; shade down slightly for 2,500 members) |
| **Members per committee** | **8** | 5–12 | ASAE RF derived ≈7–8; BoardSource guidance |
| **Task forces (concurrent)** | **3** | 1–5 | ASAE RF median 4, shaded down for size |
| **Committee meeting cadence** | Board: 4–6 meetings/yr; exec committee: monthly-ish (8–12/yr); standing committees: **quarterly (4/yr)** with 1–2 monthly outliers (e.g., conference program committee in the run-up) | ±2 meetings/yr per body | ESTIMATE — practitioner guidance ("monthly or quarterly"); no survey distribution exists |
| **% of members in a volunteer role** | **5%** (~125 members across board+committees+task forces; consistent with 14 board + ~60 committee seats + task forces, less overlap) | 4–8% | ASAE RF ratio-derived 3.75–6% |
| **Member-service contacts** | **0.6 inbound contacts/member/yr** (~1,500/yr ≈ 6/business day), seasonal spike ×2–3 in renewal month(s) and pre-conference | 0.3–1.2 | ESTIMATE — no association benchmark exists; bounded by IT-support per-user rates (6.5–16.5/user/yr) being a clear over-bound for a member relationship |
| **Internal staff messages** | **~40 messages/staff/business day** on the internal channel (chat + email combined), log-normal across staff | 20–80 | ESTIMATE — no citable benchmark found; generic knowledge-worker figure, marked low confidence by design |

### Cross-checks the generator should satisfy

1. `dues_revenue ≈ Σ(members × dues_rate_by_type)` and `dues_revenue / total_revenue` within the 27–43% band.
2. `renewals_on_time + renewals_late = renewal_rate × renewable_members`; late renewals must fall inside the configured grace period for members whose benefits stayed active, with a small tail (~2–5% of renewals) reinstating after grace expiry (MGI: reinstatement contact continues indefinitely for 22% of orgs).
3. Committee seat count (~60) ÷ active membership ≈ the 4–8% volunteer-share band.
4. Revenue ÷ staff FTE in the $110k–$190k band (PARN £101k avg income/employee, US-adjusted).
